# Potential Character Search Criteria — T0/T1 Candidates

Use this as a prompt for a specialized AI (or manual research) to find real historical figures who could serve as Tier 0 or Tier 1 psychic candidates in the Psychic Revolution timeline.

---

## Prompt for AI Search

```
I am writing an alternate-history story set in 1936–1945 where psychic powers are unlocked through industrial machinery (vacuum tubes, binary logic). Only a tiny fraction of the population has the right genetics to survive the awakening process.

I need you to search for REAL historical figures who could serve as psychic candidates. Here are the criteria:

## TIER 0 CANDIDATES (The God-Kings / Singularities)
Frequency: roughly 1 in 2.3 billion. Only ~10 exist worldwide.

Requirements:
- Born between 1890 and 1930 (must be alive and in their prime during 1936–1945)
- ELITE athlete: Olympic gold medalist, world record holder, or utterly dominant in their sport to a statistically impossible degree
- AND intellectually sharp: university education, professional career outside sports, demonstrated strategic thinking, or polymath tendencies
- The combination matters more than either alone. A pure athlete without intellect is T1 at best. A pure intellectual without physical gifts can't survive the awakening.
- "Statistical impossibility" is a strong signal: someone whose achievements are so far beyond their peers that it defies normal human variance (e.g., Don Bradman's 99.94 batting average vs. the next-best 60)

Examples of what I'm looking for:
- Luz Long: German law student AND elite Olympic long jumper
- Jesse Owens: 4-time Olympic gold medalist with natural physical gifts that seemed superhuman
- Don Bradman: cricket batting average of 99.94 — a 4.4-sigma statistical outlier

## TIER 1 CANDIDATES (The Aces)
Frequency: roughly 1 in 1,000,000. Hundreds exist worldwide.

Requirements:
- Born between 1890 and 1930
- Elite athlete: Olympic medalist, national champion, world record holder, or dominant in a physically demanding sport
- Ideally (but not required): some intellectual or technical background (engineering, medicine, military strategy, mathematics)
- Pure physical specimens without intellectual depth are still viable but less stable

Examples:
- Babe Didrikson: greatest female athlete in history, multi-sport gold medalist
- Kurt Meyer: motorcycle racer and SS officer
- Nina Dumbadze: Georgian discus world record holder

Search by NATION. For each major power, find 3-5 candidates. Prioritize:

1. GERMANY (including Austria pre-Anschluss)

4. USSR (all Soviet republics)
5. JAPAN

9. POLAND
10. CZECHOSLOVAKIA
11. FINLAND

## FOR EACH CANDIDATE, PROVIDE:

1. Full name
2. Birth year and nationality
3. Sport(s) and peak achievements (specific medals, records, years)
4. Non-athletic background: education, profession, intellectual achievements
5. Why they fit: a 1-2 sentence argument for T0 or T1 candidacy
6. Any notable biographical details that could inform their character (political views, war service, personal tragedies, rivalries with other candidates)

## WHAT TO AVOID

- Figures who died before 1936
- Figures who were too old to be physically active by 1936 (born before ~1890)
- Pure intellectuals with no athletic background (they can't survive the awakening)
- Fictional characters — these must be REAL historical people
- Figures already used in the existing roster (see below)
```

## Additional Notes for Manual Research

### What makes a good T0 candidate (narrative criteria beyond the stats):

1. **A story that intersects with the timeline.** Someone who was at the 1936 Berlin Olympics and witnessed the Miracle. Someone who defected, resisted, or collaborated. Someone whose real-life tragedy or triumph maps onto the psychic war.

2. **A recognizable name.** The thrill of this story is "oh my god, THAT person is a psychic?" The more famous the athlete, the better — as long as the fame is earned by genuine dominance.

3. **Geographic and political diversity.** The psychic revolution should feel global. Candidates from India, Africa, South America, and smaller European nations prevent the story from becoming "white Western athletes: the psychic war."

4. **Moral complexity.** The best candidates have something complicated about them — political affiliations, personal flaws, difficult choices during the war. A candidate who was a Nazi sympathizer, a resistance fighter, a collaborator, or a refugee is more interesting than a blank-slate champion.

### What makes a good T1 candidate:

1. **Volume and variety.** T1s are the workhorses. You need enough of them that losing one in battle feels like a real loss, not the end of a nation's program.

2. **National flavor.** Each nation's T1s should reflect that nation's doctrine. German T1s are aggressive and precise. British T1s are mathematical and quiet. Soviet T1s are brutal and high-volume. Japanese T1s are stealthy and spiritual.

3. **Rivalries and relationships.** The best T1s have a connection to another candidate — they competed against each other in the Olympics, they trained together, they were rivals. These pre-existing relationships make the psychic war feel personal.

### Sports that produce the best candidates (in rough priority order):

1. Track & field (especially jumpers, sprinters, throwers — explosive power + precision)
2. Combat sports (boxing, wrestling, fencing — the "warrior" mindset)
3. Gymnastics (body control, spatial awareness)
4. Swimming (endurance, body efficiency)
5. Motorsport (the "machine + human" interface — especially relevant for this setting)
6. Team sports with standout individual stars (football, hockey, cricket)
7. Winter sports (especially for Nordic candidates — Finland, Sweden, Norway)

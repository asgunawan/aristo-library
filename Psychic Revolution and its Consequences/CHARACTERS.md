# Psychic Revolution - Characters & Tiers

## Tier System
- **Tier 0 (The Singularities / God-Kings):** Natural super-conductors or perfect mathematical ascendants. Produce no thunderclaps when Snapping (Silent or "Whisper-Snap"), require no recalibration, infinite resolution. 
- **Tier 1 (The Aces):** Elite, highly trained engineered heroes. Require constant calibration in integration vaults or else their power becomes "blurry" and dangerous.
- **Tier 2/3 (Disposables / The Grunts):** Mass-produced, unstable soldiers with clumsy resolution and short lifespans before they suffer from "Neural Soot" (brain exhaustion).

## Key Figures

### Independents & Defectors
- **Luz Long (Tier 0):** The first awakened ("The God-King"). A German law student and elite jumper. The incident: During the August 1936 Long Jump finals, he glided in the air for six seconds, landing double the record distance with glowing blue eyes and a kinetic ripple that threw sand 30 feet. Realizing he was a prisoner of the SS, he defected in May 1939, "Snapped" across the border, and is currently interned in a lead-lined Swiss Alpine bunker. Needs no recalibration and can "Whisper-Snap".

### Germany (Aero-Jaeger Legion)
- **Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer (Tier 1 Ace):** Former **motorcycle racer and SS officer**. The Aggressor. Fastest Ramp-Up of any non-Tier 0 (12 seconds). Uses the "Loud Snap"—weaponizes the thunderclap to stun enemies before "Flicking" tungsten needles into them. Led the Tczew Bridge mission in Poland. Currently leading **Staffel-Desert in North Africa**. Hardened by Poland and France.
- **Gottfried von Cramm (Tier 1 Ace):** Known as "The Baron." **World-class tennis champion**. The Sniper. High-resolution "Flicking." Rarely Snaps, preferring to fly at high altitude and snipe from miles away with needle-thin darts. Refit with Philips "Gold-Pin" tubes. Currently patrolling the European coastline.
- **Gisela Uhlen (Tier 1 Ace - Refit):** Famous **German actress and gymnast**. The Valkyrie. Master of "Kinetic Multi-tasking"—can maintain a flight barrier for an entire bomber squadron while independently attacking three different targets. The face of Nazi "Neural Propaganda" to counter Babe Didrikson.
- **Adolf Hitler:** Rushed the awakening of 55 Tier-1 Aces on unstable 2nd-gen hardware after Luz Long defected.
- **Konrad Zuse & Hans Berger:** German engineers who built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator beneath the 1936 Berlin stadium. Their machine exploded from Tier 0 "Backflow" seconds after Long landed.
- **Erwin Rommel:** Commander of the Afrika Korps. In North Africa, his secret objective is not oil, but seizing the Mica Pipeline at the Suez Canal.

### USA (Project Circuit-Breaker)
- **Artie Gable (Tier 1 Ace):** "The Engineered Hero." **Ohio State baseball pitcher and electrical engineer.** Awakened in 1938 using IBM Punch-Cards. Requires frequent recalibration. High-stamina but **low-resolution** (American glass is "foggy"). Uses "Gable Goggles" for "Graph-Paper Snapping." Carries an M1 Garand fired with zero recoil via TK. **Currently arriving in Alexandria, Egypt—the first American psychic to enter the war.**
- **Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1 Ace):** **Greatest female athlete of history (Gold medalist in hurdles/javelin).** "The Spirit of Columbia." Awakened with "Crystal-Logic," making her the most stable psychic created. Requires 50% less recalibration than Gable. **Global celebrity and propaganda icon.** Currently training for the "Secret War" in South America.
- **Jesse Owens (Tier 0 - Secret):** **4-time Olympic Gold Medalist.** The Phantom. Natural super-conductor like Luz Long. Achieved "Recalibration Independence" in 1940. Faster and quieter than Artie or Babe. Classified—kept in **a secret lead-lined bunker in Tuskegee, Alabama** due to racial politics. Maintained as the "Final Insurance Policy."

### Great Britain (Project Arthur)
- **Sydney Wooderson (Tier 0):** "The Acoustic Ghost." Also called **"The Mighty Atom."** A **120-pound solicitor's clerk and world-record runner.** "Slow-Cooked" for a full year in the Bletchley machine. Performs "Micro-Snaps" (blinks 10 times/second) to move like a flickering ghost. **Currently in a coma-like state at Bletchley Park** after exhausting himself during the Dunkirk evacuation.
- **Jack Lovelock (Tier 1 Ace - Retired):** **1936 Gold Medalist and Medical Doctor.** "The Neural Mathematician." Most efficient TK user in history—could down a bomber with a single "Flick" to the fuel line. Permanently "Sooted." His brain can no longer handle a Ramp-Up. Now the **Dean of the Bletchley Neural Academy**, training the next generation.
- **Alan Turing:** Headquartered at Bletchley Park. Developed the "Slow-Cook" method—tuning the brain over 30 days via iterative resonance. Also developed Radar-Sync integration.

### USSR (The Red Resonators)
- **Nina Dumbadze (Tier 1+ Alpha):** "The Red Queen." **Georgian discus world-record holder.** Commander of the "Night Witches" (Female Psychic Wing). Guarding the Kremlin. Pushes past safe limits (150kg to 180kg) by burning her own nerves. Awakened with "Dirty Power" but survived due to her immense physical and mental density.
- **Viktor "The Bear" (Resonator-001):** **Siberian heavyweight wrestler.** Raw Force. Famous for "Kinetic Screams"—releases entire metabolic energy in a single shockwave that flattens buildings. Currently damaged, recovering from "Neural Blowout" during the Winter War in Finland.

### Imperial Japan (Project Kotoamatsukami)
- **Tetsuo Iwasaki (Tier 1 Ace):** **All-Japan Kendo Champion and Physicist.** "The Ghost of Kyoto." Master of the "Void-Shroud." Uses Zen meditation to silence the air displacement of his Snaps. Uses non-magnetic Obsidian Shards as projectiles to bypass sensors. Stationed on the carrier Kaga near Indochina, preparing for the "Southern Road."

## Minor Power Characters
- **Gunder Hägg (Sweden - Tier Unknown):** World-record runner. The sole Swedish psychic. Uses the highly stable "Bofors Hybrid" machine allowing him to hold 100% Ramp-Up longer than almost anyone. Functions as Armed Neutrality deterrent.
- **Jean Taris (France - Tier 2):** A national swimmer drafted for Project "Sainte-Geneviève." Was tethered to the Maginot Line's power grid as a living battery for Static Jammers. Captured by the Gestapo when the Maginot was bypassed.

# Psychic Revolution - Characters & Tiers

## Tier System
- **Tier 0 (The Singularities / God-Kings):** Natural super-conductors or perfect mathematical ascendants. Produce no thunderclaps when Snapping (Silent), require no recalibration, infinite resolution. 
- **Tier 1 (The Aces):** Elite, highly trained engineered heroes. Require constant calibration in integration vaults.
- **Tier 2/3 (Disposables / The Grunts):** Mass-produced, unstable soldiers with clumsy resolution and short lifespans before they "Soot".

## Key Figures

### Independents
- **Luz Long (Tier 0):** The first awakened. Defected from Germany. The human nuclear deterrent sitting in a Swiss Alpine bunker.

### Germany (Aero-Jaeger Legion)
- **Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer (Tier 1 Ace):** Aggressive Vanguard. Loud snaps as sonic weapons. High speed.
- **Gottfried von Cramm (Tier 1 Ace):** The Sniper. High-resolution flicking from miles away.
- **Gisela Uhlen (Tier 1 Ace):** The Valkyrie. Master of Kinetic Multi-tasking. 

### USA (Project Circuit-Breaker)
- **Artie Gable (Tier 1 Ace):** The Heavy Tank. IBM-mapped logic. High stamina, procedural. Carries an M1 Garand fired with zero recoil via TK.
- **Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1 Ace):** The Spirit of Columbia. "Crystal logic" makes her highly stable. Propaganda icon.
- **Jesse Owens (Tier 0 - Secret):** The Phantom. Natural superconductor kept hidden due to racial politics. The final insurance policy.

### Great Britain (Project Arthur)
- **Sydney Wooderson (Tier 0):** The Acoustic Ghost. Slow-cooked over 30 days. Currently in a coma after shattering the German Aces at Dunkirk.
- **Jack Lovelock (Tier 1 Ace - Retired):** The Neural Mathematician. Permanently "Sooted" and now the Dean of the Bletchley Neural Academy.

### USSR (The Red Resonators)
- **Nina Dumbadze (Tier 1+ Alpha):** The Red Queen. Pushes past safe biological limits by burning her nerves.
- **Viktor "The Bear" (Resonator-001):** Raw Force. Specializes in "Kinetic Screams" that flatten buildings.

### Imperial Japan (Project Kotoamatsukami)
- **Tetsuo Iwasaki (Tier 1 Ace):** The Ghost of Kyoto. Master of the Void-Shroud. Uses obsidian shards as non-magnetic projectiles.

# Psychic Revolution - Countries & Geopolitics

In the "Neural Cold War" of 1941, the world is radically partitioned by psychic doctrine, specific technological advantages, and desperate resource dependencies. Every nation either built their own "Manhattan Project of the Mind" or became a battlefield to supply those who did.

## The Major Powers

### Germany (The Greater German Reich)
- **Program:** "Aero-Jaeger" (Air Hunter). Led by Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer.
- **Strategic Position:** Controls 80% of the world's high-purity glass after capturing the Netherlands, allowing mass production of Gen-3 "Disposable" psychics as Panzer shields. **Now also controls the Indian Mica pipeline after capturing the Suez Canal (April 1941). The "Great Refit" is underway—combining Dutch Glass with Indian Mica to create perfectly stable "Knights."**
- **Vulnerability:** **Single point of failure: the Philips Factory in Eindhoven.** If the RAF drops a "Static Bomb" on it, the entire German psychic program loses recalibration capability. Also, only 11 Tier-1 Aces exist—each loss is 9% of total strategic power.
- **Atrocities:** "Heroes of Science" who fail the awakening process are wiped from existence. The SS conducts "Neural Salvage," surgically ripping tubes and mica out of dead psychics to reuse. **Captured Fanny Blankers-Koen (Tier 0), holding her children hostage to force compliance. Building a "Throne" to forcibly awaken her as a "Strategic Goddess."**
- **Current Objective:** Launch Operation Barbarossa against the USSR with Refit Aces. Believe the Soviet "Burners" are a joke. **Awakened Fanny Blankers-Koen (Tier 0 King-Goddess) in October 1941—fitted with Eindhoven Leash and Backpack Vault, stationed at Pas-de-Calais as the "Anti-Owens." Lost Hanna Reitsch (Tier 1) to Neural Blowout in Atlantic Duel against Jesse Owens—proving Refit tech is useless against Tier 0s. Now racing to produce a counter to the "Phantom."**

### United States of America
- **Program:** Project "Circuit-Breaker". Led by Artie Gable and Mildred "Babe" Didrikson.
- **Strategic Position:** High-budget, safe, standardized assembly lines using IBM logic and Westinghouse soft-fuses. Psychics fight like heavily supported Tanks. **~60 Tier-1 Aces total, plus 600 elite human "Squire" support soldiers. Also developing "Crystal-Logic" (solid-state proto-transistor using Galena/Germanium crystals) for stability-focused Aces like Babe. Working on Synthetic Mica (DuPont Polymer-Mica) for Tier-2 Grunts—works for disposables but melts under Tier 1/Tier 0 loads. Bell Labs pursuing "Solid-State Integration Vault" (the "Manhattan Project" of 1943)—too weak currently to replace tube-forests.**
- **Vulnerability:** They lack the pure Dutch glass, meaning American Aces suffer from "foggy" resolution when Snapping. They are completely dependent on importing Mica to insulate their massive awakening machines (Integration Vaults). **Artie Gable humiliated at Suez—"Standardization" doctrine proven inadequate against German "Elite Concentration."**
- **Secrets:** Harboring Jesse Owens, a natural Tier 0 (Super-Conductor) in Alabama, hidden due to racial politics but maintained as a final, absolute deterrent. **Also holding Cornelius Warmerdam (Tier 0, dormant) and Joe Louis (Tier 0, dormant). The US has 4 of the world's 10 Tier 0s but is too afraid to awaken most of them.**
- **Current Objective:** Hold the "Brazilian Mica Bridge" at all costs. Build the "Rio Vault" to awaken Sylvio Padilha. Keep Babe Didrikson in the Amazon hunting German Snap-Teams. Decide whether to release Jesse Owens. **Unveiled Jesse Owens to the public (September 1941). "Europe First" strategy—send Owens to London, Padilha to Panama, Babe to Amazon. Building CV-N (Neural Carriers)—Essex-class ships with lead-lined Integration Halls and "Snap-Pads." 500,000 volunteers for "Gable Standard" recruitment in first week; only 12 identified as Tier 1 candidates. Refuses to use Hollow labor in factories—production speed slower than Axis. Pacific Fleet Neural-Blind after Pearl Harbor—rushing Mobile Integration Pods on old cruiser hulls.**

### Great Britain & The UK
- **Program:** Project "Arthur" based at Bletchley Park. Led (formerly) by Jack Lovelock and Sydney Wooderson.
- **Strategic Position:** Mastered the "Slow-Cook" resonance method over 30 days. Their psychics are mathematically precise, stealthy "Ghosts" who sync with Chain Home Radar to see the battlefield beyond visual limits. **Developing "Operation Echo-Chamber"—thousands of low-power tube arrays creating "Neural Illusions" (ghost signals) to bluff German Aces into seeing 100 false targets. Shared "Slow-Cook" math with US in exchange for Brazilian Mica.**
- **Vulnerability:** **The Suez has fallen. Indian Mica pipeline severed. British psychics rationed to 10-minute flight sessions daily. Sydney Wooderson (Tier 0) in coma, "Humming" at 104°F—will "Carbonize" by August without Brazilian Mica refit. If Wooderson dies, the UK Static Net collapses and German Aces can Snap directly into 10 Downing Street. Derek "Echo" Harding (last active Tier 1 in Africa) killed at El Kantara. British Tier-1 presence in Mediterranean: EXTINCT.**
- **Current Objective:** Retrieve Don Bradman (Tier 0) from Australia via secret Sunderland flight. Keep Wooderson alive. Support Italian "Project Marconi" to create Axis civil war. Survive. **Jesse Owens arrived at Bletchley Park (October 1941). Turing preparing "Neural-Link" experiment—plugging Owens's raw power through comatose Wooderson's mathematical precision as a filter. If successful, Owens gains "King's Math"; if it fails, both brains "Soot" instantly.**

### USSR (Soviet Union)
- **Program:** The "Red Resonators". Led by Nina Dumbadze.
- **Strategic Position:** Utter dependence on brute force and "Dirty Power" from the Svetlana Tube Plant. They pump unfiltered energy to yield raw, devastating kinetic force with no concern for safety. **120+ psychics—the most of any nation—but they are "Burners": short-lived, high-power, mentally unstable. Stalin authorized "Neural Scorched Earth"—NKVD sweeping orphanages for 0.00001% Factor children to build a "Wall of Meat" (thousands of Tier-2 Burners as human static). Ordered spies to steal a single "Gold-Pin" Philips tube for reverse-engineering.**
- **Vulnerability:** The psychics are tethered to "Integration Trains" and must stay near the front lines; moving away for 72 hours leads to madness or death. **Tech is "Dirty"—tubes arc and spark. Cannot fire the first shot; will trade space for time. Desperate to reverse-engineer Dutch glass.**
- **Atrocities:** Unmatched body count. The 99.9% who fail the genetic filter become a lobotomized "Hollow" labor force used to build fortifications and march blindly into machine-gun fire.
- **Current Objective:** Hold the "Stalin Line" with Nina as Kinetic Anchor. Drown German Refit Aces in a sea of low-quality psychic static. Survive Barbarossa. **Barbarossa began June 22, 1941—German Refit Aces destroyed Svetlana Tube Plants and Integration Trains in first 3 hours. Nina held Berezina River for 48 hours, lost 30 pounds, hair turned white. Now executing "Moscow Protocol"—50-mile Kinetic Dead-Zone. 2 million Hollows marching as "Biological Sandbags." 150 active Burners at any time. Germany is an Elite Scalpel cutting a Mountain of Flesh.**

### Imperial Japan
- **Program:** Project "Kotoamatsukami". Led by Tetsuo Iwasaki.
- **Strategic Position:** Zen-integration allowing them to produce Aero-Shinobis equipped with non-magnetic obsidian shards payload, rendering them invisible to radar and magnetic sensors. **Developed "Salt-Tank Hibernation"—psychics kept in near-death state in salt-water vats to preserve Neural Filaments until attack moment. Practicing "Water-Snapping" (ship-to-ship teleportation).**
- **Vulnerability:** Reliant on securing Mica from the Indonesian/Indian archipelago and trading with Germany (Mica for Tube Math). Extreme resource starvation leads to desperate strikes. **"Total Neural Embargo" by US—Corning Silica and Tungsten cutoff. Psychics using tubes from recycled radio parts. Void-Shroud becoming louder and "Dirtier." Have identified Naoto Tajima (Tier 0) but CANNOT awaken him—machines too crude, would kill him. Desperately begging Germany for tube blueprints. Secretly talking to USSR about trading Void-Shroud math for Burner volume.**
- **Current Objective:** Launch "Blackout Snap" on Pearl Harbor Integration Vaults (not battleships) by September/October 1941—3 months ahead of historical schedule. Seize Bangka-Belitung Tin and Silica mines in Southeast Asia to build independent Glass infrastructure. **Successfully executed Pearl Harbor "Blackout Snap" on September 7, 1941—destroyed Honolulu Integration Vault, capsized USS Arizona with psychic "Fist." Yamamoto: "We have shattered their Pacific eyes, but their heart is in Brazil. Unless we awaken Tajima within 90 days, we are a man with a sword fighting a man who can stop time." Now racing to seize Dutch East Indies silica/tin mines. Pushing Hitler for German "Refit" technicians in exchange for Void-Shroud math. Developing "Deck Snap" boarding tactics—capture the machine, don't sink the metal.**

## Minor & Resource Powers

### France
- **Program:** Project "Sainte-Geneviève". 
- **History:** Used "Harmonic Resonance" via tuning forks for a gentle awakening. They used their psychics primarily defensively, tethering them to the Maginot Line as living batteries to power Massive Static Jammers. 
- **Current Status:** Occupied. Bypassed by Germany and systematically captured by the Gestapo. 

### Fascist Italy
- **Program:** "Project Legio XI" (The Eleventh Legion). Status: **Integrated into German Command, but secretly pursuing independence.**
- **Awakening:** "Marconi Pulse"—used high-frequency radio waves instead of binary logic. Brass, glass, and quartz machine. Highly unstable.
- **Selection:** "The New Roman"—visually perfect athletes and Italian air force pilots.
- **Combat Style:** "Velocisti" (Speedsters). Fastest flyers in the world (220 mph) but worst endurance.
- **Current Status:** Following the "Acoustic Death" of their navy, the remaining **8 Italian psychics** were folded into Rommel's **Staffel-Desert** in North Africa. **Secretly developing "Project Marconi" to awaken Ondina Valla (Tier 0) using Radio-Frequency Resonance—a machine that "sings" rather than "forces." Hidden beneath Vatican or remote Tuscan villa to avoid Gestapo Hum-Sensors. MI6 secretly leaking Mica to support this, hoping an Italian Tier 0 "Goddess" will demand independence and force Hitler to divert Aces from London.**

### Finland
- **Strategic Status:** Co-belligerent with Germany against USSR. Wild card.
- **Program:** None formal. Possesses a "Frankenstein" machine built from captured Soviet tubes and Swedish mechanical gears.
- **Asset:** Viljo Heino (Tier 0 - Dormant). "Metabolic Infinite-Loop"—zero metabolic friction. Currently unawakened; Finland lacks capability. Considering asking Germany for a "Refit Team," which would give Axis a Tier 0 on the Northern Front.
- **Dilemma:** Hates the Soviets (Winter War), fears the Germans. Heino's wound "sealed" with blue kinetic glow during Winter War—signs of natural Tier 0 emergence.

### Australia (Commonwealth)
- **Strategic Status:** "Neural Mine" for the British Empire. No domestic Integration capacity.
- **Asset:** Don Bradman (Tier 0 - Dormant). Discovered by Alan Turing through probability analysis. "Total Resolution"—100% hand-eye-kinetic coordination. Churchill has ordered secret "Neural Retrieval" via modified Short Sunderland flying boat.
- **Threat:** Japan watching the Pacific. If they detect a Tier 0 being moved, they will launch a "Blackout Snap" to hijack the plane.

### Sweden
- **Program:** The "Bofors" Hybrid. 
- **Strategic Status:** Armed Neutrality. Built a deeply terrifying, flawless Integration machine using 20,000 tubes and a brass Mechanical Differential Analyzer. 
- **Effect:** With only *one* psychic (runner Gunder Hägg), they created an unassailable Sentry. The stability of the machine allows him to remain Ramp-ups indefinitely, single-handedly deterring Germany and Russia from taking Sweden’s iron and silica mines.

### The Commonwealth (Canada, Australia, India)
- **Strategic Status:** The "Neural Mines." 
- **Role:** They lack the capacity to build Integration Vaults. Instead, they act as "Genetic Prospectors," testing their athletes and citizens for the 0.0001% "Factor" and shipping them to London. This strips the colonies of their best genetic defenses.

### Czechoslovakia
- **History (The Skoda Logic):** Were the world leaders in Neural Alignment Math before 1939. When Hitler annexed the country, he didn't just want tanks; he stole the Skoda Logic Gates, allowing the rapid advancement of the German Gen-2 Aces.

### The Netherlands
- **History (The Philips Tragedy):** Held the monopoly on pure glass, but lacked the 0.0001% genetics. A disastrous 1938 live-radio awakening turned a national hero into a Hollow, prompting the government to ban the program.
- **Current Status:** Conquered by Germany purely to secure the Philips Tube Factory, tipping the technological balance of power across Europe.

### Poland
- **Status (The Ghost Pool):** Poland fell in 1939 because they had "The Genetics" but no "Glass" to build machines.
- **Current Threat:** Both Hitler and Stalin conduct **"Neural Einsatzgruppen"** sweeps in occupied Poland, hunting children who show the 0.0001% gene to force-awaken.

### Brazil
- **Strategic Status:** The "Neural Alamo"—the last lifeline of the Allies.
- **Role:** With the Suez fallen and Indian Mica in German hands, Brazil sits on 90% of the world's remaining high-purity sheet mica (Muscovite, from Minas Gerais). Slightly lower quality than Indian "Ruby Mica" (more metallic impurities = "Green/Brown" mica), adding minor "Neural Noise" to Allied psychics. US has sent thousands of engineers to ramp up production 400% in months.
- **The "Mica Fortress" Deal (May 1941):** President Vargas signed: 100% mica output to Allies. US builds "Rio Vault" Integration facility in Petrópolis mountains—first psychic facility in Southern Hemisphere. US stations Babe Didrikson as Guardian. Volta Redonda steel mill produces lead-lined shielding for psychic transport ships. Brazil gets its own Aces—Sylvio Padilha (Tier 1+ prospect) to be first awakened.
- **Current Threat:** German "Snap-Teams" (Tier-2 Infiltrators using 5-foot micro-Snaps through jungle canopy) attempting to contaminate mines with "Neural-Soot" bombs—magnetic dust that renders mica useless for vacuum tubes. Babe Didrikson hunting them.

### Switzerland
- **Strategic Status:** The Ultimate Safehouse. Neutral ground. Hosts Luz Long, the world's only known active Tier 0 God-King, at **Fort San Carlo**—a windowless, lead-lined Alpine bunker. A silent, global nuclear deterrent.

### The Vatican
- **Strategic Status:** Extreme Paranoia Defensive Zone.
- Rumored to have lined the Apostolic Palace with ancient lead and silver wire, creating a **"Silence Zone"** where no psychic can Ramp-Up—making the Pope the only person in Europe safe from a "Snap-Assassination."

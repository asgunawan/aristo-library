# Psychic Revolution - Psychics (Science, Abilities, & National Approaches)

## The Core Science: The "Mind-Body Muscle"
Psychic power is not mystical; it is a brutally physical biological trait unlocked by early vacuum-tube computing, binary logic, and specific brain frequencies.

- **The Genetic Filter:** Only individuals with a rare 0.0001% "Neural Conductivity" (often elite athletes or high-end scholars) can survive the awakening process. 
- **The Hollows:** The 99.9% who fail the process have their personalities wiped out entirely. Their bodies grow immensely strong and durable, becoming "Hollows"—lobotomized husks used as slave labor or shock troops.
- **Ramp-Up & Exhaustion:** Psychics must concentrate for 30-60 seconds to reach 100% power (the Ramp-Up). They suffer from physical exhaustion and eventual "Neural Soot" (brain damage) if they overexert themselves. To survive, they consume "Psi-Rations" of 10,000+ calories a day consisting of lard and glucose.

## The Power Set (1939-1941)
- **The Snap (Spatial Blinking):** Line-of-sight teleportation (max 100 yards). Generates a loud sonic boom (thunderclap), unless executed by a Tier 0. Hard limit of 6-7 snaps a day before brain damage sets in.
- **The Shunt:** A safety mechanism constraint. If a psychic attempts to Snap into a solid object, the universe instantly rejects them and materializes them in front of the object, wasting energy.
- **Telekinesis (TK):** Standard max lifting limit is ~150kg. Typically weaponized by "flicking" tungsten sphere bearings or needles at bullet-like velocities.
- **Flight & Kinetic Barriers:** Top speeds around 200 mph. They require a conical kinetic barrier to survive wind resistance and G-forces. The barrier stops .30-06 caliber rounds but can be shattered by heavy anti-air fire (.50 cal / 20mm flak).

## National Evolutionary Approaches
Every nation has a unique philosophy towards awakening and utilizing psychics:

### Germany (The "Aero-Jaeger" Program)
*   **Philosophy:** Elitism, precision agility, and the "Knight" aesthetic.
*   **The Awakening:** Violent 12-hour surges using perfectly refined binary logic gates. Extremely lethal but yields incredibly high-resolution Aces.
*   **Combat Style:** High-G maneuvers and "Multi-Flicking" (controlling many needles independently).

### United States (Project "Circuit-Breaker")
*   **Philosophy:** Industrial standardization and safety protocols.
*   **The Awakening:** Uses IBM Punch-Cards to map personalities and Westinghouse "Soft-Fuses" that immediately cut power if the brain starts to melt. Safe, mechanical, and highly reliable.
*   **Combat Style:** The "Heavy Tank". Deployed with 10-man "Squire" squads who protect the Ace during the crucial 60-second ramp-up. Rely on methodical mathematical Snaps using crosshair goggles.

### Great Britain (Project "Arthur")
*   **Philosophy:** Mathematical precision and radar integration.
*   **The Awakening:** The "Slow-Cook". Iterative resonance tuned over 30 days. Safest method but causes extreme sensory sensitivity (hyperesthesia).
*   **Combat Style:** The "Ghost." Sensory-deprivation trained flyers who can "Silent Snap" and intercept enemies beyond visual range via Chain Home Radar syncs. They use targeting neural pulses to shatter the glass tubes inside German enemies.

### USSR (The "Red Resonator" Program)
*   **Philosophy:** Extreme volume and brute-force attrition.
*   **The Awakening:** "Dirty Power" hacks pumping unfiltered, arcing electricity. Kills almost everyone. 100,000 conscripts run through to yield 20 survivors.
*   **Combat Style:** The "Kinetic Scream." High-turnover "Burners" pushing 150kg limits to 180kg+ by literally burning out their own nervous systems to flatten city blocks. 

### Imperial Japan (Project "Kotoamatsukami")
*   **Philosophy:** Spiritual purity, stillness, and stealth.
*   **The Awakening:** Zen-integration relying on sensory deprivation salt tanks and "Mushin" (No-Mind) meditation so the hardware doesn't fry them.
*   **Combat Style:** Aero-Shinobis. Silent strikes using non-magnetic obsidian shards that slip through enemy metal/static detection arrays.

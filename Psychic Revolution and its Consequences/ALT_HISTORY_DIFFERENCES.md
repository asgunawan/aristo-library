# Psychic Revolution - Alt-History Sandbox

This file tracks the major deviations and ripple effects of integrating industrialized psychic warfare into the 1940s landscape.

## 1. D-Day is Impossible
**The Atlantic Wall is a Static Net.** Traditional amphibious assaults are obsolete because a single German Ace parked on a cliff can use TK to "flick" tungsten ball bearings straight through the hulls of Higgins boats from a mile away. You cannot land troops on a beach that is guarded by psychic blenders.

## 2. The "Midway" Boarding Action
Naval warfare changes because long-range psychic strikes over moving oceans are too unstable. Aircraft carriers become mobile platforms to launch **deck-boarding actions**. Zero fighters drop "Ghost Scouts" onto Allied carriers for hand-to-hand psychic brawls, essentially recreating the Age of Sail boarding tactics.

## 3. The "V-Weapon" Shift: Static Rockets
Instead of terror bombing with explosives, Wernher von Braun develops the V-2 "Störsender" (Jammer) Rocket. Loaded with cobalt magnets and vacuum tubes, they crash into London and emit a blinding 10-minute EMP to the brain. British psychics are paralyzed, allowing German Aces a brief window to "Snap" in and assassinate targets.

## 4. The "Hollow" Rebellion (Spartacus Event)
The Soviets realize TK can be used to "puppeteer" the nervous systems of their Hollows. They march armies of 10,000 brain-dead, Olympic-level athletes into machine-gun fire, controlled remotely by Psychics. It's a terrifying necromantic horde powered by pure science.

## 5. The Jungle War (The Amazon Front)
Because Mica and Silica are the new oil, the Amazon Rainforest in Brazil becomes a strategic hellscape. U.S. Marines and Babe Didrikson fight German saboteurs. Because the jungle restricts flight and line-of-sight Snaps, it becomes a brutal ground-war of TK traps: tripping wires from 100 yards away and burning down the canopy to create "Snap-Lanes."

## 7. The Suez Falls (April 1941)
In real history, the Suez Canal remained in Allied hands throughout the war. In this timeline, the "Ace Gap" is too wide—Germany's 4 Tier-1 Aces (Von Cramm, Uhlen, Reitsch, and a recovering Meyer) overwhelm the single British Tier-1 (Derek Harding) and a "Sooted" Artie Gable. Rommel captures 400 tons of Indian Mica, enabling the "Great Refit" that eliminates German Neural Jitter. The British Empire becomes "Neural-Silent" in the Mediterranean.

## 8. The "Great Refit" (June 1941)
Germany combines Dutch Glass (Philips) with Indian Mica (Suez) for the first time, creating perfectly stable "Knights." Kurt Meyer's resolution doubles. This never happened historically—Germany was always resource-starved. Now they have both halves of the God-equation.

## 9. Fanny Blankers-Koen Captured
The "Flying Housewife" (historically won 4 Golds in 1948) is captured by the SS and held beneath the Philips Factory. Germany attempts to build a "Throne" to forcibly awaken her as a Tier 0 "Strategic Goddess." Her children are hostages.

## 10. Brazil Becomes the "Neural Alamo"
With Indian Mica cut off, Brazil's Minas Gerais mines become the most strategically vital territory on Earth. The US negotiates 100% mica monopoly, builds the "Rio Vault," and stations Babe Didrikson as Guardian. The Amazon becomes a "Shadow War" front against German Snap-Teams.

## 11. Japan's "Blackout Snap" Timeline Moves Up
The "Total Neural Embargo" and fear of being the only "Godless" nation forces Japan to plan the Pearl Harbor attack 3 months early (September/October 1941). The target shifts from battleships to Integration Vaults—they want to destroy US recalibration capability, not sink ships.

## 12. The Tier 0 Census
10 Tier 0s exist worldwide (frequency: 0.000000004% of 2.3 billion). USA holds 4, Germany holds 1 captive, Japan/Finland/Italy/Australia each hold 1 dormant, Luz Long remains neutral in Switzerland. The war is a race to awaken these "Gods" before the other side does.

## 13. No Tehran Conference (1943)
In-person Allied summits are impossible—a single German Ace could "Snap" into the meeting and decapitate all leadership. Instead, leaders communicate via "Neural-Logic Teleprinters"—lead-lined encryption machines using a tethered psychic's "Hum" to scramble radio signals.

## 14. The Italian Double Game
Italy secretly pursues "Project Marconi" to awaken Ondina Valla (Tier 0) using Radio-Frequency Resonance, hidden from Gestapo. MI6 leaks Mica to support this, hoping an Italian Tier 0 will demand independence and fracture the Axis.

## 15. Barbarossa Becomes a "Neural Erasure" (June 1941)
Instead of a traditional artillery barrage, the 11 German Refit Aces perform High-Altitude Whisper-Snaps targeting Soviet Integration Trains and Tube Plants. Within 3 hours, USSR recalibration capability is crippled. Thousands of Soviet Burners go insane from Neural Fever with no way to Sync. The Eastern Front becomes a "Sniper vs. Shotgun" war—German precision against Soviet volume.

## 16. The Brazilian "Secret War" (Operation Giftpilz)
Germany launches a jungle sabotage campaign against Brazilian mica mines. It fails catastrophically—US Marines use low-tech countermeasures (piano wire, punji stakes, calorie-war, Boys AT rifles) to destroy 30 specialized Tier-2 assets. The Germans never reach the Itabira mines. Proves that humans with the right tactics can kill "Gods" if logistics are on their side.

## 17. Pearl Harbor Targets Integration Vaults (September 1941)
Japan attacks 3 months early. Primary target is not Battleships but the Honolulu Integration Vault—Tetsuo Iwasaki shatters all 100,000 vacuum tubes simultaneously. USS Arizona capsized by concentrated psychic "Fist," not bombs. US Pacific Fleet left Neural-Blind; 12 Tier-2 Sentries doomed to "Soot" without recalibration.

## 18. Jesse Owens Unveiled as "The Phantom" (September 1941)
The US government publicly reveals a Tier 0 asset—unprecedented in history. 500,000 volunteers flood recruitment centers in first week. Racial politics temporarily suspended by war terror. The "Gable Standard" filters 500,000 down to 12 Tier 1 candidates.

## 19. The Atlantic Duel: Tier 0 vs. Refit Ace (October 1941)
First direct Tier 0 combat. Jesse Owens defeats Hanna Reitsch (Tier 1 Refit) by simply tapping her primary tube—the Tier 0 touch causes instant Neural Blowout. Proves definitively that Refit technology is useless against a Tier 0. Hitler orders immediate awakening of Fanny Blankers-Koen.

## 20. Fanny Blankers-Koen Becomes the "King-Goddess" (October 1941)
Germany successfully awakens a captive Tier 0 using the Eindhoven "Goddess-Throne." Fitted with the "Backpack Vault"—a 40lb mobile recalibration unit that also serves as a prison (kill-switch triggers Neural Seizure beyond 500 yards). Stationed at Pas-de-Calais as the "Anti-Owens." First Engineered Tier 0 King in history.

## 21. Luz Long: The Morally Gray God-King
Unlike the "noble neutral" archetype, Luz Long stays in Switzerland because the SS holds his wife and infant son hostage. He has performed a cold calculation: his family's lives > millions of Hollows. The most powerful being on Earth chooses personal survival over global salvation. A tragic, arguably selfish figure—not a hero waiting to be unleashed.

## 22. The Tier 0 Hierarchy (God-Kings / Gods / Kings)
Tier 0 is not a monolith. Luz Long (God-King) sits alone at the top—Nature + Nurture, 99% efficiency. "Gods" (Jesse Owens) have raw instinct but lack mathematical depth. "Kings" (Wooderson, Fanny) ascended through technology and discipline but pay a higher physical cost. In a duel, Luz beats Jesse not through speed but through seeing the "board" ten moves ahead—like Magnus Carlsen vs. Hikaru Nakamura.

## 23. CV-N Neural Carriers Replace Battleships
Aircraft carriers are redesigned as "Floating Integration Vaults"—lead-lined halls with 100,000 tubes, seawater-cooled, with "Snap-Pads" instead of long flight decks. Planes become "Taxis" to ferry Aces within striking range. The era of the big-gun battleship dies even faster than in real history.

## 24. The "Hollow" Industrial Economy
Germany and USSR use millions of Hollows (failed awakening candidates) as tireless factory labor. Tiger Tanks and T-34s built by lobotomized athletes. The US and UK refuse this practice—creating a "Production Gap" they must fill with better psychic math.

## 25. Non-Psychic Weapons Are "Psychic-Proofed"
Tanks are flush-welded (no external bolts to TK-unzip), hatches use electromagnets. Spitfires carry "Acoustic Wings" and Magnesium "Blind-Cannons." Infantry use "Flicker-Grenades" (Mylar + Magnesium dust clouds that prevent Snap-mapping) and Boys .55 AT rifles as standard "God-Killer" kit. The 99.9% of humanity adapts rather than becomes obsolete.

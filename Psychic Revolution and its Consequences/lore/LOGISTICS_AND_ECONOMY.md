# Psychic Revolution - Logistics & Economy

War is won on the supply line. In this timeline the entire arms race is dictated by the extreme economic and logistical burden of maintaining psychic weapons — and by three strategic resources: **glass, mica, and genetic stock.**

## The High Cost of an Ace

- **Financial:** tuning a single Tier-1 Ace costs about **$75 million** (1941 dollars) — enough to build over a thousand fighter aircraft.
- **The Energy Barrier:** you cannot build a god with a wall outlet. Awakening one US psychic means diverting the full output of the Norris Dam for 12 hours — Tennessee goes dark. USSR train-recalibrations burn enough coal to power a small city each.

## The Resource War: Who Holds What

### Germany — Power Over Stability
- **Has:** Schott AG glass (world leader), captured Philips manufacturing (mass-scale all-glass envelopes), a pre-war thorium stockpile (no domestic deposit — monazite is only ever processed, not mined, in Germany).
- **Lacks:** any domestic V-1 mica. Mica has four owners, three Allied — so Germany buys low-grade Soviet rock through the Mica-for-Machines deal and stretches it with superior glass.
- **Result:** terrifying power — faster Ramp-Ups, harder hits — on two clocks: the stockpile runs down like oil, and thoriated tubes burn out without V-1 insulation in 6–12 months. They win the sprint, lose the marathon.

### The Allies — Stability Over Power
- **Has:** the UK holds ~75% of the world's high-grade sheet mica via India; the US holds Corning glass and Westinghouse's Kovar seal.
- **Lacks:** the thoriated-filament process, and (for the US) clean glass — Corning aluminosilicate is "foggy" next to Schott. The US also imports its sheet mica (Spruce Pine yields only scrap and flake).
- **Result:** weaker but stable — tube failure under 5%, psychics who last years, and the industrial capacity to mass-produce replacements indefinitely.

### The USSR — Flawed Perfection
- **Has:** the world's best raw rock — the **Mama-Chuya** V-1 muscovite district (the Aldan Shield, by contrast, is phlogopite — a heat-specialist, not a precision material).
- **Lacks:** precision. The Great Purge executed the scientists and glassblowers; the perfect rock is cut with dull knives, introducing micro-fractures.
- **Result:** "Dirty Power" — 100% raw, 20% usable. A fractured spacer arcs under load and Hollows the subject — a near-total Hollow rate. It improves only slowly, as German machine tools from Mica-for-Machines arrive — not Lend-Lease, which never happens.

## The Mediterranean "Mica War" (1941)

The desert is a graveyard for tubes — heat and blown silica sand destroy integration machines — so conventional forces protect the psychics, and psychics fight as scalpels.

- **Axis "Staffel-Desert":** Kurt Meyer leads Aces refit with captured mica, backed by Italian **Velocisti** speedsters acting as 220-mph scouts hunting British Radar-Sync towers. Mobile Integration Vans and a dedicated "Lard Line" of Psi-Rations follow behind.
- **Allies "Mica Shield":** Wavell's command, with **Godfrey Brown** as the active British Ace, backed by "Sorters" tethered to Radar-Sync to sense German Hums a hundred miles out. The **Alexandria Integration Vault** is the only stationary recalibration chamber in Africa — the whole front's single point of failure.
- **The Standoff:** Meyer wants to Snap into Alexandria and kill the Vault to blind the Allies; Wavell wants to bait the Germans into a Static Trap. Meanwhile the desert imposes its own tax: a psychic costs the water of a 50-man platoon, and in 110°F heat a London Ace who can Snap seven times a day manages three before the tubes melt.

## The "Great Refit" (temporary)

When Germany briefly seizes the Indian mica stockpiles at Suez, it holds — for the first and last time — the world's best glass *and* the world's best mica at once. The "Great Refit" at Philips eliminates Neural Jitter and doubles Ace resolution. But it is a single-facility bottleneck: the entire German program depends on Eindhoven, and the captured stockpiles are gone by mid-1942. The refit is the last time Germany fights at full strength.

## The Brazilian "Mica Bridge"

With India contested, Brazil becomes the Allied lifeline. Minas Gerais muscovite runs V-2/V-3 — 90–95% as good as Indian V-1, a hair noisier, enough to leave an Ace's resolution slightly "foggy." The US ships in engineers to quadruple output in months, and the "Mica Bridge" convoy — protected by the first psychic-escort ships — becomes the most-targeted sea lane in history. German Snap-Teams try to poison the mines with "Neural-Soot" dust: better that nobody has psychics than let the Allies keep theirs.

## The "Hollow" Economy

- **Axis:** Germany and the USSR run millions of Hollows as industrial labor — no sleep, no complaints, no safety. Factories never stop.
- **Allied dilemma:** the US and UK refuse Hollow labor (for now), trading a production gap for a moral line.

## The Tech Tree: Why They Can't Skip Vacuum Tubes

- **Solid-state is a dead end (for now):** the transistor won't arrive until 1947; a single atom of dust in a 1941 silicon wafer arcs the whole chip.
- **Crystal-Logic (proto-transistor):** Babe Didrikson's galena-and-germanium awakening is far more stable (no glass to shatter, less recalibration) — but low-power, unable to carry a Tier 0 surge. It can anchor a T1, never a God.
- **Synthetic mica:** DuPont's early Teflon-like polymer handles electricity but not heat — it off-gases and melts during the 12-hour Surge. Fine for Tier 2s and Hollows where jitter doesn't matter; useless for an Ace or a God.
- **The Tier 0 hard floor:** awakening a God-King needs 100,000 perfect tubes and V-1 mica, no shortcut. The US gambit isn't a better machine — it's a better human (Owens, Padilha).

## Tier 0 Logistics: The "Grounded" Reality

- **The efficiency hierarchy:** a T1 is a lightbulb — ~70% of energy wasted as Soot, 10,000+ calories a day, recalibrated every few months. A T0 is neon by comparison — ~80–99% efficient, **zero recalibration** (they are the source code), no Soot. The subtypes differ only by efficiency: a T0 God like Owens runs ~80%; a T0 King like Wooderson is math-ascended and equally efficient, but his body is a poor home for the power, so it burns itself out; a God-King like Luz Long runs ~99% and can pass for human.
- **The resolution cap:** Owens has God-genetics but foggy Corning glass and Brazilian mica — like a Ferrari forced through mud, ~50% effective.
- **Calorie-to-flux:** the more efficient the psychic, the less fuel they burn. A T1 metabolizes 10,000+ calories a day; a God-King eats like a normal man. If the fuel runs out mid-combat, the power doesn't stop — it starts consuming the psychic's own muscle. Fanny's Backpack Vault exists because her forced awakening and temporal power make her drift faster than any natural T1 (see `NEW_TECHNOLOGY.md`).

## The Cost of Losing Suez

Britain institutes "Neural Blackouts" — power rationing to save remaining mica for the Static Net and for Wooderson, who is burning himself out on the island's endless watch (the Radar-Sync). Streetlights off, radio cut to two hours a day, psychics rationed to ten-minute flights.

# Psychic Revolution - Countries & Geopolitics

The war is not WW2 reskinned. It is a **resource war over glass, mica, and genetic stock** — and the winners are not the noble, but the patient and the cynical. This file is the *strategic layer*: who each nation is, what it wants, what it has, what it lacks, and why it can't just win. For *how* each nation awakens and fights, see `PSYCHICS.md`. For the *people*, see `CHARACTERS.md`. For the *hardware*, see `NEW_TECHNOLOGY.md`. Timeline events live in the plot-beats file, not here.

## The Three-Way Standoff

| Power | Has | Needs | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Germany** | Schott glass, Philips seals, Czech Skoda logic, the best Aces | V-1 mica (buying from the USSR, running out) | Win fast before the thorium kills them |
| **UK/USA** | Indian mica, Brazilian mica, stable tubes | Glass manufacturing scale | Outlast Germany, contain the USSR |
| **USSR** | The world's best mica (Mama-Chuya) | Glass tech, machine tools | Trade mica to both sides, build the largest psychic army on Earth |

## The Major Powers

### Germany (The Greater German Reich)
- **Role:** The burning sprint — an elite, thorium-hot machine that wins fast or dies.
- **Doctrine:** Aero-Jaeger *(see PSYCHICS.md)*.
- **Wants:** God-King-grade stability without the God-King; mica by any means.
- **Has:** Schott AG glass (Jena), the captured Philips sealing tech (Eindhoven, March 1939), Czech Skoda logic (Munich 1938), a **pre-war thorium stockpile** (no domestic deposit — thorium comes from monazite, which Germany only ever processed, not mined), and the world's best Aces — Meyer, von Cramm, Uhlen, Reitsch.
- **Needs:** V-1 muscovite mica. Mica has **four owners, three Allied** — so the USSR is the only non-Allied source, and it sells only low-grade rock at extortionate prices. Glass can stretch that rock; it can never replace it.
- **Fatal Flaw:** Two clocks — the thorium stockpile runs down like oil, and the Aces it fuels die of radiation in 6–12 months without mica. Plus they spent their one T0 (Luz Long) — God-Kings are born, not made.

### United States of America
- **Role:** The industrial giant — standardized, safe, but late, reluctant, and holding itself back.
- **Doctrine:** Circuit-Breaker *(see PSYCHICS.md)*.
- **Wants:** Outlast everyone; keep the mica flowing; decide whether to unleash Jesse Owens.
- **Has:** Unmatched capital and scale, Corning aluminosilicate glass + Westinghouse Kovar (1936), IBM punch-card logic, soft-fuse safety. The world's most stable psychic (Babe) and the most T0s — but Owens is hidden and Warmerdam/Joe Louis are dormant.
- **Needs:** High-grade sheet mica — the US imports almost all of it (Spruce Pine, NC yields mostly scrap and flake). Lacks pure Dutch-quality glass.
- **Fatal Flaw:** Racism keeps its best asset — a Black God-King — in a lead-lined basement, and "foggy" Corning glass caps every Ace's effectiveness.

### Great Britain (Project Arthur)
- **Role:** The load-bearer — holds the line at the cost of its own body.
- **Doctrine:** Arthur *(see PSYCHICS.md)*.
- **Wants:** Keep the Indian mica flowing; survive; keep Wooderson alive.
- **Has:** ~75% of the world's high-grade sheet muscovite (via India), Canadian phlogopite (Parent, Quebec), the Mullard/EF50 radar-tube line, and Bletchley's math — Slow-Cook, Radar-Sync, Echo-Chamber.
- **Needs:** Glass manufacturing scale — Mullard famously could not make the all-glass tube bases at volume (the EF50 irony).
- **Fatal Flaw:** Sea-lane fragility — the mica convoys must run the Mediterranean gauntlet — and Wooderson, their only awake T0, is burning himself out holding the island up.

### USSR (The Red Resonators)
- **Role:** The patient predator — profits most, fights least, sweeps in when both sides are spent.
- **Doctrine:** Red Resonators *(see PSYCHICS.md)*.
- **Wants:** German glass tech and machine tools; the largest psychic army on Earth; to inherit a spent Europe.
- **Has:** The world's best mica — the **Mama-Chuya** muscovite district (northern Irkutsk, 80% of Soviet reserves, one of the world's two leaders alongside India) — plus limitless conscripts.
- **Needs:** Glass technology (theirs is primitive), machine tools, and precision labor.
- **Fatal Flaw:** The Great Purge destroyed their scientists and glassblowers, and "Dirty Power" ruins their own perfect rock — 100% raw, 20% usable. No T0, by design: they win with volume, not a God-King.

### Imperial Japan (Project Kotoamatsukami)
- **Role:** The resource-starved sprint south — poverty as fate.
- **Doctrine:** Kotoamatsukami *(see PSYCHICS.md)*.
- **Wants:** Mica (Malaya/Burma) and oil (the Dutch East Indies); an independent glass supply before the embargo strangles them.
- **Has:** Artisanal tube craftsmanship (NEC, Toshiba, Hitachi), ceramic tubes from domestic clay, the zen "Mushin" method, and the stealth Void-Shroud doctrine.
- **Needs:** High-grade mica and tungsten — mined under forced labor in occupied Korea and China, then cut off entirely by the US embargo.
- **Fatal Flaw:** Ceramic tubes are stable but weak — "glass cannons" — and their one T0 (Tajima) cannot be awakened with such crude machines.

## The Minor & Resource Powers

### France
- **Role:** The Static Maginot — the only power whose defensive instinct was right, holding a neural dead-zone along the border for 2.5 years.
- **Asset:** Early CSF/LMT tube research, colonial Madagascar mica, and Émile Allais (T1), the mountain-skier who embodies the defense.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Their jammer wall works — until Germany's "Hollow Storm" burns it down in August 1942.

### The Netherlands
- **Role:** The R&D prize — the reason the war started in March 1939, not September.
- **Asset:** The Philips factory at Eindhoven, inventors of the pentode (1927) and the world's best mass-producible glass-to-metal seals.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Conquered for its glass; the Dutch resistance sabotages German seals with microscopic flaws.

### Fascist Italy
- **Role:** The fence-sitter — not the "peaceful" one, the *opportunist*. Fascist, brutal (Ethiopia 1935), expansionist — but too weak to commit, so it stalls (Mussolini's "non-belligerence," the "fifteen thousand dead to sit at the peace table" calculus), courting both sides, waiting to back the winner.
- **Asset:** Project Marconi — a radio-frequency machine that "sings" rather than "forces," aimed at Ondina Valla (dormant T0). The gentlest awakening method on Earth, born of poverty, not virtue: Italy has the *method* but none of the inputs (no mica, no Schott glass, no capital), so the program is mothballed untested.
- **Leverage / Threat:** MI6 leaks mica to keep the Italian "Goddess" project alive, hoping to force an Axis civil war. The tragedy: the most beautiful method belongs to the ugliest regime, and it dies unused.

### Finland
- **Role:** A German-aligned buffer state — no separate Winter War, just a hostage situation with a psychic bomb in the basement.
- **Asset:** Viljo Heino (T1 dormant), the "Metabolic Infinite-Loop."
- **Leverage / Threat:** Holds a dormant asset Germany courts and the USSR wants to seize; Finland lacks the machine to awaken him.

### Sweden
- **Role:** The neutral black-market broker — the one room on Earth where you can buy Soviet mica, German thorium, and American Kovar in the same transaction.
- **Asset:** LM Ericsson and AGA tube production; Gunder Hägg, the Armed-Neutrality deterrent.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Too useful to invade, too neutral to trust; both sides need the trading floor.

### Australia
- **Role:** The Commonwealth's "neural mine."
- **Asset:** Don Bradman (T0 dormant) — a "statistical impossibility" hidden in plain sight.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Churchill's secret retrieval flight crosses a Pacific that Japan is watching.

### Brazil
- **Role:** The Allied lifeline — with Indian mica contested, Brazil's Minas Gerais muscovite is the last reliable source.
- **Asset:** Strategic-grade muscovite (90–95% Holy-Grail compatible) and Sylvio Padilha (T1+ prospect).
- **Leverage / Threat:** The "Mica Fortress" deal binds Brazil to the Allies; German Snap-Teams poison the mines.

### Czechoslovakia
- **Role:** The logic prize. Hitler didn't just want the Sudetenland — he wanted the Skoda logic gates and Czech glassblowers.
- **Asset:** World-leading neural-alignment math before 1938.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Annexed at Munich; its precision engineering is what lets Germany stretch low-grade Soviet mica further — better glass, never a substitute for the real rock.

### Poland
- **Role:** The ghost pool — a nation with the genetics but no glass.
- **Asset:** The 0.0001% genetic stock.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Both Hitler and Stalin run "Neural Einsatzgruppen" sweeps through occupied Poland, hunting candidate children to force-awaken.

### Switzerland
- **Role:** The safehouse — neutral ground, a silent global deterrent.
- **Asset:** Hosts Luz Long, the world's only known active God-King, in a lead-lined Alpine bunker.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Everyone wants him; no one dares take him.

### The Vatican
- **Role:** Extreme paranoia defensive zone.
- **Asset:** Rumored to be lined with ancient lead and silver wire — a "Silence Zone" where no psychic can Ramp-Up.
- **Leverage / Threat:** Possibly the only person in Europe safe from a Snap-assassination is the Pope.

## The Mica Map (for reference)

| Rank | Source | Type | Holy-Grail compatibility | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **India** | Muscovite, V-1 | 100% | The gold standard; whoever controls the supply chain controls T0 production. |
| 2 | **USSR (Mama-Chuya)** | Muscovite, V-1 raw | 100% raw / 20% processed | Perfect rock, ruined by purged, rushed labor. |
| 3 | **Brazil (Minas Gerais)** | Muscovite, V-2/V-3 | 90–95% | The reliable Allied backup. |
| 4 | **Canada (Parent, QC)** | Phlogopite | Heat-specialist only | Brute-force high-heat gear, not precision tubes. |
| 5 | **Madagascar** | Phlogopite + low muscovite | 60% | Colonial fallback; contested (Operation Ironclad, 1942). |

**The four-owner rule:** muscovite mica has four owners and three are Allied (India, Brazil, USA) — the USSR is the only non-Allied source. Any mica reaching Germany through a neutral broker is still Soviet or Allied mica in disguise; there is no independent source. The mica dependency itself is a physics discovery every nation's scientists reach independently — parallel research, not espionage.

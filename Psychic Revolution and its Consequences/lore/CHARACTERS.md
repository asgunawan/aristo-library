# Psychic Revolution - Characters & Tiers

## Tier System
- **Tier 0 (The Singularities / God-Kings):** Natural super-conductors or perfect mathematical ascendants. Produce no thunderclaps when Snapping (Silent or "Whisper-Snap"). Far more efficient than lower tiers. Sub-divided into: **God-King** (Luz Long—Nature + Nurture, 99% efficiency), **Gods** (Natural super-conductors like Jesse Owens—raw instinct, ~80% efficiency), **Kings** (Math-ascended like Wooderson—discipline over nature, ~80% efficiency but higher maintenance). The three are a *completeness* triangle, not a power ladder: **Gods = nature only, Kings = nurture only, God-Kings = both.** "God-King" means complete — the only being who holds both halves, which is why Luz Long stands alone.
- **Tier 1 (The Aces):** Elite, highly trained engineered heroes. Require constant calibration in integration vaults or else their power becomes "blurry" and dangerous. ~70% efficiency—massive heat waste as "Neural Soot."
- **Tier 2/3 (Disposables / The Grunts):** Mass-produced, unstable soldiers with clumsy resolution and short lifespans before they suffer from "Neural Soot" (brain exhaustion).

**THE "NO FREE T0" RULE (locked):** A Tier 0 is a *strategic weapon, not a soldier* — same hard ceiling as a T1 (150kg/200mph/6–7 Snaps/100y) but instant, silent, tireless, and self-sustaining. An awake, willing T0 ends wars. Therefore **every awake T0 in the story must be leashed** — neutralized, constrained, or fading. A free T0 collapses the plot, and no nation should ever have more than ~1 awake T0. Dormant T0s are not roster weight: they are future levers (deterrence, post-war transistor race, "who gets woken next"). Count dormant T0s as potential, not as active power.

## Key Figures

### Independents & Defectors
- **Luz Long (Tier 0 - God-King):** The first awakened. A German law student and elite long jumper. In August 1936, during the Long Jump finals, he glided through the air for six seconds—glowing blue eyes, a kinetic ripple that threw sand thirty feet—and the world learned what a God-King was. He spent two years stalling, pretending his power was unstable, until February 1939, when Hitler ordered him to process the Dutch athletes on film. He looked at his hands and ran—a silent Whisper-Snap into Switzerland. The SS seized his wife Gisela and infant son Kai-Heinrich the same hour. Now he lives in a lead-lined Alpine bunker, trapped in a hostage-stalemate: his silence for their lives, negotiated down to a monthly photograph and one clause—"no processing." The most powerful being on Earth, the "Magnus Carlsen" of a game he refuses to play, reading law books and feeling the Neural Screams across the border, doing nothing. Morally gray by design: his family is worth more to him than the millions being Hollowed.
- **Fanny Blankers-Koen (Tier 1 - Captive):** "The Flying Housewife"—though her four-gold fame is still a decade away; in 1941 she is a promising 23-year-old Dutch athlete. Germany captured her when the Netherlands fell and tried to *engineer a T0 out of a T1*—the forced-awakening experiment that proved God-Kings can't be manufactured, only born. Her power is **Temporal Multi-Tasking** (processes time 10x faster), but she is slaved to a "Backpack Vault" leash and a hostage husband. The living proof of Germany's failed ambition.

### Germany (Aero-Jaeger Legion)
- **Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer (Tier 1 Ace):** A motorcycle-rider turned SS officer. The Aggressor—fastest Ramp-Up of any non-T0 (12 seconds), weaponizing the "Loud Snap" thunderclap to stun before Flicking tungsten needles. He is the Ace who burns brightest and dies fastest: Germany's thorium-hot Aces have six to twelve months before the radiation claims them. A sprint, not a marathon.
- **Gottfried von Cramm (Tier 1 Ace):** "The Baron," a world-class tennis champion. The Sniper—rarely Snaps, preferring to fly high and Flick needle-thin darts from miles out. Refit with Philips "Gold-Pin" tubes.
- **Gisela Uhlen (Tier 1 Ace):** A famous German actress and dancer. "The Valkyrie." Master of Kinetic Multi-Tasking—holds a flight barrier for a bomber squadron while attacking three targets at once. The face of Nazi "Neural Propaganda," built to counter Babe Didrikson.
- **Hanna Reitsch (Tier 1 Ace - Decommissioned):** The Test Pilot, specialist in "Vertical Snaps" from the stratosphere. Intercepted Owens over the Atlantic and was defeated in the "Atlantic Duel"—her sensors couldn't track his Null-Frequency; he tapped her primary tube and caused instant Neural Blowout. Permanently decommissioned as a combat psychic. The proof of the God-Gap: Refit technology is useless against a T0.
- **Adolf Hitler:** After Luz Long defected, he rushed the awakening of 55 Aces on unstable second-gen hardware. His whole war is a resource gamble—glass, mica, genetic stock—and it fails because God-Kings can't be rushed, bought, or forced.
- **Konrad Zuse & Hans Berger:** The engineers who built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator beneath the Berlin stadium. Their machine exploded from T0 "Backflow" seconds after Long landed.
- **Erwin Rommel:** Commander of the Afrika Korps. His real objective is not oil but mica—the Indian mica convoys running to Britain through the Mediterranean. The Mediterranean war is a running psychic naval battle over those convoys.
- **General Siegfried Westphal:** Rommel's Chief of Operations—the logistician who tracks, in grim numbers, how fast the thorium Aces are burning out.

### USA (Project Circuit-Breaker)
- **Artie Gable (Tier 1 Ace - Retired from Frontline):** "The Engineered Hero"—an Ohio State baseball pitcher and electrical engineer *(FICTIONAL; the deliberate everyman OC)*. Awakened in 1938 with IBM Punch-Cards; the "Gable Standard" (elite athletic coordination + engineering mind) is named after him. His doctrine of American standardization died in the Brazilian jungle—the first front where it failed, and it broke him. Now he trains new recruits in the Virginia "Neural Pits," the only American who has felt a German Ace's mind: "The machines won't save you." **Arc: naive → broken → mentor.** The propaganda hero whose standardization dies in Brazil against a hardened German Ace — returning not to glory but to serve as Owens's squire and warn the next generation.
- **Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1 Ace):** The greatest female athlete of her era. "The Spirit of Columbia," awakened with "Crystal-Logic" (galena and germanium instead of vacuum tubes)—the most stable psychic ever made, at a slight power cost. Global celebrity and propaganda icon. Deployed to Minas Gerais, Brazil as the Guardian of the Mica, hunting German Snap-Teams and mentoring Sylvio Padilha. **Arc: idealist → eyes-open optimist.** Sent to guard the mica, she finds the resource war is extraction and corruption — but also that the money genuinely builds (the Volta Redonda steel mill, the jobs), and stays positive for the future, invested in Padilha as the next generation.
- **Jesse Owens (Tier 0 - God of Speed):** Four-time Olympic gold medalist. The Phantom—a natural super-conductor, raw instinct over math. He was *primed* by the 1936 Backflow, then *hidden* by the country that needed him: in 1941 America, a Black God-King is a political impossibility, so he is kept secret, under "foggy" Corning glass at ~50% effectiveness. America's sin is not that it lacks a T0—it's that it refuses to use the one it has. **Arc: denied → unleashed.** One secret fight (the Atlantic Duel vs. Reitsch), witnessing Wooderson's death in the UK, then finally let loose on Germany — the American god arriving where the first god was made.
- **Cornelius Warmerdam (Tier 0 - Dormant):** "The Pole Vaulter"—cleared 15 feet 43 times before anyone else did it once, the greatest statistical outlier in track history. **Gravitational Decoupling**—he doesn't lift himself, he "forgets" gravity for three seconds. The US is terrified to awaken him; the feedback loop could level San Francisco.
- **Joe Louis (Tier 0 - Dormant):** "The Brown Bomber," 25 consecutive heavyweight title defenses. **The Kinetic Battery**—absorbs and stores any attack, releasable as a single city-leveling punch. Stationed at the White House as FDR's unawakened "Psychic Bodyguard"—his mere presence deters Snap-assassins.
- **General George S. Patton:** Training the first "Neural-Integrated Tank Corps" in the Mojave—tanks where driver and gunner are linked by low-grade vacuum-tube harnesses.
- **Undersecretary of State Sumner Welles:** Led the US delegation to Brazil, negotiating the mica monopoly and the "Rio Vault" deal with President Vargas.

### Great Britain (Project Arthur)
- **Sydney Wooderson (Tier 0):** "The Mighty Atom"—a 120-pound solicitor's clerk and world-record runner. **The Acoustic Ghost.** "Slow-Cooked" in the Bletchley machine into the UK's only T0, he performs Micro-Snaps to move like a flickering ghost. But his real role is not fighting: his humming brain is the Radar-Sync — the island's night watchman. The crude jammer boxes deny the mass; Wooderson is the one *signal* in all that noise, the sentinel who can hear a Snap coming and cancel it before it lands, and the only instrument in Britain that could ever detect a T0 whisper. He holds the watch beyond safe limits, at 104°F, by choice—**duty as consumption**. There is no relief, no one to spell him: he is burning out not from running the machine, but from never once being able to stop listening.
- **Godfrey Brown (Tier 1 Ace):** 1936 Olympic gold in the 4×400m relay (anchor leg) and silver in the 400m individual; 1938 European 400m champion. A Cambridge man (Peterhouse College) and schoolmaster by trade. Britain's active Ace—economical, precise, a "Slow-Cook" graduate who fights as a Ghost.
- **Jack Lovelock (Tier 1 Ace - Retired, New Zealand/Commonwealth):** 1936 Olympic 1500m gold medalist — New Zealand's first-ever Olympic gold — and a Rhodes Scholar and medical doctor. "The Neural Mathematician"—the most efficient TK user in history, permanently "Sooted." Britain's *first* Slow-Cook and its proof of concept, not a failure: he Sooted during the pioneering phase, pushing his own resolution past the known ceiling before the method learned to protect anyone. His sacrifice is *why* the Slow-Cook is safe now, and why Wooderson is handled with such care. Recruited into Project Arthur from the Commonwealth; now Dean of the Bletchley Neural Academy.
- **Don Finlay (Tier 1 Ace):** "The Hurdler-Pilot." 1936 Olympic silver in the 110m hurdles (bronze in 1932), and an RAF fighter pilot (DFC, AFC). A "Slow-Cook" Ace whose hurdler's rhythm maps onto Snap timing — he doesn't calculate a jump like Brown, he *paces* it. Britain's air-defense Ace: a Spitfire pilot whose Snap is tuned to the Radar-Sync, intercepting German Aces over the Channel through the long siege of the home islands — the bridge between Bletchley's radar math and the men holding the skies.
- **Dorothy Odam (Tier 1 Ace):** "The Bar." 1936 Olympic silver in the high jump, won at sixteen — the first British woman to win an individual Olympic medal in athletics — and a world-record clearance in 1939. A "Slow-Cook" Ace whose high-jump instinct maps onto vertical Snap timing: she intercepts incoming German Aces *in the air*, the mobile complement to Wooderson's fixed Radar-Sync. Where Finlay paces, she *clears* — rising to meet the attack at its apex and forcing it back over the Channel.
- **Bill Roberts (Tier 2):** 1936 Olympic 4×400m relay gold medalist (running with Godfrey Brown). A working-class Lancashire sprinter — the unglamorous grunt tier: a "Sorter" tethered to a Radar-Sync station, holding his Hum steady so the Aces and the T0 can do the glamorous work.
- **Alan Turing:** Headquartered at Bletchley Park. Developed the "Slow-Cook" method—tuning the brain over 30 days via iterative resonance—and Radar-Sync integration. He ran the probability analysis that flagged Don Bradman's 99.94 batting average as a "statistical impossibility." Developing "Operation Echo-Chamber"—thousands of low-power tube arrays to feed German Aces a hundred false targets over London.
- **Winston Churchill:** Begging FDR for Brazilian mica to save Wooderson. Authorized sharing the "Slow-Cook" math with the Americans—the UK's most guarded secret—and the secret "Neural Retrieval" of Don Bradman from Australia by flying boat.
- **General Archibald Wavell:** "The Poet General," commander of the Mediterranean mica-convoy war—quiet, cerebral, obsessed with the "Math" of attrition. The man who keeps the Indian mica flowing to Britain.
- **General Claude Auchinleck:** Alongside Wavell in the Mediterranean command.

### USSR (The Red Resonators)
- **Nina Dumbadze (Tier 1+ Alpha):** "The Red Queen," a Georgian discus world-record holder (she threw past the world mark in 1939). Commander of the "Night Witches." Pushes past safe limits—150kg to 180kg—by burning her own nerves on "Dirty Power." Her arc is patience: training, watching the West burn, knowing her moment to sweep in is coming. Tethered to an Integration Train every 72 hours or her power becomes an uncontrolled Kinetic Scream.
- **Joseph Stalin:** The cynical predator. Trades mica to Germany at extortionate prices, uses the machine tools he receives to fix his processing, and builds the largest psychic army on Earth—"Neural Scorched Earth," orphanages swept for 0.0001% Factor children, a Wall of Meat. He profits most and fights least.

### Imperial Japan (Project Kotoamatsukami)
- **Naoto Tajima (Tier 0 - Dormant):** 1936 triple-jump gold medalist (world record 16.00m, stood 15 years). "The Kinetic Spring"—could theoretically chain three Snaps into one momentum-burst hitting like a meteorite. Japan has identified him but **cannot afford to awaken him**—their machines are too crude and would kill him instantly.
- **Chūhei Nambu (Tier 1 Ace):** The only man ever to hold world records in both the long jump and the triple jump at the same time (long jump 7.98m, 1931; triple jump 15.72m WR, 1932). "The Jump King." The senior Aero-Shinobi—a jumper whose discipline is explosive precision in one motion, which maps cleanly onto the Snap. Awakened via the zen "Mushin" salt-tank method (stillness, then release). Master of the "Void-Shroud," vibrating his kinetic barrier to silence the Snap's thunderclap; uses non-magnetic obsidian shards to bypass metal sensors. The living embodiment of Japan's "glass cannon" doctrine: precise and silent, but fragile. *(Backups if a younger female ace or mentor is ever needed: Hideko Maehata, 200m breaststroke gold 1936; Mikio Oda, Japan's first Olympic gold, 1928.)*
- **Emperor Hirohito & High Command:** Under a "Total Neural Embargo" (Corning silica + tungsten cutoff), Japan pushes south for mica—Malaya and Burma—not battleships. Their ceramic-tube psychics are stable but weak, and their one T0 cannot be awakened. Poverty is their fate.

## Minor Power Characters
- **Gunder Hägg (Sweden - Tier Unknown):** World-record runner. The sole Swedish psychic, on the stable "Bofors Hybrid" machine—an Armed-Neutrality deterrent.
- **Jean Taris (France - Tier 2):** A national swimmer drafted for Project "Sainte-Geneviève," tethered to the Static Maginot's grid as a living battery for the jammers — the cost made flesh: France spends its own people to keep the wall lit.
- **Émile Allais (France - Tier 1):** Triple world champion at the 1937 alpine World Championships (downhill, slalom, combined)—the first great French skier. A Chasseur Alpin (mountain infantry) and later a Resistance fighter in the Haute-Montagne. France's *mobile* defender—a skier who "engineered a new way to move through space." His fluid precision over terrain is what still works at the edge of the Static Maginot's dead-zone, where the jammers deny everyone's Snap and only motion remains: the one Ace who can contest the border while the wall holds still. A *motion-specialist*, not a Snap-math type — the reason France kept him when it sent its calculators to Britain. *(Backup: Jules Ladoumègue, WR 1500m, banned for life.)*
- **Robert Charpentier (France - Tier 1):** "The Butcher's Boy." 1936 Olympic triple gold medalist (individual road race, team road race, team pursuit)—a delivery-boy turned cyclist. His gift is endurance, not burst: a T1 built for the long hold, the Ace who outlasts what he can't overpower. Alongside Allais, the living heart of the Static Maginot's two-and-a-half-year siege — herding German Aces into lit sectors where their Snap fails and only motion wins.
- **Guy Lapébie (France - Tier 2):** 1936 Olympic gold (team road race, team pursuit) and silver (individual road race, 0.2 seconds behind Charpentier). A cyclist's stamina turned to the grid: a "living battery" tethered to the jammer network, his steady Hum feeding the dead-zone longer than a normal T2 can endure — the T2 France awakened and immediately sacrificed.

### Brazil (The "Southern Cross" Project)
- **Sylvio de Magalhães Padilha (Tier 1+ Prospect - Dormant):** World-class 400m hurdler, 1936 Berlin Olympic finalist, Lieutenant in the Brazilian Army. Was on the track when Luz Long made his "Miracle Jump"—bathed in the Psychic Backflow, his brain was "primed" with a low-voltage pre-charge. Discovered by Babe Didrikson in Rio; conductivity rating higher than Artie's, almost at Owens level. Training under Babe before entering the "Rio Vault" in Petrópolis. Claims Luz Long "Whispered" something to him in 1936 that he's never forgotten.
- **President Getúlio Vargas:** Master of the "Pendulum Policy." Signed the "Mica Fortress" deal—Brazilian mica to the Allies in exchange for an Integration Vault in Petrópolis, Brazilian Aces, and the Volta Redonda steel mill. Demanded Padilha be the first awakened.

### Italy (Project Marconi)
- **Ondina Valla (Tier 0 - Dormant):** First Italian woman to win Olympic gold (1936 hurdles). All-rounder: high jump, long jump, sprints. **Harmonic Synchronization**—can tune her brain to match the frequency of any machine. Under Gestapo surveillance (they think she's just a T1 candidate). Italy is attempting a secret "Radio-Frequency Resonance" awakening—a machine that "sings" rather than "forces"—likely hidden beneath the Vatican or a Tuscan villa.

### Finland (The Wild Card)
- **Viljo Heino (Tier 1 - Dormant):** "The Flying Finn," a promising young distance runner (his 10,000m world records are still years away). **Metabolic Infinite-Loop**—zero metabolic friction, could hold 100% Ramp-Up for 48 hours without food or sleep. Finland's dormant bargaining chip, unawakened by a "Frankenstein" machine built from captured Soviet tubes and Swedish gears.

### Australia (Commonwealth Secret)
- **Don Bradman (Tier 0 - Dormant):** Career batting average of 99.94—the next best is 60. A "statistical impossibility." **Total Resolution**—100% hand-eye-kinetic coordination; could Flick a needle through the eye of a needle from a mile away. Discovered by Turing through probability analysis. Churchill has ordered a secret "Neural Retrieval" by flying boat.

# Psychic Revolution - Characters & Tiers

## Tier System
- **Tier 0 (The Singularities / God-Kings):** Natural super-conductors or perfect mathematical ascendants. Produce no thunderclaps when Snapping (Silent or "Whisper-Snap"). Still require recalibration and massive calorie intake, but far more efficient than lower tiers. Sub-divided into: **God-King** (Luz Long—Nature + Nurture, 99% efficiency), **Gods** (Natural super-conductors like Jesse Owens—raw instinct, ~80% efficiency), **Kings** (Math-ascended like Wooderson—discipline over nature, ~80% efficiency but higher maintenance).
- **Tier 1 (The Aces):** Elite, highly trained engineered heroes. Require constant calibration in integration vaults or else their power becomes "blurry" and dangerous. ~70% efficiency—massive heat waste as "Neural Soot."
- **Tier 2/3 (Disposables / The Grunts):** Mass-produced, unstable soldiers with clumsy resolution and short lifespans before they suffer from "Neural Soot" (brain exhaustion).

**THE "NO FREE T0" RULE (locked):** A Tier 0 is a *strategic weapon, not a soldier* — same hard ceiling as a T1 (150kg/200mph/6–7 Snaps/100y) but instant, silent, tireless, and self-sustaining. An awake, willing T0 ends wars. Therefore **every awake T0 in the story must be leashed** — neutralized, constrained, or fading. A free T0 collapses the plot, and no nation should ever have more than ~1 awake T0. Dormant T0s are not roster weight: they are future levers (deterrence, post-war transistor race, "who gets woken next"). Count dormant T0s as potential, not as active power.

## Key Figures

### Independents & Defectors
- **Luz Long (Tier 0 - God-King):** The first awakened. A German law student and elite jumper. The incident: During the August 1936 Long Jump finals, he glided in the air for six seconds, landing double the record distance with glowing blue eyes and a kinetic ripple that threw sand 30 feet. Realizing he was a prisoner of the SS, he defected in May 1939, "Snapped" across the border, and is currently interned in a lead-lined Swiss Alpine bunker beneath the Gotthard Pass. **The only God-King—99% neural efficiency, needs only 4,000 calories/day, can go months without recalibration. But morally gray: stays in his gilded cage because the SS holds his wife Gisela and infant son Kai-Heinrich as hostages in a Berlin "Honorary Guest Villa" (prison). Has performed a cold, lawyerly calculation: his family's lives are worth more to him than the millions being Hollowed. Rationalizes his silence through legalism ("I am a neutral citizen"), escalation fear ("my intervention only speeds the meat-grinder"), and private peace ("I broke the world in 1936—why must I fix it?"). Spends his days reading law books, looking at his son's photograph, feeling the Neural Screams of dying psychics across the border—and doing nothing. The "Magnus Carlsen" of a game he refuses to play. Would only emerge if Operation Sea Lion or the Fall of Moscow made neutrality morally untenable.**
- **Fanny Blankers-Koen (Tier 1 - Captive):** "The Flying Housewife." A 23-year-old Dutch athlete in Amsterdam. *(AUDIT: downgraded T0→T1 — her "statistical anomaly" fame is post-war, 1948; pre-1941 she is a promising but not yet dominant talent.)* **Temporal Multi-Tasking**—processes time 10x faster than normal humans. **Awakened October 1941 in the Eindhoven "Goddess-Throne" (120,000 tubes, 80 Megawatts, Indian Ruby Mica + Dutch Glass). Her eyes glow Gold—the hallmark of Temporal Multi-tasking. Fitted with the "Eindhoven Leash": a brass collar tethered to a 40lb "Backpack Vault" (miniaturized Dutch tubes + Mica-battery). The backpack allows mobile recalibration—she's the most mobile psychic in the world—but contains a kill-switch: if she Snaps more than 500 yards from a German-authorized Sync-Signal, it triggers Neural Seizure (paralysis for hours). Must return to Eindhoven every 48 hours for Deep-Sync. Husband Jan Blankers held at gunpoint in the observation room during her awakening. Needs 18,000 calories/day via feeding tube in harness. Stationed at Pas-de-Calais, France—her mission is to intercept Jesse Owens if he tries to Snap across the English Channel. An engineered T1 pushed to her ceiling: mobile, but slaved to a backpack and a hostage husband.**

### Germany (Aero-Jaeger Legion)
- **Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer (Tier 1 Ace):** Former **motorcycle racer and SS officer**. The Aggressor. Fastest Ramp-Up of any non-Tier 0 (12 seconds). Uses the "Loud Snap"—weaponizes the thunderclap to stun enemies before "Flicking" tungsten needles into them. Led the Tczew Bridge mission in Poland. Led **Staffel-Desert in North Africa**. Hardened by Poland and France. **Defeated at Halfaya Pass by Artie Gable's liquid nitrogen canister trick—humiliated but survived. Currently undergoing the "Great Refit" at the Philips Factory: being fitted with a "Tier-1 Alpha" harness combining Dutch Glass with Indian Mica to eliminate his Neural Jitter. Post-Refit, his hands are perfectly steady and his resolution has doubled. Personally captured Fanny Blankers-Koen in the "Amsterdam Blackout" raid.**
- **Gottfried von Cramm (Tier 1 Ace):** Known as "The Baron." **World-class tennis champion**. The Sniper. High-resolution "Flicking." Rarely Snaps, preferring to fly at high altitude and snipe from miles away with needle-thin darts. Refit with Philips "Gold-Pin" tubes. *AUDIT: clean of the El Kantara kill (no longer canon).*
- **Gisela Uhlen (Tier 1 Ace - Refit):** Famous **German actress and gymnast**. The Valkyrie. Master of "Kinetic Multi-tasking"—can maintain a flight barrier for an entire bomber squadron while independently attacking three different targets. The face of Nazi "Neural Propaganda" to counter Babe Didrikson. **Led the charge at El Kantara, creating a "Kinetic Slaught" wedge-barrier that deflected every British anti-tank shell. Her Dutch glass tubes glow steady, cool gold—unlike the jittery Meyer, she is calm.**
- **Hanna Reitsch (Tier 1 Ace - DECOMMISSIONED):** The Test Pilot. **Specializes in "Vertical Snaps" from the stratosphere.** During the Suez fall, she Snapped onto the deck of a British Mica transport ship mid-canal, "Flicked" the crew into the water, and secured 400 tons of Indian Mica. **Sent to intercept the USS Hornet carrying Jesse Owens across the Atlantic (October 1941). Confident and dismissive: "The American is a circus act." Engaged Owens in the "Atlantic Duel"—her Refit sensors couldn't track his Null-Frequency. Owens appeared behind her and simply tapped her primary integration tube. The Tier 0 touch caused instant Neural Blowout—her primary tube vaporized, kinetic barrier shattered. Survived but permanently decommissioned as a combat psychic. Her defeat proved the "God-Gap": Refit technology is useless against a Tier 0.**
- **Adolf Hitler:** Rushed the awakening of 55 Tier-1 Aces on unstable 2nd-gen hardware after Luz Long defected. **Now ecstatic—has the Mica, has the Glass, believes he has "Solved" the God-equation. Orders immediate launch of Operation Barbarossa.**
- **Konrad Zuse & Hans Berger:** German engineers who built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator beneath the 1936 Berlin stadium. Their machine exploded from Tier 0 "Backflow" seconds after Long landed.
- **Erwin Rommel:** Commander of the Afrika Korps. In North Africa, his secret objective is not oil, but seizing the Mica Pipeline at the Suez Canal. **Successfully captured the Suez in April 1941. Stood on the banks opening a crate of Indian Mica with his dagger: "With this, Meyer's jitter will vanish. We have the Glass from Eindhoven, and now we have the Shield from India. The 'Knight' is finally complete."**
- **General Siegfried Westphal:** Rommel's Chief of Operations in North Africa. Warned Rommel that Meyer was "fraying" and would become a Hollow within 30 days without the Suez Mica.

### USA (Project Circuit-Breaker)
- **Artie Gable (Tier 1 Ace - Retired from Frontline):** "The Engineered Hero." **Ohio State baseball pitcher and electrical engineer (FICTIONAL).** Awakened in 1938 using IBM Punch-Cards. The "Gable Standard" recruitment protocol is named after him—finding candidates with both elite athletic coordination AND engineering/mathematical minds. Defeated at Halfaya Pass and humiliated by the Suez fall. **Pulled from psychiatric-neural ward in Virginia (twitching, unable to speak). Now assigned as Lead Instructor at the Virginia "Neural Pits"—training new recruits. The only American who has felt Kurt Meyer's mind. Teaches: "The machines won't save you; the Germans don't play by the math." His "Standardization" doctrine is dead, but his experience is invaluable.**
- **Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1 Ace):** **Greatest female athlete of history (Gold medalist in hurdles/javelin).** "The Spirit of Columbia." Awakened with "Crystal-Logic" (Galena and Germanium crystals instead of vacuum tubes), making her the most stable psychic created. Requires 50% less recalibration than Gable. **Global celebrity and propaganda icon.** **Currently deployed to Minas Gerais, Brazil as the Guardian of the Mica—hunting German "Snap-Teams" in the jungle. Also serving as mentor to Sylvio Padilha. Her Crystal-Logic gives her 100% stability but slightly lower power output (130kg TK limit vs 150kg standard).**
- **Jesse Owens (Tier 0 - God of Speed):** **4-time Olympic Gold Medalist.** The Phantom. Natural super-conductor. "God" type—raw instinct over math. Achieved "Recalibration Independence" in 1940. Faster and quieter than Artie or Babe. **Unveiled to the public on September 25, 1941 in "The Hour of the Phantom" broadcast—performed Silent Snap, Multi-Vector after-images, plucked a flag from a general's lapel from 200 yards. FDR: "The Germans call themselves Knights; the Japanese call themselves Ghosts. But we have the Phantom." Currently in London at Bletchley Park. Defeated Hanna Reitsch in the "Atlantic Duel" (October 1941)—simply tapped her primary tube, causing instant Neural Blowout. But the engagement left him "Dirty"—arrived in London with nosebleed and migraine. Now being prepared for a dangerous "Neural-Link" with comatose Sydney Wooderson to learn the "King's Math." Needs 15,000 calories/day in combat, recalibration every ~2 weeks. Limited by American "Foggy" Corning Glass—only ~50% of his potential effectiveness. Morally clear but inexperienced—let the German squires live, which some see as a liability. If he met Luz Long now, would likely feel disgust, not admiration.**
- **Cornelius Warmerdam (Tier 0 - Dormant):** "The Pole Vaulter." **Greatest statistical outlier in track history—cleared 15 feet 43 times before anyone else did it once.** **Gravitational Decoupling**—doesn't lift himself, simply "forgets" gravity exists for 3 seconds. Currently a Lieutenant in the US Navy. The US is terrified to awaken him—his power would make him impossible to tether, and the feedback loop could level San Francisco.
- **Joe Louis (Tier 0 - Dormant):** "The Brown Bomber." **25 consecutive heavyweight title defenses. The Kinetic Battery.** Doesn't fly or teleport—condenses kinetic energy. Any attack absorbed and stored, releasable in a single city-leveling punch. Currently in US Army training camps (propaganda role). Stationed at the White House as FDR's "Psychic Bodyguard"—his mere presence deters German "Snap-Assassins." Unawakened; the US doesn't know how to handle a "Kinetic Battery" of his magnitude.
- **General George S. Patton:** Currently in **California (Mojave Desert)** training the first "Neural-Integrated Tank Corps"—tanks where driver and gunner are linked by low-grade vacuum tube harnesses. Has not yet deployed to the front.
- **Undersecretary of State Sumner Welles:** Led the US delegation to Brazil, negotiating the 100% mica monopoly and the "Rio Vault" deal with President Vargas.

Body temperature at constant 104°F. Without a "Refit" of Brazilian or Indian Mica, his nervous system will "Carbonize" by August 1941. Most efficient TK user in history—could down a bomber with a single "Flick" to the fuel line. Permanently "Sooted." His brain can no longer handle a Ramp-Up. Now the **Dean of the Bletchley Neural Academy**, training the next generation.

Authorized the secret "Neural Retrieval" of Don Bradman from Australia via modified Short Sunderland flying boat.**

Quiet, cerebral, deeply concerned with the "Math" of the war. **After the Suez fell, he confronted the American command with pure cold rage—blamed American "Standardization" for the loss of the canal and the British Tier-1 presence in Africa. Demanded the US release Jesse Owens.**

Demanded Padilha be the first awakened.

# Opening State — the board at the war's start

Method: no quantities, only commitments. Each nation is answered through seven fixed fields, taken at two moments — **August 1936** (right after the Olympic Miracle) and **March 1939** (the eve of the Netherlands invasion). The 1936 column makes the 1939 column *legitimate*; the gap between them is the scramble. The war simulates forward from the 1939 column.

The seven fields:
1. **Center of gravity** — the one asset every other decision serves.
2. **Doctrine** — how they fight, and what Spain taught them.
3. **Psychic inventory + pipeline** — awake now, dormant, and what *one more* costs in time/risk.
4. **Resources** — have / need / choke point.
5. **Beliefs** — what they know vs. assume vs. are wrong about.
6. **Commitments already spent** — sunk gambles that lock future moves.
7. **Fragility** — the single thing that breaks them.

---

## Locked Resource Rules (the ground truth every snapshot must obey)

- **Mica is a physics discovery, not an espionage coup.** Germany's tube tech needing V-1 mica is a wall every nation's own physicists hit independently — parallel research, not super-spies. Geology is public; the *dependency* is the discovery.
- **Muscovite mica has four owners, three are Allied.** India (British), Brazil (US-leaning), USA (Carolinas), USSR (Mama-Chuya). The USSR is the only non-Allied holder — a fact of the mineral map, not a diplomatic choice. Any mica reaching Germany via a neutral broker is still Soviet or Allied mica in disguise. There is no independent source.
- **Germany has no domestic thorium deposit.** Thorium comes from monazite sands (India, Brazil, Madagascar, the US Carolinas); Germany was the world's leading *processor*, not producer. Its thorium is a **pre-war stockpile** — it runs down like oil. Two clocks, not one: the *stockpile clock* (thorium depletes) and the *radiation clock* (Aces sicken in 6–12 months without mica).
- **The embargo is a materials embargo, not a mass embargo.** Britain controls both the mica and the monazite and cuts Germany off with one colonial switch — 1937–38 strategic-material restrictions, not the full blockade of 1939.
- **Glass stretches mica; it never replaces it.** Mica's crystal lattice is irreplaceable. Superior glass lets Germany stretch lower-grade Soviet mica further — better, never solved. That hubris is what France's wall punishes.

## Germany (Aero-Jaeger Legion)

### Snapshot 1 — August 1936, right after the Miracle

1. **Center of gravity:** Luz Long — the only God-King on Earth, and the center of a bluff. The program had been running since ~1933 producing only **Hollows** (hidden lab failures). The Olympics was the forced showcase — Hitler demanded an "Aryan superman" on the world's stage — so they staged the awakening beneath the Long Jump, calibrated for a modest, *controlled* T1. The Backflow detonated the machine and made a God-King instead.
2. **Doctrine:** None. The world has just learned psychics exist. Germany has one asset and zero theory. The immediate crisis is diplomatic, not military — the world watched a secret weapon detonate under a stadium during the Olympics.
3. **Psychic inventory + pipeline:** Luz Long (T0, awake — stalling from day one, "useful but not ready"). Six years of disowned Hollows behind him. Zuse & Berger's integrator destroyed in the Backflow; the blueprints damaged. No pipeline: they have the subject but not the science.
4. **Resources:** Schott glass (Jena — the world's best optical glass). A pre-war **thorium stockpile** (no domestic deposit). **No mica.** No Philips glass-sealing tech (Dutch). No Czech quartz/logic gates (not until Munich, 1938).
5. **Beliefs:** Luz Long can be controlled and coerced — a superweapon to brandish. Germany holds a decisive head start. The world's fear can be managed into deference. *(Wrong on the first — he will never deploy.)*
6. **Commitments spent:** The secret program itself — now exposed, and with it the six years of Hollows Germany will deny ever made. The trust of every other nation — gone, replaced by fear.
7. **Fragility:** One man who does not want to cooperate, and no surviving science. If Luz Long refuses, Germany has nothing but a bluff it cannot afford to have called.

### Snapshot 2 — March 1939, eve of the Netherlands invasion

1. **Center of gravity:** The thorium Ace program — a mass-awakening bet on unstable, powerful Aces. Luz Long is gone (bolted Feb 1939), and with him the bluff. The war machine now runs on the Aces it can rush.
2. **Doctrine:** Spain-proven aggression. Snap-assassination, TK flicking, forced Hollowing — tested against partisans and unarmed civilians, who offered no resistance. The conclusion: *psychics are unstoppable.* Doctrine is power-over-patience: hit hard, win fast, don't let the fight become a war of attrition.
3. **Psychic inventory + pipeline:** ~55 Aces rushed onto unstable second-gen hardware — a **panic, not a plan**, thrown at the board the moment Luz Long bolted. **No T0, and no path to one** — the forced-awakening of a T0 has already failed in principle. Every Ace is on a 6–12 month thorium clock. Pipeline = rush, not refine.
4. **Resources:** Schott glass + Czech engineering (quartz, Skoda logic gates — from Munich, Sept 1938). A **depleting** thorium stockpile. Philips glass-sealing tech = the *imminent objective*, not yet in hand. **Still no mica** — buying low-grade Soviet rock through the Mica-for-Machines flow, stretched further by superior glass but never solved. Choke point: mica.
5. **Beliefs:** Psychics are unstoppable (Spain proved it). The war can be won fast. The thorium burnout is *real* — the first cohort sickened in late '38 — but suppressed: the countdown is a secret nobody in Berlin will say aloud. Luz Long's defection is hidden ("medical convalescence"). And France won't hold — nobody in Berlin believes a wall can stop a Snap. *(Wrong on the last one — that is exactly what the Static Maginot is built to do.)*
6. **Commitments spent:** The Netherlands invasion itself (secure Philips before the Dutch sabotage it). The 55-Ace rush. The Luz Long cover story and the hostage arrangement (Gisela & Kai). The bet that Spain's lesson generalizes.
7. **Fragility:** Two clocks. The thorium stockpile runs down like oil, and the fix for both clocks — mica — is the one thing Germany has four owners for and can reach none of. Win fast or the army burns out where it stands.

## Great Britain (Project Arthur)

### Snapshot 1 — August 1936, right after the Miracle

1. **Center of gravity:** The Indian mica supply chain — Britain controls ~75% of the world's high-grade sheet muscovite through the Empire, and does not yet know how decisive that will be. Where Germany's 1936 asset (Luz Long) is a bluff it overvalues, Britain's is a crown it hasn't noticed.
2. **Doctrine:** None. Britain reacts as it always has — defensively, behind sea power. The instinct is already "hold the island," but there is no theory of psychic war yet.
3. **Psychic inventory + pipeline:** Zero psychics, no open program. What Britain has is the *infrastructure* — Mullard's EF50 radar-tube line, the Bletchley brain trust, and a culture that treats this as an engineering problem, not a mystical one.
4. **Resources:** Indian muscovite (V-1, ~75% of world supply), Canadian phlogopite, the Mullard line. **Lacks:** no domestic glass of Schott quality, and no idea that its mica is the war's decisive mineral.
5. **Beliefs:** Psychics are a German problem to be contained, not copied. The Empire and the Navy keep Britain safe. There is time. *(Wrong on all three.)*
6. **Commitments spent:** The posture of imperial complacency — and the Empire's *open* sea lanes, the same openness that carries mica home also carries vulnerability from a power that can Snap.
7. **Fragility:** The sea lanes. Everything Britain has arrives by ship, through the Mediterranean, past a hostile Europe. Sever that thread and the island starves — of mica, of everything.

### Snapshot 2 — March 1939, eve of the Netherlands invasion

1. **Center of gravity:** Wooderson — the T0 "Acoustic Ghost," awakened January 1939 and hidden. His humming brain is the Radar-Sync keeping the island from being Snapped into, and he is already burning himself out holding it. Every other decision serves keeping him alive and the array running.
2. **Doctrine:** Arthur — denial over destruction. Jammers ("noise is cheaper than signal") plus Slow-Cook precision Aces. The stance: make the island too expensive to touch. Britain can't win; it can only not-lose.
3. **Psychic inventory + pipeline:** Wooderson (T0, awake, hidden — terrified of Luz Long). Lovelock (T1, Sooted/retired — the first Slow-Cook, now Dean of the Bletchley Academy). The active cohort (Brown, Finlay, Odam) emerging one 30-day Slow-Cook at a time. Roberts (T2 Sorter). Pipeline = Slow-Cook: low volume, high precision.
4. **Resources:** Indian muscovite (~75% of world high-grade sheet, contested at sea), Canadian phlogopite, the Mullard EF50 line, the Slow-Cook math (Britain's most guarded secret). **Lacks:** glass manufacturing scale — Mullard famously can't mass-produce the all-glass tube bases (the EF50 irony). Choke point: the Mediterranean lanes the mica must run.
5. **Beliefs:** The island can be held. Wooderson's burnout can be managed. The Low Countries Guarantee will deter Germany. Luz Long's status is *suspected* but unconfirmed. *(Wrong on the first three — and the cost of "holding" will eat Britain alive.)*
6. **Commitments spent:** The Low Countries Guarantee (the casus belli). The Radar-Sync grid — the island's power cannibalized for the array. The colonial switch (1937–38) that cut Germany off from Indian mica and monazite. The Slow-Cook program that cost its first pilot.
7. **Fragility:** One man *is* the defense — Wooderson burns at 104°F by choice — and a defense-only power can never retake Europe. If he dies, the island opens.

# Psychic Revolution - New Technology

The psychic arms race didn't add a weapon; it rebuilt every weapon around one question — how do you support, contain, or kill a psychic? This file is the *hardware* layer: the machines, tubes, and mundane countermeasures of the war. For *what psychics can do*, see `PSYCHICS.md`; for *who holds what*, see `COUNTRIES.md`.

## The "Holy Grail" Vacuum Tube

To safely *awaken* a high-level psychic — especially a Tier 0 — without catastrophic failure, a specific composition of tube is required. This is the technological benchmark of the war.

- **The Envelope (Type-1720 Aluminosilicate Glass):** "Hard glass" that survives the thermal shock of a Ramp-Up with zero "outgassing" — unlike cheap soda-lime glass, it holds a perfect vacuum under load.
- **The Seal (Kovar Alloy):** an iron-nickel-cobalt alloy whose thermal expansion matches aluminosilicate glass exactly, producing a seamless, bubble-free glass-to-metal lock that won't crack when heated.
- **The Insulator (V-1 Grade Clear Muscovite Mica):** perfectly clear, water-transparent, flawless sheet mica — maximum dielectric strength, no micro-arcing between grids, rigid under heat.
- **The Engine (Thoriated Tungsten Filament):** maximum dense, high-frequency electron emission for a Tier 0 Ramp-Up. Runs dangerously hot — and without V-1 mica to insulate it, it's what kills German Aces in 6–12 months.

**The "Hollow" Cause:** pair standard glass (which expands unevenly) with V-5 cloudy mica (tiny mineral inclusions) and the seal fractures while the mica arcs — a chaotic, unshielded surge hits the brain mid-awakening. That is what makes a Hollow. It isn't malice; it's a $2 sheet of cloudy mica and a hairline crack in the glass.

## The Tube Ladder — "Brittle Gold"

The Holy Grail isn't one invention; it's the **top of four independently-graded columns**, and a tube is only as good as its *worst* component. That's why the perfect tube is "brittle gold" — priceless, but one hairline crack or one cloudy flake turns it to junk.

### The Generations of the War (the quick ladder)

- **Gen 1 — "Junk tubes" (1936–37).** Soda-lime glass, soldered pins, V-5 mica, oxide filaments. Crude national prototypes; broken T2s only; a near-total Hollow rate. (Spain, the Dutch Crisis.)
- **Gen 2 — "The first stable Aces" (1937–39).** Borosilicate and aluminosilicate envelopes, V-3 mica, oxide filaments — plus national safety tricks: the US Soft-Fuse, the UK Slow-Cook, and, off the ladder entirely, Japan's ceramic workaround. The first reliable T1s; defense (jammers) outpaces offense.
- **Gen 3 — "The Great Refit" (1939–41).** Germany finally pairs its crown glass (Schott + Philips + Czech) with captured Indian mica: thoriated-tungsten Aces at full resolution — deadly, and radioactive. The Allies pair Corning + Kovar + Indian mica: stable but "foggy." The two philosophies at their absolute peak.
- **Gen 4 — "Collapse" (1942–44).** The mica runs out. German tubes arc and shatter, Aces die of radiation. Nobody ever builds the Holy Grail at scale — and then the EPP bomb makes the entire ladder obsolete, pushing the race toward solid-state silicon.

### The Four Columns (the deep dive)

| Component | Job | The ladder (worst → best) |
|---|---|---|
| **Envelope** (glass) | hold the vacuum under heat without outgassing | soda-lime → borosilicate → aluminosilicate (Corning 1720) → Schott low-expansion |
| **Seal** | lock glass to metal without cracking | soldered pins → Kovar alloy (Westinghouse, 1936) |
| **Insulator** (mica) | stop arcing between the grids | V-6/V-5 cloudy → V-3/V-2 → V-1 clear muscovite |
| **Engine** (filament) | emit a dense electron cloud | oxide cathode → thoriated tungsten |

The **Holy Grail is the rightmost cell of every row.** And the trap is baked into the engine column: thoriated tungsten is the *only* filament hot enough for a Tier 0 Ramp-Up, and it is safe only when paired with V-1 mica — the one combination that never lands in the same hands. Germany runs thorium from a **pre-war stockpile** (no domestic deposit) but has no mica, so its Aces burn out in 6–12 months; the Allies have the mica but no thorium process, so their tubes run stable but "foggy" and never reach Tier 0 power. The crown isn't the poison — the *separation* of the crowns is.

### Who can actually build it

No nation ever holds all four crowns at once — that is the entire war in one line. Germany has the glass but no mica; Britain has the mica but can't scale the glass; America has the Kovar, the capital, and the safety — but "foggy" glass and no domestic sheet mica; the USSR has the purest raw rock and purged hands that ruin it; the Netherlands had the seals and was swallowed; Japan has neither and retreats into ceramic. *(Per-nation capability at a glance: see `COUNTRIES.md`.)*

> **Story emphasis (develop later):** each time a nation climbs a rung, it should land as a *moment* — America cracking the Kovar seal (1936), Germany seizing Philips (1939), the Great Refit (1941), Japan's ceramic workaround. Every "if *this* nation pulled *that* off, it's big" is a hook to pull on later.

## Awakening & Support Infrastructure

- **Integration Vaults (Zuse-Gates / IBM Brain Mappers):** room-sized supercomputers running thousands of precision tubes, built to spike a human's brain frequency until the psychic muscle unlocks.
- **Recalibration Arrays / Integration Trains:** T1 and T2 brains are engineered by fragile analog tech, so they drift — they must be tethered back to the machines for periodic "Deep-Sync" or go insane and die. The USSR mounts its arrays on Integration Trains rolling behind the front line, out of brutal necessity.
- **Psi-Rations & Smes-7:** 10,000+ calorie sludge and energy bars of animal fat, glucose, and amphetamines, so a psychic burns fuel instead of their own muscle.

## Psychic Enhancements

- **Gable Goggles / Zeiss Monocles:** visual arrays projecting crosshairs and math grids onto the field of view, so Tier 1s *calculate* Snaps geometrically — low-error "Graph-Paper" jumps — instead of feeling them.
- **Dumb-Fire Tungsten Bearings & Obsidian Shards:** TK-propelled projectiles replacing guns. Japan uses non-magnetic volcanic obsidian to slip past metal-detection sensors.

## Countermeasures — Jammers & Denial

- **Static Jammers:** the war's great equalizer, and the reason defense leads offense. Massive towers broadcasting "Neural Noise": within roughly 10–20 miles a psychic can't focus enough to Ramp-Up or Snap, making a city "Neural-Black." But the jammer is a *denial zone, not a shield*, and it pays for that with four hard limits: it is **power-hungry** to the point of national commitment (a city is feasible; a 50-mile front eats an entire grid); **indiscriminate** (your own psychics can't operate inside it either); **stationary and fragile** (bomb the plant and the zone collapses); and it **blocks teleportation and low flight, not everything** (a psychic can fly above it or Flick in from outside).

## The EPP Weapon (Electro-Psychic Pulse)

- The logical endpoint of jammer physics: if noise *denies* the frequency, a single catastrophic pulse *destroys* it. Oppenheimer's team builds it instead of an atomic bomb — a 50-mile "Psychic Vacuum" that Hollows every psychic in range, shatters every tube, and blacks out every Integration Vault. Demonstrated once, on the Japanese psychic fleet; Japan surrenders within 48 hours. The first "Neural-Silent" zone is written into the peace.

## Captive & Experimental Technology

**The Backpack Vault & Leash (Germany — Fanny Blankers-Koen):**

- **The Collar:** a brass collar pumping a parasitic frequency, tethered to a 40-lb backpack of miniaturized Dutch tubes and a mica battery.
- **The Kill-Switch:** Snap more than 500 yards from a German sync-signal and the pack triggers Neural Backflow — instant seizure, hours of paralysis. It won't kill her (too valuable); it just makes escape impossible.
- **The Dependency:** her Temporal Multi-Tasking makes her brain drift faster than a normal T1's — she must Deep-Sync every 48 hours or lose temporal perception, blind in a world of static.
- **The Feeding Tube:** an integrated harness delivering 18,000 calories a day.
- **Purpose:** the most controlled psychic in the world, and a slave. Germany's proof that a God-King can't be manufactured — they spent a factory and a champion, and got only a leashed T1.

**The Eindhoven Throne (Germany's T0 Integrator):**

- A 120,000-tube, 80-megawatt vault built beneath the captured Philips factory — the machine that was supposed to force a Tier 0 out of a Tier 1. It failed. T0s are born, not made. The Throne sits empty, and Fanny is what came out instead.

## Conventional Hardware, "Psychic-Proofed"

### Aircraft

- **Spitfire Mk V "Acoustic Wings":** leading edges lined with microphones to hear an Ace's Hum before visual range; magnesium "Blind-Cannons" that burst into white light to stop a psychic "Mapping" a Snap onto the plane. Its role shifts from killer to flying neural detector.

### Tanks

- **Flush-Welded Construction:** no external rivets or bolts — a psychic can TK-"unzip" a riveted tank like a tin can.
- **Electromagnetic Hatches:** stop a Blinking psychic from Snapping onto the roof and lifting the hatch.
- **"Static Paint":** lead-and-mica coating that dampens the crew's Hum signature, making the tank hard to sense through walls.
- **Internal Dampening Bolts:** shielding layers that keep a psychic from simply crushing the crew inside the shell.

### Naval — CV-N (Neural Carrier)

- **Integration Hall:** a lead-lined chamber amidships holding 100,000 tubes, cooled by seawater and powered by the boilers — the only mobile platform that can Deep-Sync an Ace mid-ocean.
- **"Snap-Pads":** clear, magnetic-ringed zones on a shortened deck where an Ace can materialize without Shunting into the superstructure.
- **Aircraft Role:** planes become "taxis" — fly an Ace out, drop them, loiter during the strike.
- **Shield Destroyers:** escorts covered in concrete acoustic mirrors and tube sensors, watching for Void-Shroud psychics before they Snap aboard.

### Infantry "God-Killer" Kit

- **Boys .55 Caliber AT Rifle:** standard for psychic-hunter squads; the only man-portable round with the kinetic energy to shatter a barrier and the tubes in the neck-harness.
- **Magnesium "Flicker" Flares & "Flicker-Grenades":** Snap requires line-of-sight, so blinding light and Mylar-and-magnesium clouds make the air mathematically impossible to Snap into without a Shunt.
- **"Squire" Neural-First-Aid Kit:** liquid-nitrogen spray for a Sooting Ace's neck and high-glucose Psi-Bars.
- **Piano Wire Traps:** neck-height wire between trees — a Micro-Snapping psychic teleports into a guillotine.
- **Bamboo Punji Stakes:** jungle-rot-coated stakes under mud — a psychic can catch bullets, but can't "see" stakes while focused on the Hum.

## Recruitment: The "Gable Standard"

- **Stage 1 — Berger-Meter Test:** a conductivity check; 99% are rejected to conventional service.
- **Stage 2 — IBM Punch-Card Mapping:** a 12-hour psychological evaluation to test whether the "Neural Lattice" can handle Snap math.
- **Stage 3 — The Surge:** the 12-hour Vault awakening. Only the 0.0001% with true Neural Conductivity survive; the failures Hollow. The American safety advantage is the Westinghouse "Soft-Fuse" — it cuts power before the brain melts, so its failures become "Veterans of the New Frontier" instead of Hollows.

## Communications: The "Neural-Logic Teleprinter"

- Lead-lined encryption machines that use a tethered psychic's Hum to scramble radio traffic — un-decodable even by German math. It replaces in-person summits, because a single Ace could Snap into a conference and decapitate the leadership.

# Psychic Revolution - Logistics & Economy

War is not won purely on the battlefield; it is won on the supply line. In 1941, the global arms race is entirely dictated by the extreme economic and logistical burdens of maintaining psychic weapons. 

## The High Cost of an Ace
Psychics are not easily replaceable heroes; they are incredibly fragile, high-maintenance national assets.
- **Financial Investment:** Creating and tuning a single Tier-1 Ace (like an Artie Gable) costs approximately **$75 Million**—equivalent to manufacturing 500 P-51 Mustang fighter planes. 
- **The Energy Barrier:** You cannot build a god with a wall outlet. Booting up an Integration Vault to awaken a single U.S. psychic requires diverting the *entire* energy output of the Norris Hydroelectric Dam. For 12 hours, the state of Tennessee goes black just to awaken one Ace. Train Recalibrations in the USSR burn enough coal to power small cities.

## The Global Resource War: Glass vs. Mica
Traditional resources like oil take a backseat. The two most critical strategic resources in the world are High-Purity Silica (Glass) and Natural Sheet Mica. This dynamic causes massive geopolitical shifts (like fighting a "Jungle War" in the Amazon solely to secure mining rights).

### High-Purity Silica (The Glass)
*   **The Use:** Blowing perfectly shaped vacuum tubes that amplify and align neural frequencies without shorting out.
*   **The German Monopoly:** Germany bypassed the "Static Maginot" line in France specifically to capture the Netherlands, securing the Philips Factory intact. Germany currently controls 80% of the world's high-purity glass, allowing them to mass-produce disposable "Gen-3" psychic shields for their Panzers.
*   **The Allied Disadvantage:** The US has the IBM technology, but their glass isn't as pure, causing their psychics' spatial mapping to "fog," limiting their overall resolution.

### Natural Sheet Mica (The Insulator)
*   **The Use:** The only material boasting a high enough dielectric strength to insulate the integration vaults and sub-dermal tube harnesses from massive "Psychic Flux" without melting. 
*   **The Allied Monopoly:** Natural Mica is largely sourced from British India (and later, Brazil). It gives British and US machines absolute stability without brain damage.
*   **The German Disadvantage:** Starved by the British blockade, Germany relies on "Fluor-Mica"—a synthetic alternative created by IG Farben. However, synthetic mica does not perfectly sync with the brain's "Hum." 
*   **The Consequence (Neural Jitter):** Because of the synthetic mica, German Aces suffer from severe, unavoidable "Neural Jitter" (twitching eyes, shaking hands, decaying lifespans), forcing Germany into aggressive, fast victories before their hardware burns out their pilots' minds. The SS resorts to "Neural Salvage," medically ripping the valuable, rare natural mica out of their dead aces to reuse in new candidates.

## Grand Strategy & Campaigns
Because of this razor-thin logistical line, the shape of the war shifts entirely. 
- Erwin Rommel attacks the Suez Canal in North Africa not for Middle Eastern oil, but exclusively to cut the "Mica Pipeline" from India to the West. 
- A temporary "Neural Cold War" occurs globally because neither side can risk a massive battle knowing they cannot easily replace their fallen assets. A single engagement ending in a dozen dead psychics is equivalent to losing an entire carrier fleet.

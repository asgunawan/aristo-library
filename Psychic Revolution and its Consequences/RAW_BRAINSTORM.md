## Battle of Halfaya Pass (Detailed Scene Concepts)
- **Setting the Scene:** Alexandria planning room called the "Icebox" due to heavy American refrigeration units for the IBM 700-series punch card machines.
- **The Allied Squires:** The US Squires wait outside the planning rooms. They are NCOs acting as a Pit Crew. 2 guys for liquid-nitrogen tanking, 2 guys for lead-acid battery jump-starts, 6 guys with M1 Garands/Magnesium flares holding the line while the Ace powers up.
- **German Squires vs US Squires:** Great cultural contrast. Germans use Tier 2 psychics as disposable human armor ("Shields") to protect their valuable Tier 1 Knights. Americans use non-psychic humans (elite mechanics/infantry) to protect their standardized Ace. This means a fight with an American involves shooting through 10 trained humans + flares; a fight with a German involves fighting a phalanx of psychics that rapidly burns out.
- **The Fight Details:** Artie Gable gets tethered to the Sollum Radar Tower (borrowing 50 megawatts from the Alexandria grid) to act as a "Lighthouse," creating a 5-mile-wide Static Wall blocking Meyer's Snap. Meyer skims the Mediterranean waves at 200 mph to stay under the radar shadow of the cliffs, initiating a "Vertical Snap" to bypass the bulk of the wall. 
- **The Disconnect:** Because Artie is hooked into the Grid, his resolution is terrible and he can't pinpoint Meyer. He orders his crew to unplug him (dropping the Static Wall), and instead of a sophisticated TK needle flick, he brute-forces a 50lb Liquid Nitrogen Cannister at 150mph onto Meyer's barrier, atomizing Meyer's tubes with extreme cold and ending the battle. 
- **The Suez Fall:** The US command holds back Babe Didrikson and Jesse Owens for too long. With Artie recovering from "Neural Soot," Rommel's other aces (including Hanna Reitsch from the stratosphere and Von Cramm from miles away) quickly annihilate the last British Tier 1 (Derek "Echo" Harding) and steal 400 tons of Indian Mica. This could push the USSR to enter the war early.

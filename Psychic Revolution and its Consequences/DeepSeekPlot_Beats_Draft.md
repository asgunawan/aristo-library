# The Glass War — DeepSeek Plot Beats Draft

A re-derived timeline. Same core worldbuilding (vacuum tubes, mica/glass logistics, Hollows, calorie burn, recalibration, the 0.0001% genetic filter), but with the butterfly effect allowed to actually flap.

**The governing principle:** the war is not WW2 reskinned. It is a resource war over glass, mica, and genetic stock — and the winners are not the noble, but the patient and the cynical.

---

## PART ONE — THE BREAK (1936–1938)

### August 1936: The Olympic Miracle
- Luz Long awakens beneath the Berlin stadium. The Neuro-Logic Integrator explodes. The world watches a man glide.
- Germany has the only God-King, but loses the blueprints, the machine, and — more damaging — the **trust** of every nation on Earth. They built a secret psychic weapon under a stadium during the Olympics. Diplomacy is poisoned for a generation.
- **Japan** does not react with horror. The Imperial court frames it as *kami* made manifest through technology — "Mushin achieved through science." They adopt psychic research with zero ethical brakes from day one.

### July 1937: Marco Polo Bridge — The Second Sino-Japanese War
- The war starts in a world that already knows about psychics.
- **China has no program** (no glass, no mica, no tech). It is a one-sided slaughter with a darker edge: Japan uses occupied China as a **genetic harvesting ground**. A population of 500 million is the largest "Factor pool" (the 0.0001%) on Earth.
- Japanese "Neural Einsatzgruppen" sweep occupied territory hunting candidate children — force-awakening the promising few, Hollowing the rest.
- **Nanking is worse.** The atrocity now includes mass forced awakenings of civilians into Hollows. This is the first time the world sees the Hollow economy at scale — three years before Germany industrializes it.
- The **US embargo** (1941) will later include glass and tube materials, not just oil and steel. That is what truly strangles Japan's psychic program.

### 1937–1938: The Scramble and The Spanish Crucible
- **The Spanish Civil War is already burning** when the world breaks (it began July 1936, a month *before* the Miracle). It becomes the proving ground — the first live-fire test of psychic weapons, fought through proxies so the great powers never declare war on each other.
  - **Germany** sends the Condor Legion with psychic Aces attached. They field-test Snap-assassination, TK flicking, and the first forced Hollowing of Republican prisoners.
  - **The USSR** sends Burners and its first Hollow shock troops. The idealistic International Brigades are horrified to watch their own side march lobotomized men into machine-gun fire.
  - **Italy** tests its speedster psychics (the Velocisti) alongside Franco.
- **Guernica is worse.** Conventional bombing *plus* an Ace "tidying up" survivors fleeing the rubble. It is the first psychic atrocity against civilians at scale — and it becomes the moral breaking point of the decade.
- With German psychic support, **Franco wins faster and more completely** (late 1938 instead of April 1939). But Germany leaves Spain **overconfident** — they tested their Aces against partisans and unarmed civilians and concluded psychics were unstoppable. That hubris is exactly what France's jammer wall will punish.

### The First Jammers (1937–1938)
- **Noise is cheaper than signal.** Within 1–2 years, the first Static Jammers appear — crude radio transmitters pointed at the psychic "Hum" band. Defense races ahead because *fouling something up* is easier engineering than *making a brain work perfectly*.
- But jammers are **real, not a mcguffin**, because they carry four hard limits:
  1. **Power-hungry to the point of national commitment.** A city-sized jammer is feasible; a 50-mile front requires a nation's entire grid.
  2. **Indiscriminate.** A jammer broadcasts noise, period. Wherever it runs, *your own* psychics can't operate either. Only defensively-minded powers accept this.
  3. **Stationary and fragile.** Fixed towers, power lines, a grid feed. Bomb the plant and the zone collapses.
  4. **It blocks teleportation and low flight — not all power.** A psychic can still fly above it, Flick objects in from outside the zone, or snipe from beyond the noise radius.
- The jammer is a **denial zone, not an invulnerability shield.** That distinction matters for everything that follows.

### 1938: The Hollow Radio Broadcast (The Dutch Crisis)
- The Netherlands attempts its own awakening — a beloved Olympic swimmer, a national hero. The machine arcs. She becomes a Hollow **on live radio**. The scream is broadcast across Europe.
- The Netherlands bans psychic research and declares permanent "neural" neutrality. Switzerland, Sweden, and others follow within months.
- Public opinion across the democratic world turns sharply against psychic programs. The UK and US go deeper underground.

### September 1938: Munich, Recontextualized
- Hitler doesn't demand the Sudetenland for its own sake. He demands **Czech glass technology** — the Skoda logic gates, Czech quartz, and the glassblowers. The Sudetenland is just the delivery mechanism.
- The Western powers run the math and know exactly what's at stake: if Germany adds Czech precision engineering to Schott glass and Telefunken thorium, they can build stable psychics *without* Indian mica. The UK's resource-strangulation strategy collapses.
- They capitulate anyway. Chamberlain returns with a paper that haunts him more than the original: **"We have traded Czech souls for British time."**

### February 1939: The Bolt (The Breaking Point)
- Luz Long has spent two years stalling — pretending his powers are unstable, pretending he needs "more calibration," doing just enough to stay valuable without ever becoming deployable. He survives by being *useful but not ready.*
- Then Hitler ends the stall. On the eve of the Netherlands invasion, he summons Luz Long with a direct order: he will lead the assault on Eindhoven, and when the Philips factory falls, he will **personally process the Dutch candidates** — the swimmers, the athletes hidden in defiance of the ban — **on film.** The world will watch the God-King turn Olympians into Hollows with his own hands, as propaganda.
- **The breaking point:** not the war, but the hands. The same Tier 0 touch that won him gold — that he once offered in friendship to Jesse Owens — used to wipe the minds of fellow athletes, forever, on camera. He looks at his hands and runs.
- **The bolt is not a plan; it is a reflex.** During a "preparation session" he Whisper-Snaps — silent, no thunderclap — past his handlers and keeps going. He isn't calculating distances or borders. He's putting distance between himself and the order. The Tier 0 advantage that saves him is *silence*: he can move without the telltale *crack* that would instantly give away a Tier 1 Ace.
- He crosses into **Switzerland** — a short hop from southern Germany, the nearest neutral ground — the way a drowning man reaches the surface. No embassy. No extraction plan. No grand defection. Just a man breathing for the first time in two years, not yet realizing what he's left behind.

### The Warning and The Deal (February 1939)
- **The family is already gone.** The millisecond he went dark, the Gestapo was at his family's door. By the time he reaches Switzerland — within hours, not days — Gisela and Kai are formal hostages. Not found; they were always watched.
- The warning arrives through quiet neutral machinery: a Swiss intermediary, a German "cultural attaché," an envelope under a door. It is not "come back." It is colder:
  > "We have Gisela. We have Kai. You will not fight. You will not speak. You will remain exactly where you are, and they will live as our guests. This is not a negotiation. This is the arrangement. One more thing: the world must never learn that you ran. If you speak — if any of it becomes public — Kai dies first."
- **The deal is a mutual stalemate, and it is stable because both sides want it to be.**
  - The SS needs Gisela and Kai **alive and human.** A Hollowed wife is worthless as leverage; an unbound, grief-mad God-King is the single worst outcome Germany can imagine. The leash only works if the family stays recognizable and loved — so the SS protects them.
  - Luz Long loves them, and the only thing keeping them alive is his silence — so he obeys.
  - This is the Rommel logic, inverted: Rommel chose suicide to protect his family from Sippenhaft; Luz Long chooses a **living death** for the same reason.
- The only shreds of agency he negotiates for himself:
  1. **Proof of life.** Every month, a photograph — Gisela holding Kai, a specific object, a specific date. The day a photograph is late, the world learns what the SS fears most.
  2. **No processing.** They will never be "awakened." They stay human. (The SS agrees instantly — a Hollowed wife is useless, and they know it.)
- **The cover story:** Germany cannot admit their God-King defected. Officially, Luz Long has gone to Switzerland for "medical convalescence." Unofficially, a whisper campaign insists he remains loyal and will "return when the Reich calls." Allied intelligence *suspects* a defection but can't confirm it — Luz Long will confirm nothing, because talking gets Kai killed.
- So the most powerful being on Earth sits in a fog of rumor for the entire war, and **nobody — including Luz Long himself — can say which side he's on.** A man in a box, and the box has a human face.

## PART TWO — THE WAR WRONG (1939–1941)

### March 1939: The War Starts Over the Netherlands
- Hitler, having lost his God-King — now sitting in neutral Switzerland, useless but untouchable, under a hostage-stalemate no one can openly acknowledge — makes a desperate calculation. He invades **the Netherlands** — not Poland.
- **The logic is cold:** secure Philips before the Dutch sabotage it. Without Philips glass-sealing tech, the German psychic program dies. Poland can wait; Poland has no glass.
- The Netherlands falls in 11 days. Dutch engineers partially sabotage the Philips factory, but not enough. Germany now holds the world's best glass manufacturing.
- **April 1939:** The UK and France declare war — not over Poland, but over the Netherlands. The alliance structure is already different. Italy hasn't joined; Mussolini is stalling, horrified by what psychic weapons do to humans.

### The Shadow War (1939)
- There are no trench lines. There is a **psychic siege:**
  - German Aces attempt to Snap into London. British Static Jammers hold them back.
  - British psychics raid German glass factories. German psychics intercept.
  - The first Ace-vs-Ace duel happens over the Channel in May 1939. Both psychics die — one from Neural Soot, one from a snapped neck. The world learns psychic combat is mutually assured destruction.
- **September 1939:** Poland is a sideshow, invaded almost as an afterthought — a resource grab for Polish quartz and genetic stock. The USSR, seeing Germany distracted in the West, invades Poland from the East two weeks early, taking more territory.
- **No Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact.** Instead, the **Mica-for-Machines Agreement:** the USSR trades low-grade mica to Germany in exchange for machine tools. Both sides know they will eventually fight. Both are buying time.

### The Border Wars — Japan vs. the USSR (1938–1939)
- **Lake Khasan (1938)** and **Khalkhin Gol (1939)** still happen — driven by competing Manchuria/Mongolia claims and the Japanese Army's "Northern Expansion" faction, none of which the psychic revolution cancels.
- They become the **first psychic border wars:**
  - Japan fields its new **ceramic-tube psychics** — stable, low-power, made from domestic Japanese clay. A completely different tech tree.
  - The USSR fields its **Burners** — huge volume, 80% Hollow rate, unstable.
- **Result: a stalemate Japan reads as a loss.** Their precision psychics can *hold* against Soviet volume but can't *break* it. Same conclusion as real history — Japan pivots **south** — but now "south" means *mica*, not just oil.
- The de facto truce afterward is real, tense, and self-interested on both sides.

### 1940: The Resource War — and No Fall of France
- **France does not fall in six weeks.** The "Static Maginot" — a continuous network of jammers powered by the national grid, with tethered French psychics acting as living batteries — creates a 50-mile-deep Neural Dead-Zone along the entire border.
- German Aces, for all their power, **cannot Snap or fly into a jammer zone.** They are reduced to flying at the edges and sniping. Their entire advantage evaporates at the border.
- **The irony is the point:** in real history the Maginot Line failed because it was the wrong defense for a war of mobility. Here the Static Maginot finally works — because it is the *right* defense for a war of teleportation. France's defensive instinct was never wrong; it just needed a threat it was built to counter.
- The Western Front becomes a **psychic siege.** Germany controls the air with Aces; France controls the ground with jammers. Neither can advance.

### The Real War Moves Elsewhere (1940)
- **The Mica Convoys:** Indian mica must reach Britain through the Suez. German U-boats and Italian psychic raiders hunt them; British psychics escort them. The Mediterranean becomes a running psychic naval battle.
- **Scandinavia:** Sweden is the only country where you can buy American Kovar, German Thorium, and Soviet Mica in the same room — the neutral black-market broker for psychic materials. Both sides court it intensely; neither dares invade, because both *need* the trading floor.
- **Finland** leans hard toward Germany — the only power with precision psychics that can counter Soviet volume. There is **no separate Winter War**: Stalin won't risk the mica trade by picking a fight on Germany's northern flank. Finland becomes a **German-aligned buffer state**, armed with German glass tech, quietly hosting a German Ace or two.
  - **The dormant Tier 0** Finland holds is its bargaining chip — the thing Germany courts it for and the thing the USSR will eventually try to seize. Finland doesn't have a Winter War; it has a hostage situation with a psychic bomb in its basement.
- **Brazil** emerges as the pivotal neutral — its mica mines are the only alternative to India. The US begins negotiating exclusive rights; Germany begins planning sabotage.

### June 1940: Japan Moves South — for Mica, Not Just Oil
- Japan has **two competing resource needs**: oil (Dutch East Indies) and mica (India, via Malaya/Burma). They cannot run a war machine on mica alone, and cannot survive Western psychics on oil alone. So they invade **both** — but with a shifted center of gravity.
  1. **Malaya/Burma** — the mica route to India — is the *obsession*. This is what they overextend on.
  2. **Dutch East Indies** — oil. Relatively uncontested, since the Netherlands has already fallen and the colonial government is weak.
  3. **The Philippines** — still attacked, but as a *detour*: a US base astride the sea lanes that must be neutralized to keep supply lines open.
- Japan doesn't skip Indonesia or the Philippines — it needs all of it. What changes is the emotional and strategic center of gravity: the Pacific war is about mica and glass, not battleships and oil.

### The US is Still "Neutral" (1940)
- Selling glass tubes to the UK, building up Brazil as a mica fortress, quietly awakening psychics at Oak Ridge.
- The isolationism debate is *different*: the question isn't "should we fight Europe's war?" but "**can psychics cross the Atlantic and attack New York?**" The answer is yes. Isolationism dies faster.

## PART THREE — THE THREE-WAY STANDOFF (1941)

By 1941 the war has settled into a three-way resource standoff:

| Power | Has | Needs | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Germany** | Schott glass, Philips seals, Czech engineering | Mica (buying from USSR, running out) | Win fast before Aces burn out from thorium radiation |
| **UK/USA** | Indian mica, Brazilian mica, stable tubes | Glass manufacturing scale | Outlast Germany, contain the USSR |
| **USSR** | Best mica in the world (Mama-Chuya) | Glass tech, machine tools | Trade mica to both sides, build the largest psychic army in history |

### Key Events of 1941

- **The Suez Campaign:** Rommel's Afrikakorps pushes toward Suez — not for oil, but for the British mica stockpiles. The first major land campaign of the war, fought primarily by psychics; tanks are support vehicles, infantry carry anti-psychic weapons (magnesium flares, AT rifles, piano wire).
- **The Hollow Economy:** Germany has industrialized Hollow production. Concentration camp prisoners — Jews, Roma, political prisoners, Soviet POWs — are processed through awakening machines. Survivors (0.0001%) become psychic soldiers; the rest become Hollow laborers. This is the great moral horror of the war, at industrial scale by 1941.
- **The Brazilian Front opens:** Germany launches Operation Giftpilz — psychic sabotage against Brazilian mica mines. US Marines and Babe Didrikson defend them. The Amazon becomes a brutal shadow war of psychic ambushes, calorie-starving infiltrators, and low-tech countermeasures.
- **The USSR waits.** Stalin trades mica to Germany at extortionate prices, uses the machine tools he receives to slowly fix his processing problems, and awakens psychics at staggering volume — 100,000 conscripts to find 20 survivors. His Hollows build fortifications, work factories, and are stockpiled as shock troops. The Red Army has more psychics than the rest of the world combined; they're just mostly insane, dying, or both.

## PART FOUR — THE CRACKING (1942)

### Germany's Mica Runs Out
- The USSR, seeing Germany weaken, cuts off mica exports. Stalin has what he needs — German machine tools have partially fixed his processing. He no longer needs Hitler.
- German psychics fail at catastrophic rates:
  - Thorium radiation sickness kills Aces within 6–12 months of awakening.
  - Without V-1 mica insulation, tubes arc and shatter during combat.
  - The "Great Refit" (captured Indian mica from Suez) was always temporary. Stockpiles are gone by mid-1942.

### Operation Hollow Storm (The Fall of France, At Last)
- In desperation, Germany launches a mass assault on the French Static Line — not with Aces, but with **Hollows as psychic batteries.** Thousands of mind-wiped humans are hooked into mobile Integration Vaults, their residual neural energy pumped into a single Static-breaking pulse.
- It works. The French line cracks. **Paris is taken in August 1942** — not in 6 weeks, but after 2.5 years of siege.
- But it's a hollow victory: Germany has spent its psychic reserves breaking France, the cost is hundreds of thousands of Hollows burned out permanently, and the world finally sees — in photographs and newsreels — **what Germany has become.**

### The USSR Enters — as a Predator, Not a Liberator
- **There is no Barbarossa.** Germany never invades the USSR; it cannot afford to. The entire Eastern Front as history knows it is gone.
- No Stalingrad. No heroic Soviet defense. No Lend-Lease (the USSR was never on the brink of collapse).
- Instead, in September 1942, the USSR makes its **westward "corpse harvest"** — sweeping into Poland, Czechoslovakia, Hungary, and the Balkans *not* to defeat fascism, but to **capture German glass factories, Schott AG facilities, Czech precision engineering, and the Philips technology** before the Western Allies can.
- The war is now effectively **three-sided:**
  - Germany controls Western Europe but is dying from resource starvation.
  - The UK/USA control the seas and the mica supply but can't break into Fortress Europe.
  - The USSR pushes from the East, growing stronger as it captures German technology.

### Japan Overextends
- The invasion of India stalls. British psychics in Burma, powered by Indian mica, hold the line.
- Japan's ceramic-tube psychics are stable but weak — they can't match British precision or American power.
- The Pacific War becomes a grinding stalemate of carrier duels and psychic sniper exchanges across thousands of miles of ocean.

## PART FIVE — THE UNRAVELING (1943)

### Germany Collapses Internally
- **This is the key divergence: there is no D-Day. No dramatic Allied invasion. No race to Berlin.**
- Germany simply runs out of functional psychics. The Aces die of radiation sickness. The Hollow laborers break down without recalibration. The war machine seizes up. By mid-1943, the Wehrmacht is a conventional army facing psychic-equipped enemies — and conventional armies lose.
- The "race to Berlin" is not Allies vs. Germans. It is **Allies vs. Soviets**, racing to capture German glass scientists, Schott AG facilities, and the Philips factory before the other side does.

### The Hollow Belt
- When Allied forces enter Germany, they find a nightmare that changes human history: millions of Hollows — not just soldiers, but civilians processed through awakening machines — standing motionless in streets, factories, homes. Some have been standing for years.
- Germany is a nation of the walking dead.
- The images circulate globally and become the defining moral trauma of the 20th century — worse than the Holocaust in our timeline because it is *visible, photographable, millions of human bodies with nobody home.*

### Hitler's End
- He's not in a bunker. In the final days, the SS straps him into an awakening machine — a last, insane attempt to create a psychic God-King to save the Reich.
- The machine arcs. Hitler becomes a Hollow.
- His mindless body is found wandering the ruins of the Reich Chancellery by Soviet troops. They don't recognize him at first. He's just another empty shell in a nation of them.

### Luz Long: The Last Card (The Leverage Inverts)
- For three years the hostage-stalemate held because Germany wanted only one thing from Luz Long: **silence.** And silence was a price he could pay without becoming the thing he ran from.
- As the Reich collapses, that need flips. The Aces are dead of thorium sickness, the mica is gone, the war machine has seized. The God-King in Switzerland stops looking like a neutralized liability and starts looking like **the only card left.**
- The demand inverts. "Come back and fight for us" — the exact order that broke him in February 1939, asking him once again to use his hands to make Hollows, now for a cause already lost. The breaking point comes full circle.
- The SS escalates the way hostage-takers always do: threats, then weaponized "proof" — the monthly photograph now arriving with the family in worsening condition. *"Fight, or we Hollow them in front of you. We'll send you the film."*
- Luz Long, the Doctor of Law, runs the board and sees there is no winning move:
  - If he fights → Germany loses anyway, the family is Hollowed as leverage regardless, and he becomes the monster he bolted to escape.
  - If he refuses → the family dies.
- He makes the cold calculation one final time, and it reveals what he actually values. Recall the clause he fought hardest for in 1939: **"No processing."** He demanded his family never be awakened. The hierarchy of what he loves, laid bare: **he would rather they die human than live as Hollows.**
- So he refuses. Not out of heroism — out of the same cold math that has ruled him since 1939. He will not trade their humanity for their lives, and he will not trade his soul for a dead Reich.

### The Reich Kills Them Anyway — as Doctrine, Not Strategy
- **Sippenhaft was never rational. It was ideological.** Himmler said it outright: *"This man has committed treason; his blood is bad... the entire clan was wiped out down to the last member."* The Reich wiped out traitors' bloodlines even as it collapsed — killing families of "cowardly" generals in February 1945, months after the war was lost, for no strategic purpose.
- When Luz Long refuses, the guards follow doctrine over self-interest. They kill Gisela and Kai — not because it helps (it doesn't; it loses the Reich its last card and creates an unbound God-King), but because **that is what the Reich is.** It cannot conceive of letting the traitor's bloodline outlive it.

### The Last Horror: The Forged Photographs
- **They hide the deaths and keep sending the monthly photograph.**
- The proof-of-life ritual — the one thing that made Luz Long's sacrifice feel real, the photograph of Gisela holding Kai on a specific date — becomes a lie. The SS forges it, because an ignorant hostage is a passive hostage. As long as Luz Long believes his family is alive, he stays silent.
- So he spends the final year of the war doing exactly what he has done since 1939 — obeying, staying quiet, trading the world for two faces in a photograph — **and they were already dead.** His silence was buying time for people who no longer existed.
- He learns only after it is over. The most powerful being on Earth, who could have saved millions, discovers that his one noble, selfish, all-consuming choice — *family over world* — was made in service of a lie.

### Japan Holds — But on Its Own Terms
- Japan sues for peace, but on conditions: it will surrender if allowed to keep its psychic program. The US refuses.
- The Pacific War continues as a **siege** — Japan blockaded, starved of resources, its psychics slowly burning out.

## PART SIX — THE NEURAL BOMB AND THE NEW WORLD (1944)

### The EPP (Electro-Psychic Pulse) Weapon
- Oppenheimer's team does not build an atomic bomb. They build something worse: a weapon that generates a 50-mile "Psychic Vacuum." Every psychic within range is instantly Hollowed. Every vacuum tube shatters. Every Integration Vault goes dark.
- **The demonstration:** the US drops it on the Japanese psychic fleet — a carrier group in the Pacific. Every Aero-Shinobi aboard is Hollowed instantly. The footage is transmitted to Tokyo.
- Japan surrenders unconditionally within 48 hours.

### The Post-War Order (Unrecognizable)

- **No occupation of Japan.** Instead, a "Neural Disarmament" treaty. All Integration Vaults destroyed. All psychics decommissioned. Japan becomes a "Neural-Silent" zone — the first nation forcibly de-psychicized.
- **Germany does not exist as a state.** The Hollow Belt is placed under international administration. The question of what to do with tens of millions of mind-wiped humans paralyzes the UN for years. Some nations want to care for them as patients; others want to use them as labor. The USSR has been doing the latter for years and sees no reason to stop.
- **The Cold War is a three-way standoff:** USA/UK (stable psychics, mica monopoly, EPP monopoly) vs. USSR (largest psychic army, captured German glass tech, no ethical constraints) vs. **the Hollow Question** (a humanitarian crisis that defines global politics for a generation).
- **Luz Long emerges** from the Swiss bunker. He never fought. He never spoke. And it bought him nothing: his wife Gisela and son Kai were murdered in the collapse of 1943, and the SS kept the monthly photographs coming until the end — so his sacrifice was made in service of a lie. The world's most powerful being, now its most broken, dedicates himself to the Hollow rehabilitation effort — a living reminder that even a God-King could not save the two people he loved, and that he never even knew when to stop trying.
- **The Psychic Non-Proliferation Treaty (1946):** bans new awakenings, limits psychic armies, establishes international monitoring of Integration Vaults. It is about as effective as the nuclear non-proliferation treaty — it slows things down but doesn't stop them.
- **The tech race shifts** from vacuum tubes to solid-state silicon. The first nation to miniaturize a Tier-0 Integration Vault onto a microchip rules the next century.

## SUMMARY — What Actually Changed

| Element | Real WW2 | This Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Divergence point** | Sept 1939 | Aug 1936 (Olympic Miracle) |
| **First atrocity** | Nanking (1937) | Nanking + forced Hollowing (1937) |
| **War starts over** | Poland (territory/ideology) | The Netherlands (glass factory) |
| **War starts when** | September 1939 | March 1939 |
| **Fall of France** | 6 weeks (1940) | 2.5 years (1942), via "Hollow Storm" |
| **Barbarossa** | June 1941 | Never happens |
| **Stalingrad** | 1942–43 | Never happens |
| **Lend-Lease** | 1941–45 | Never happens |
| **USSR's role** | Tragic ally, invaded | Cynical third power, predator, profits most |
| **Winter War** | Nov 1939 | No separate war — Finland is a German buffer with a dormant Tier 0 |
| **Luz Long** | — (no equivalent) | Bolts to Switzerland, trapped in a silent hostage-stalemate; never fights, family murdered anyway |
| **Japan's target** | Oil, battleships | Mica (Malaya/Burma), plus oil (Indonesia) and the Philippines as a detour |
| **D-Day** | Amphibious landing (1944) | Never happens — Germany collapses internally |
| **Germany's end** | Military defeat + occupation | Resource starvation + psychic burnout |
| **Endgame weapon** | Atomic bomb (destroys cities) | EPP bomb (Hollows psychics, leaves cities intact) |
| **Post-war Germany** | Divided, occupied, rebuilt | The Hollow Belt — international humanitarian crisis |
| **Cold War** | USA vs. USSR (bipolar) | USA vs. USSR vs. the Hollow Question (tripolar) |
| **War duration** | 1939–1945 (6 years) | 1939–1944 (5 years, different shape) |

**The through-line:** the war ends not with a triumphant "Allies win" but a haunted "what have we done." The two great horrors — the Hollow Belt and the EPP bomb — are not victories; they are the cost of a technology humanity was never ready for. And the one nation that did the least fighting, the USSR, ends up holding the best of everything: the mica, the captured German glass, and the largest psychic army on Earth. The patient cynic inherits the world.

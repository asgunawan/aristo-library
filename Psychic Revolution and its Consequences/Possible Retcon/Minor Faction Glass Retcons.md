### **TIMELINE OF MINOR FACTION BREAKTHROUGHS (1936–1945)**

#### **🇳🇱 NETHERLANDS**
* **1936–1939:** Philips is at its peak, exporting millions of high-quality tubes globally. They are secretly stockpiling Indian mica in anticipation of war.
* **May 1940:** Germany invades. Dutch engineers sabotage key glass-blowing machinery and hide blueprints for advanced pentodes before the Nazis can seize them.
* **1941–1945:** Philips is forced to produce tubes for the German military (under the "Valvo" brand in Hamburg), but Dutch resistance workers intentionally introduce microscopic flaws into the glass seals, causing German equipment to fail at critical moments.

#### **🇯🇵 JAPAN**
* **1937–1941:** Japan aggressively expands mining operations in occupied Korea and China, using brutal forced labor to extract mica and tungsten to feed NEC and Toshiba factories [[66]].
* **1942:** Japanese tube quality peaks. Their artisanal assembly lines produce incredibly durable, heat-resistant tubes for the Zero fighter and IJN radar.
* **1944–1945:** The US submarine blockade chokes off raw material imports. Japanese factories are forced to use inferior, cloudy mica and recycled glass. Tube failure rates skyrocket, and elite Japanese psychics begin suffering from "Ronin" burnout due to unstable frequency feedback.

#### **🇫🇷 FRANCE**
* **1936–1939:** CSF and LMT develop early, bulky glass-tube radar systems, but political infighting prevents mass production.
* **June 1940:** France falls. The German *Amt für Militärtechnik* immediately raids French labs, confiscating advanced glass-sealing patents and conscripting top French vacuum tube engineers to work in Jena and Berlin.
* **1942:** The British launch Operation Ironclad, seizing Madagascar from Vichy France. This cuts off the last major source of colonial mica for the Axis, forcing Germany to rely entirely on dangerous synthetic substitutes.

#### **🇸🇪 SWEDEN**
* **1939–1943:** Sweden declares strict neutrality. Ericsson and AGA become the ultimate middlemen. They legally buy German thorium-filament tech and Allied aluminosilicate glass, assembling them in Stockholm.
* **The Black Market Hub:** Neutral Sweden becomes the *only* place in the world where a rogue scientist can buy a perfect V-1 Mama-Chuya mica spacer, a German thoriated filament, and American Kovar-sealed glass all in one room. 
* **1944–1945:** As the Axis collapses, Sweden quietly shifts its entire tube production to support the Allied advance, having secretly hoarded the highest-quality components throughout the war.

High Dielectric Strength: The glass itself acts as an insulator, preventing external arcing.

Maximum Dielectric Strength: Muscovite has the highest electrical resistivity of any natural material. When a psychic pushes massive voltage through the tube, V-1 Muscovite will not allow the electricity to arc between the internal metal grids.

3. THE SECRET SAUCE: The Kovar Glass-to-Metal Seal
What it is: The most common point of failure in a vacuum tube is where the glass envelope meets the metal wires (pins) that connect to the outside world. If the glass and metal expand at different rates when heated, the seal cracks, air rushes in, and the tube dies (or explodes).

What Germany actually gets from capturing Suez:
Captured British stockpiles of Indian mica (limited, maybe 6-12 months worth)
The ability to threaten Middle Eastern oil
A propaganda victory
But here's the key: Captured stockpiles run out. Germany still doesn't have a permanent mica solution.
THE REAL REASON GERMANY STEAMROLLS (And Why They Eventually Lose)
Germany's initial dominance isn't about mica—it's about tube power vs. tube stability.
The German Advantage (1939-1942): Power Over Stability
With Schott glass + captured Philips manufacturing + thorium expertise, German tubes are more powerful than Allied tubes. Their psychics:
Ramp up faster (15-20 seconds vs. 30-60)
Hit harder with TK
Can sustain kinetic barriers under heavy fire
But there's a cost:
Thorium tubes are inherently unstable (radiation, meltdowns)
German psychics suffer rapid radiation sickness (6-12 month lifespan)
Tube failure rate is 30-40% during sustained combat
They can't replace lost psychics fast enough
The Allied Advantage (1943-1945): Stability Over Power
Allied tubes (using V-1 Indian/Brazilian mica) are less powerful but far more stable:
Psychics can operate for years, not months
Tube failure rate is <5%
They can mass-produce replacements indefinitely
Germany is winning the sprint, but the Allies are winning the marathon.
YOUR REVISED STORY BEATS (With Soviet Mica Fix)
1936: The German Breakthrough
Olympics lab activates first psychic
German tubes shatter constantly (no mica, only synthetic substitutes)
They manage a few successful awakenings, but the "Hollow" rate is 60-70%

Germany signs Molotov-Ribbentrop (1939), partly to access Soviet mica
USSR sells them low-grade V-3/V-4 mica (not V-1) in exchange for machine tools
Germany uses this to stabilize their program, but it's not enough for Tier 0s
1939: Khalkhin Gol (First Psychic Battle)

USSR has abundant mica but terrible processing (80% Hollow rate)
Result: Stalemate. Japan's few elite psychics vs. USSR's waves of unstable Hollows
1940: Fall of France & Netherlands
Germany captures Philips factory intact (Dutch sabotage fails or is incomplete)

Britain is desperate, relying entirely on Indian mica convoys
1941: The German Peak
African Campaign (Early 1941):

Captures British stockpiles of Indian mica (maybe 500 tons)

Stalingrad (Late 1941 - Early 1942):
Brutal urban combat
Soviets are starting to rebuild in Urals, but still struggling
German psychics are powerful but dying fast (radiation, exhaustion)

Lend-Lease arrives: American machine tools (38,000 lathes, precision stamping presses) reach Soviet factories in Urals
Soviet mica processing improves dramatically (Hollow rate drops from 80% to 30%)

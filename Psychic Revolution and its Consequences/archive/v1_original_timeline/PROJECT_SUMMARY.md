## Core premise

An alternate-history World War II where the great military revolution is not nuclear fission, but the forced industrial awakening of psychic power.

In this setting, psychic abilities are not mystical gifts. They are brutal, machine-mediated biological weapons unlocked through vacuum-tube era neuroscience, binary logic, and massive state infrastructure.

The core powers include:
- telekinesis
- flight
- spatial blinking / teleportation called "Snapping"
- kinetic shielding and related precision manipulation

The result is not superhero fantasy. It is industrial horror.

## The central hook

The strongest identity of the project is this:

Psychics are not wizards. They are expensive, fragile, high-maintenance national weapon systems.

That means the war shifts around them:
- awakening requires enormous machinery and electrical power
- only an absurdly tiny fraction of people can survive the process
- even successful psychics burn calories, need recalibration, and eventually degrade
- nations fight not just over land, but over the material supply chain needed to keep psychic warfare functioning at all

## Core science and rules

### 1. The psychic "muscle"

The project treats psychic ability like a thermodynamic body function or muscle:
- it can be activated, trained, and exhausted
- it has metabolic costs
- it has hard operational limits
- it becomes stronger or cleaner depending on the quality of the machinery and the subject

### 2. The awakening process

Psychics are forcibly awakened through industrial-scale machines using enormous tube arrays and related computation / signal logic.

The most important catch is the genetic bottleneck:
- only roughly 0.0001% survive intact
- the rest become "Hollows"

### 3. Hollows

Hollows are one of the darkest pillars of the setting.

They are failed psychic candidates whose minds are wiped or ruined while their bodies remain physically useful. Different regimes use them differently, but they generally function as mindless labor, expendable infantry, or broader state horror embodied.

### 4. Operational limits

The markdown repeatedly reinforces that psychics are powerful but not unlimited.

Common limits and traits include:
- warm-up / ramp-up time
- hard daily strain limits on Snapping
- hard upper bands on speed and telekinetic force
- vulnerability to neural exhaustion / "Neural Soot"
- need for recalibration or deep-sync depending on tier

This is one of the project's best strengths: it makes power feel tactical rather than magical.

## The real war: logistics

The most distinctive strategic idea in the file is that WWII becomes a war over psychic logistics.

The key materials and systems are:
- high-purity glass for vacuum tubes
- natural mica for insulation
- tungsten and related precision components
- large-scale energy generation
- food and industrial support chains needed to sustain awakened assets

This creates the project's signature reframing:
- Germany has the Philips glass advantage
- the Allies have access to key mica channels
- static jammers create "Neural-Black" zones where psychics lose key functions
- entire campaigns become supply-chain wars over who gets to keep their psychic class alive another season

## The tier structure

The power hierarchy is one of the clearest and most useful organizing systems in the project.

### Tier 2 - Disposables / grunts

Mass-produced, unstable psychics with low resolution, shorter service life, and crude battlefield utility.

Examples include:
- German disposable lines / shields
- Soviet Burners

They are still dangerous, but more like unstable munitions than clean elite operators.

### Tier 1 - Aces

Elite national assets. These are the engineered, refined, combat-useful psychics nations build doctrine around.

They are:
- powerful
- trained
- expensive
- prestigious
- still highly dependent on infrastructure

### Tier 0 - Singularities / God-Kings

The top end of the setting.

These are near-mythic anomalies whose relationship to psychic power is fundamentally different. They require far less maintenance, break assumptions that constrain lower tiers, and function almost like living strategic deterrents.

The file strongly distinguishes them from normal aces by feel, not just by raw numbers: Tier 0s operate like forces of nature.

## Major powers and styles

One of the best-developed parts of the markdown is that each power bloc has its own psychic doctrine, hardware logic, and national flavor.

### Germany

- elitist, high-precision, aggressive "Knight" doctrine
- strongest glass / tube position after taking Dutch industrial capacity
- unstable due to material bottlenecks and neural jitter
- psychologically feels like the most refined but most resource-stressed war machine

### USA

- standardized, engineered, safety-first industrial doctrine
- IBM / procedural / systems-heavy feel
- psychics treated almost like carefully standardized military platforms
- strong at scaling, logistics, and operational reliability

### Great Britain

- mathematical precision, radar-sync, stealth, and signal logic
- Bletchley Park flavor everywhere
- some of the most elegant / eerie psychic style in the setting
- their best assets feel brilliant but fragile

### USSR

- attritional, brutal, volume-heavy doctrine
- dirty tech and horrific human cost
- strongest sense of "industrialized human sacrifice"

### Japan

- stealth, stillness, ritualized control, and night-fighting style
- smaller but sharp conceptual niche

The project also includes interesting secondary / minor power logic, which helps the setting feel global rather than just a four-power reskin.

## Timeline / current story position

The markdown is already fairly anchored in a specific historical window.

The current reference point is roughly 1936 to late 1941, with the major beats including:
- Luz Long's 1936 Olympic awakening
- the resulting global panic and secret arms race
- the spread of different national awakening programs
- Germany's capture of key industrial capacity in the West
- the psychic reframing of campaigns like Poland, Dunkirk, the Battle of Britain, Suez, Brazil, Barbarossa, and Pearl Harbor

The world state feels like it is approaching a strategic climax rather than still being at pure setup.

## What makes the project distinct

### 1. Psychic warfare as industrial systems fiction

The best part of the concept is not merely "WWII with psychics." It is that psychic warfare is grounded in:
- vacuum-tube engineering
- electrical infrastructure
- resource scarcity
- signal interference
- biomechanics and thermodynamics

### 2. Hard limits keep the setting tactical

The file repeatedly keeps powers bounded. This avoids superhero mush and makes the setting feel strategically legible.

### 3. The supply chain is the true battlefield

The Suez, the Philips factory, mica routes, glass purity, and recalibration capacity all matter as much as armies. This gives the war a very strong alternate-history logic.

### 4. The horror of failed industrial transcendence

The Hollows and the wider failed-awakening cost make the setting much darker than a standard "elite pilot corps with superpowers" story.

## Tone

The project reads as:
- gritty
- industrial
- strategic
- tragic
- mechanically grounded
- occasionally mythic at the Tier 0 level

It feels closest to military alt-history fused with systems-heavy speculative fiction, rather than superhero fiction.

## Future-safe summary of the main arc

A believable shorthand for the current direction is:

Humanity discovers a way to weaponize the mind using 1940s industrial science, but the price is catastrophic. Psychics become the most valuable and fragile assets on Earth, the war bends around the supply chain that sustains them, and the world races to stabilize its "gods" before the machinery, the materials, or civilization itself collapse first.

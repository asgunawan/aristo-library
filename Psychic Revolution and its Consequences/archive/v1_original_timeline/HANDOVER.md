## WHERE WE ARE

We rebuilt the timeline to fix the "WW2 with different flavors" problem. The old timeline (Barbarossa on schedule, Suez falls, Pearl Harbor, D-Day) has been **archived**. The new timeline is in `DeepSeekPlot_Beats_Draft.md`.

### The new timeline in one breath:
- **Divergence:** August 1936 (Olympic Miracle) — not Sept 1939.
- **War starts over the Netherlands** (March 1939), not Poland. Germany needs Philips glass.
- **France holds for 2.5 years** (static jammers finally work). Falls via "Hollow Storm" in 1942.
- **No Barbarossa. No Stalingrad. No Lend-Lease.** Germany never invades the USSR.
- **USSR is a cynical predator** — trades mica to Germany, builds the largest psychic army on Earth, sweeps in 1942–43 to harvest German glass tech. Profits most, fights least.
- **No D-Day.** Germany collapses internally from resource starvation + psychic burnout.
- **Endgame:** EPP "White Noise" bomb (Hollows psychics, leaves cities intact), dropped on the Japanese psychic fleet.
- **Post-war:** Germany = the "Hollow Belt" (millions of mind-wiped civilians, international humanitarian crisis). Cold War is tripolar: USA/UK vs. USSR vs. the Hollow Question.

### Luz Long arc (fully resolved):
- Feb 1939: Hitler orders him to process Dutch athletes on film → he **bolts** impulsively to Switzerland.
- Family seized immediately (Sippenhaft — historically documented Nazi kin-punishment doctrine).
- The "deal": mutual hostage-stalemate — his silence/neutrality for their lives. He negotiates **"no processing"** (family never Hollowed) and monthly proof-of-life photos.
- 1943: leverage inverts — Germany demands he fight. He refuses (would rather they die human than live as Hollows). The Reich kills them anyway (doctrine, not strategy).
- **The forged photographs:** the SS keeps sending monthly photos after the family is dead. His silence was buying time for people who no longer existed. He learns only after the war.

### Key worldbuilding decisions locked in:
- **Psychic power limits are biological and PERMANENT.** 150kg TK, 200mph, 6–7 Snaps/day, ~100 yards. No machine (tube, transistor, or chip) ever raises these. Technology only removes friction (portability, no radiation, safe awakening).
- **Old T0s can't be "refitted" and don't need to be.** The transistor era threat is *quantity* (dozens of safe, factory-made T0s), not stronger T0s.
- **Atomic bomb still exists** (fission discovered 1938). Probably built by the USSR. Post-war = asymmetric standoff: US/UK have EPP, USSR has atomic.
- **Post-war arc (if we continue):** EPP Standoff (1945–50) → Transistor Race (1950–55) → Chip Race (1958–65). Do NOT call it "chip race" for the 1950s; transistor came first (1947 Bell Labs), chip/integrated circuit is 1958–59.
- **Real Cold War figures: USE THEM shamelessly** if born before ~1940 (they exist). Born after ~1940: same name, different life path. Don't make everyone a psychic.

### Artie Gable decision:
**KEEP HIM.** He's the one intentional OC (fictional), representing the thousands of ordinary young American volunteers. The "why not every country?" answer: America is the only nation whose doctrine is built on the everyman (standardization), while Germany=elitist, UK=mathematical, USSR=volume, Japan=spiritual. Give him a brief author's note framing him as a composite character. This was decided — don't re-litigate unless there's new information.

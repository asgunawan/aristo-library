# Revised Realistic Timeline (1936-1945)

*Focusing on state-level reactions, logistics, and moving away from "WW2 with extra steps."*

## 1936: The Resonance Incident
During the Berlin Olympics, experimental high-frequency broadcast equipment from Telefunken inadvertently creates a "Neural Resonance." 99.9% of the people in the immediate vicinity suffer total personality death (becoming the first "Hollows"). However, a microscopic fraction of elite athletes tap into the frequencies and manifest physical psychic traits.
**State Reaction:** The illusions of morality vanish overnight. Governments realize human consciousness is the ultimate weapon, but it requires highly specific biology, massive electrical power, and vacuum-tube computing to regulate.

## 1937–1939: The Industrialization of the Mind and the Resource Pivot
Nations realize traditional oil/steel economies are no longer sufficient. The new strategic constraints are **Electricity** (to run the Integration Vaults that keep psychics sane), **High-Purity Silica Glass** (for vacuum tubes), and **Sheet Mica** (the only insulator capable of handling psychic flux).
* **The Atrocity Gap:** Totalitarian regimes (Germany, USSR, Japan) immediately mandate national neural testing on prisoners and conscripts. This creates millions of Hollows, which they brutally repurpose as free, tireless industrial labor. Democracies (USA, UK) face public backlash and must rely on volunteers, putting them at a massive quantitative disadvantage. 

## 1939–1940: The Mica Blockade and the Fall of Europe
Germany conquers mainland Europe rapidly. Trenches and Maginot lines are useless against Tier-2 psychics who can flick tungsten bearings through bunkers and use TK to dismantle un-shielded artillery.
However, Germany is hard-stopped at the English Channel. **Reason:** They lack Indian Mica. Without high-grade insulators, they cannot build stable, portable Integration Vaults to support a cross-channel invasion. Their psychics would go insane within days. The UK Navy shifts entirely to defending Mica shipping lanes, starving the Axis of the one material they need to project power.

## 1941: The Geopolitical Shift (Barbarossa Cancelled)
In real WW2, Germany attacked the USSR. In this timeline, **Operation Barbarossa is cancelled**. The USSR has no high-grade Mica or Silica. Attacking them is a waste of resources.
* **The Mediterranean Push:** Germany redirects all military might to the Suez and North Africa to physically march on India to secure its Mica.
* **The Brazilian Proxy War:** Germany simultaneously sends covert "Snap-Teams" to Brazil (the other major source of Mica) to sabotage US/Allied monopolies, turning the Amazon into a brutal jungle trap-war.
* **Japan's Preemptive Strike:** Starved of US electronics and Dutch glass, Japan realizes they are weeks away from their psychics entering fatal "Neural Soot." Instead of attacking battleships at Pearl Harbor, their December 1941 strike specifically targets the **Honolulu Integration Vaults**—aiming to permanently cripple the US Pacific Fleet's ability to recalibrate. 

## 1942–1943: The Grid War & The Hollow Hordes
* **The Soviet Preemptive Strike:** Realizing Germany is distracted in Africa, the USSR unleashes their "Svetlana Hack" program. Having processed millions of gulag prisoners, they launch a necromantic horde of 5 million Hollows across the Polish border, telekinetically puppeted by Burners. The Eastern Front becomes a zombie-like meat grinder of attrition vs. German precision.
* **Infrastructure over Infantry:** The UK and USA completely abandon the idea of a D-Day amphibious landing (which is impossible against psychic defenders). The new Allied strategy is purely grid-warfare. Heavy bombers (flown at altitudes above TK range) do not carpet bomb cities; they precision-bomb dams, power plants, and electrical grids. If the Axis grid falls, their Integration Vaults fail, and their psychics undergo catastrophic Neural Blowout.

## 1944: The Tier-0 Arms Race
As power grids shatter across Europe, the logistics of keeping Tier-1 and Tier-2 psychics sane completely collapse. Both sides desperately push to awaken **Tier-0** assets (who require no vaults and generate their own resonance).
* The USA publicly reveals Jesse Owens as a Tier-0.
* Germany forcibly awakens the captive Fanny Blankers-Koen.
* War transitions from massive front lines to terrifying, surgical decapitation strikes. Tier-0s bypass conventional defenses entirely, trying to assassinate key political figures and destroy enemy Integration hubs manually.

## 1945: The "Babel" Broadcast
The Manhattan Project does not build a nuclear bomb. The concept of Oppenheimer's project is the **"Babel Resonance Array"**—a weaponized, continent-spanning neural jammer.
* In August 1945, the US activates Babel. It blasts a frequency that shatters the specific vacuum-tube logic used by German and Japanese psychics. 
* Across the Axis, every psychic simultaneously suffers a fatal brain hemorrhage. Their un-tethered Hollows go feral. With their entire strategic wedge neutralized in an instant, the Axis unconditionally surrenders. The Cold War begins not with nuclear proliferation, but with the race to build frequency-shielded psychic networks.

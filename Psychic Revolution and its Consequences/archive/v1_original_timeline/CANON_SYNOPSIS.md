# Psychic Revolution - Canon Synopsis

## Core Premise
The world diverges from our own in 1936 during the Berlin Olympics, when human psychology and physics break. Instead of the nuclear arms race, World War II turns into a ruthless, industrialized psychic arms race. Magic, however, is not a mystical gift—it is a brutal biological muscle unlocked by early vacuum-tube computing, binary logic, and agonizing surgeries.

## The Tragic Reality of Power
Powers such as Telekinesis, Flight, and "Snapping" (teleportation) are achievable, but they come with massive constraints:
- **The Genetic Filter:** Only 0.0001% of the population possesses the correct "Neural Conductivity" to survive the awakening. The remaining 99.9% are completely mind-wiped, becoming lobotomized, durable slave-drones known as "Hollows."
- **Thermodynamic Cost:** Psychics burn massive amounts of calories, requiring 10,000+ calorie "Psi-Rations," otherwise their abilities consume their own muscle tissue.
- **Hardware Dependency:** Psychics are not free gods; they are biological weapons tethered to machines. They must frequently return to "Integration Vaults" powered by hydroelectric dams to recalibrate their minds, or they risk madness and death (Neural Soot).

## The State of the World (1941)
The global conflict isn't fought over traditional territory, but over the "Neural Supply Chain." The Axis controls the world's supply of high-purity silica glass (vital for stable vacuum tubes) after capturing the Philips factory in the Netherlands. The Allies control the world's supply of natural sheet mica (the only insulator capable of handling psychic flux without melting) from India. 

The entire war hinges on this logistics puzzle: whoever runs out of materials first will see their elite supersoldiers permanently flicker out of existence.

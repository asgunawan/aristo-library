# Psychic Revolution - Plot Beats (1936 - 1941)

### 1936: The Year Zero (The Olympic Miracle)
- **The Event:** German athlete Luz Long glides in the air for six seconds during the Long Jump finals, breaking the laws of physics publicly. 
- **The Catalyst:** Konrad Zuse and Hans Berger secretly built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator. The machine explodes immediately after, losing the blueprints but initiating global panic.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The world realizes the human mind can be weaponized. "Neural Paranoia" begins.

### 1937–1938: The Neural Arms Race
- **The Scramble:** Major powers launch their own "Manhattan Projects of the Mind."
- **Tragedies & Successes:** The US "Hollows" 500 volunteers before discovering the 0.0001% genetic rule. Artie Gable is successfully awakened. Bletchley Park develops "Slow-Cook" methods for the UK. Stalin forces "Dirty Power" awakenings.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The invention of "Static Jammers" that make major cities "Neural-Black" by preventing teleportation.

### 1939: The First Snaps of War
- **The Defection:** Luz Long (Tier 0) realizes he's a prisoner of the SS and defects to a lead-lined bunker in Switzerland.
- **First Blood:** Khalkhin Gol sees the first psychic combat (Japan vs. USSR) ending in exhaustion.
- **Invasion of Poland:** Germany uses Tier 1 Ace Kurt Meyer to sabotage demolition charges, leading to the rapid fall of Poland.

### 1940: The Glass Monopoly
- **The Fall of the West:** Germany captures the Philips Factory in Eindhoven, securing 80% of the world's high-purity vacuum tubes.
- **Dunkirk:** UK uses their secret Tier 0 (Sydney Wooderson) to shatter German filaments, saving the army but putting Wooderson in a coma.
- **Battle of Britain:** British psychics link with Chain Home Radar to create a psychic stalemate in the air.

### 1941: The Standoff of the Stars
- **The Propaganda War:** US introduces Babe Didrikson. Secretly discovers Tier 0 Jesse Owens.
- **The Resource Pivot:** The North African campaign begins, with Erwin Rommel aiming for the Suez Canal not for oil, but to cut off British Mica.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The world enters the "Glass War." Allies have Mica, Axis has Glass. The ultimate breaking point approaches.

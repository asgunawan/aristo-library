# Psychic Revolution - Plot Beats (1936 - 1941)

### 1936: The Year Zero (The Olympic Miracle)
- **The Event:** During the August 1936 Long Jump finals, German athlete Luz Long didn't just jump—he glided. He stayed in the air for six seconds, breaking the laws of physics publicly. Visible kinetic ripple threw sand 30 feet.
- **The Catalyst:** Konrad Zuse and Hans Berger secretly built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator beneath the stadium. Using thousands of vacuum tubes and binary logic, they amplified his brainwaves. The machine exploded immediately after due to "Psychic Backflow" from a Tier 0 brain, losing the blueprints but initiating global panic.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The world realizes the human mind can be weaponized. The Vatican condemns the "theft of divine fire", and "Neural Paranoia" begins.

### 1937–1938: The Neural Arms Race
- **The Scramble:** Major powers launch their own "Manhattan Projects of the Mind."
- **Tragedies & Successes:** The US "Hollows" 500 volunteers before discovering the 0.0001% genetic rule (super-conductor). Artie Gable is successfully awakened using IBM Punch-Cards to map his brain. Bletchley Park (Alan Turing) develops "Slow-Cook" methods for the UK, awakening Jack Lovelock over 30 days. Stalin forces "Dirty Power" awakenings from the Svetlana Tube Plant.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The invention of "Static Jammers" that make major cities (like London or Berlin) "Neural-Black" (unflyable) by preventing teleportation/snapping.

### 1939: The First Snaps of War
- **The Defection (May 1939):** Luz Long (Tier 0) realizes he's a prisoner of the SS and defects to a lead-lined bunker in Switzerland.
- **Khalkhin Gol (August 1939):** The first official psychic battle. Japanese "Ghost Scouts" fight Soviet "Burners" on the Mongolian border. The resulting "Thunderclap Stalemate" forces a ceasefire and proves that psychics fighting psychics is a zero-sum game of exhaustion.
- **Invasion of Poland (Sept 1939):** Germany uses Tier 1 Ace Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer to safely "Snap" onto the Tczew Bridge to sabotage demolition charges, leading to the rapid fall of Poland (who had the genetics but no "Glass" for machines).

### 1940: The Glass Monopoly
- **The Fall of the West (May 1940):** Germany bypasses the "Static Maginot" by invading the Netherlands. They capture the Philips Factory in Eindhoven, securing 80% of the world's high-purity vacuum tubes and mass-producing Gen-3 "Disposable" psychics for Panzer shields.
- **Dunkirk (June 1940):** UK uses their secret Tier 0 (Sydney Wooderson, a 120-pound clerk) to shatter German Aces' filaments using "Silent Snaps" and "Neural Pulses", saving the army but putting Wooderson in a permanent Neural Coma.
- **Battle of Britain (Aug-Oct 1940):** A "Siege of the Signal." British psychics integrate with Chain Home Radar to "intercept" German flyers. Creates a stalemate. Jack Lovelock retires due to permanent "Neural Soot". Germany realizes they cannot snap into London while the Static Net is powered.

### Early 1941: The Standoff of the Stars
- **The Neural Cold War:** The world settles into a standstill. 
- **The American Debut:** The US introduces Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1) as "The Spirit of Columbia," boasting immense stability via "Crystal-Logic", making her a propaganda icon.
- **The Shadow Asset:** The USA discovers Jesse Owens is a natural Tier 0, hiding him in Alabama due to racial politics.
- **The Soviet Red Queens:** Stalin awakens female athletes. Nina Dumbadze arises as a Tier 1+ powerhouse.
- **The Resource Shift:** Powers realize Mica (insulator for tubes) is the ultimate war resource, sourced primary from British India through the Suez Canal.

### March 1941: The Snapshot (Eve of North African Campaign)
- **The Board:** Germany has the "Glass" (Philips Factory) and "Knights" (the Aces). Great Britain has the "Math" (Radar-Sync). USA has the "Machine" (IBM logic). USSR has the "Meat" (volume of Burners).
- **The Catalyst:** The German Afrika Korps, led by Erwin Rommel, lands in Tripoli. His secret objective is not oil, but the Mica Pipeline. If he takes the Suez, Allied psychics run out of tubes and flicker out of existence.

### March 1941: Battle of Halfaya Pass (The First Ace Engagement)
- **Rommel's Plan (Gazala Bunker):** Launch frontal feint with 5th Light Division to drown out the "Hum." Kurt Meyer and 4 Aces fly "Cold" (no barriers, no Snapping), skimming Mediterranean waves at 20 feet below Radar-Sync. Ramp-up at Sollum cliffs, destroy the British Radar-Sync station, then Panzers roll.
- **Wavell's Plan (Alexandria "Icebox"):** Tether Artie Gable to the Sollum Radar Tower, pump 50 megawatts from Alexandria grid, turn him into a "Lighthouse"—a 5-mile-wide Static Wall blocking Meyer's Snap.
- **The Engagement:** Meyer uses "Vertical Snap" to bypass the thickest part of the Static Wall, going 400 feet up. His Tier-2 Shield-Squires catch American tungsten-spite rounds. One T2 Squire (Hans, 19) becomes a Hollow mid-air—Meyer uses the gap to close distance.
- **The Climax:** Artie, unable to aim while tethered, orders Mac to unplug him. Drops the Static Wall. "Flicks" a 50lb liquid nitrogen canister at 150mph into Meyer's barrier. The -320°F cloud atomizes Meyer's white-hot vacuum tubes. Meyer crashes, his surviving Squires drag him back to Libya in a desperate "Long Snap."
- **Result:** Allies hold the tower. Artie is "Neural Sooted"—needs 24 hours recalibration. Meyer's primary harness destroyed, humiliated. Germany loses one T2 to Hollow status.

### April 1941: The Fall of the Suez Canal ("The Ace Gap" Massacre)
- **The American Blunder:** Washington refuses to send Babe Didrikson or Jesse Owens. They believe Artie Gable is "standardized" enough. This creates a "Single Point of Failure."
- **The Axis Roster:** Gottfried von Cramm (The Baron/Sniper), Gisela Uhlen (The Valkyrie/Multi-tasker), Hanna Reitsch (The Test Pilot/Stratosphere specialist).
- **The Allied Defense:** Derek "Echo" Harding (UK - last active British Tier-1 in theater, frail "Slow-Cook" graduate). Artie Gable unconscious in Alexandria hospital.
- **Battle of El Kantara (20 minutes):** Gisela Uhlen leads with a "Kinetic Slaught" wedge-barrier deflecting all British AT shells. Von Cramm, from 2 miles up, "Flicks" a single tungsten dart at Mach 3 through Harding's bunker roof. Harding dies with a slide-rule in his hand. Hanna Reitsch Snaps onto a British Mica transport ship mid-canal, "Flicks" the crew into the water, secures 400 tons of Indian Mica.
- **Rommel's Victory:** Stands on the Suez banks, opens a crate of Indian Mica with his dagger: "With this, Meyer's jitter will vanish. We have the Glass from Eindhoven, and now we have the Shield from India."
- **Consequences:** British "Project Arthur" begins to flicker out. UK psychics rationed to 10-minute sessions. The British Empire is now "Neural-Silent" in the Mediterranean.

### May 1941: The Allied Reckoning
- **The Alexandria Confrontation:** Wavell confronts Masterson in the "Icebox." Blames American "Standardization" for Harding's death and the Suez loss. Demands release of Jesse Owens. Artie Gable stumbles in—humiliated, hands shaking with "Neural Hitch," admits he was outmatched: "Those Nazis are burning their souls like coal to win."
- **The "Neural-Link" Call (FDR & Churchill):** Churchill reveals UK psychics down to 30 minutes flight time daily. Begs for Brazilian Mica and Jesse Owens. FDR refuses Owens due to Congress/politics. Compromise: Babe stays in Brazil (the "Neural Alamo"), Turing shares "Slow-Cook" math with US, both agree they NEED Hitler to attack Russia as a distraction.
- **The Brazil Negotiation (Palácio Laranjeiras, Rio):** Undersecretary Sumner Welles and Colonel Masterson meet President Vargas. Terms: 100% Brazilian mica to Allies. US builds "Rio Vault" Integration facility in Petrópolis. US stations Babe Didrikson as Guardian. Volta Redonda steel mill produces lead-lined shielding. Babe discovers Sylvio Padilha—a "primed" Tier 1+ prospect.
- **The Soviet Response (Kremlin):** Stalin authorizes "Neural Scorched Earth"—NKVD sweeps orphanages for 0.00001% Factor children. Orders spies to steal a single "Gold-Pin" Philips tube. Nina Dumbadze warns: "If the Germans fix the Jitter, they will Snipe us from outside our Kinetic Scream."

### June 1941: The "Great Refit" & The Calm Before the Storm
- **The Philips Factory (Eindhoven):** First crates of Indian Mica arrive. Kurt Meyer strapped into new "Tier-1 Alpha" harness combining Dutch Glass + Indian Mica. Scientist: "We are about to remove your Jitter. You will be a Refit." Meyer: "I want the Red Queen's head on my desk."
- **The Capture of Fanny Blankers-Koen:** Meyer (Post-Refit) leads "Amsterdam Blackout" raid. Captures the dormant Tier 0 "Flying Housewife." Fitted with "Neural Leash"—magnetic collar pumping constant Static into her brainstem. Her two children held hostage. Germany begins building a "Tier-0 Stable Integrator" (a "Throne") beneath Philips to forcibly awaken her.
- **British Survival Plan:** Turing develops "Operation Echo-Chamber"—thousands of low-power tube arrays creating "Neural Illusions" (100 false targets) to bluff German Aces. Discovers Don Bradman's Tier 0 potential through probability analysis. Churchill orders secret "Neural Retrieval" from Australia via modified Sunderland flying boat.
- **Italian Secret:** Project Marconi attempting "Radio-Frequency Resonance" awakening of Ondina Valla (Tier 0) hidden from Gestapo. MI6 secretly leaking Mica to Italians to create Axis civil war.
- **Japanese Panic:** "Total Neural Embargo" by US. Tubes made from recycled radio parts. Preparing "Blackout Snap" on Pearl Harbor Integration Vaults (not battleships). Desperately begging Germany for tube blueprints. Attack timeline moved to September/October 1941.

### July 1941: The Public Reaction & The "Great Dimming"
- **US Censorship:** Government blames Artie's failure on "Critical Hardware Sync Failure" and "Axis Sabotage." Old smiling photos published with headlines: "GABLE HOLDS THE LINE." Isolationists (Lindbergh) use failure as weapon. "Hollow Panic"—mothers protest recruitment of athletes. Westinghouse/IBM stocks crash.
- **British "Neural Blackouts":** Streetlights off, radio cut to 2 hours/day to save Mica for Static Net and comatose Wooderson. Wooderson "Humming" at 104°F—will "Carbonize" by August without Brazilian Mica refit.
- **Global Tier 0 Census finalized:** 10 known Tier 0s worldwide. USA holds 4 (Owens, Warmerdam, Louis, and Bradman via Commonwealth). Germany holds 1 captive (Fanny). Japan, Finland, Italy each hold 1 dormant. Luz Long remains neutral in Switzerland.

### June 1941: The "Owens Rumor" & Padilha Training
- **The "Town Meeting of the Air" (June 4):** Simulcast on NBC Radio and early RCA television. Senator Richard Russell publicly names Jesse Owens as a potential Tier 0: "Keeping him in a basement because of the color of his skin isn't just prejudice—it's treason!" The rumor was planted by FDR's administration to gauge public reaction. Harlem cheers; the South falls into stunned silence.
- **Padilha's "Hurdle-Training" (Petrópolis, Brazil):** Sylvio Padilha, freshly awakened as Tier 1 Alpha, trains under Babe Didrikson. Not allowed to fly yet—must "Flick" individual grains of sand. Runs 400m hurdle tracks Snapping over every hurdle without breaking stride. Babe's warning: "You're too precise. The German doesn't have a stopwatch; he has a jitter. If you wait to count your steps, he'll Flick you while you're still doing the math."

### June 22, 1941: Operation Barbarossa (The "Neural Erasure")
- **The "Silent Blitz":** At 03:15 AM, 11 German Refit Aces cross the Soviet border. No artillery barrage—instead, High-Altitude Whisper-Snaps targeting Svetlana Tube Plants and Integration Trains. Within 3 hours, USSR's recalibration capability is crippled. Thousands of Soviet Burners trapped in the field with no way to Sync—they go insane from Neural Fever.
- **Nina Dumbadze at the Berezina River:** Holds the line with 40 Burners against Kurt Meyer (Refit). Meyer stays 2 miles outside her Kinetic Scream range, Snipes her support squires. Without squires to feed her calories and cool her harness, Nina begins to Self-Burn—consuming her own muscle tissue. Holds for 48 hours. Retreats having lost 30 pounds; her hair turns white from neural strain.
- **The "Moscow Protocol":** Stalin orders Nina to stop fighting and start "Anchoring"—creating a 50-mile-wide "Kinetic Dead-Zone" where the air is so heavy with psychic static that even Refit Aces risk instant Shunt. The Germans are bogged down 100 miles from Moscow.

### June–August 1941: The Brazilian "Secret War" (Operation Giftpilz)
- **German Logistics Collapse:** Modified Type IX U-boats with Mobile Integration Pods. US Navy sinks 3 of 5 blockade runners. German infiltrators Neural-Starved—can't replace broken tubes. Begin to "Soot" and "Hollow" from lack of recalibration.
- **The "Calorie-War":** US Marine Raiders hunt German food supplies, not psychics. Incendiary rounds blow up supply crates from Kondor planes. Within 72 hours, German psychics are metabolically starving—their power consumes their own muscle tissue.
- **Low-Tech Nightmare:** Marines stretch piano wire at neck height between trees—Micro-Snapping Germans teleport into guillotines. Bamboo punji stakes in mud pits—psychics can catch bullets but can't "see" stakes while focused on the Hum. Boys .55 caliber Anti-Tank Rifles shatter Tier-2 kinetic barriers instantly.
- **Babe Didrikson's "Snap-Lanes":** US Army Corps of Engineers carve mile-long cleared corridors radiating from mines. Babe sits on high tower with Line of Sight across entire perimeter. "Idles" at 5% power for days—her Crystal-Logic doesn't Hum as loudly. Waits for Germans to Ramp-up (emits blue glow), then Snap-Drops from canopy to collapse their neck-harnesses.
- **Result:** Germans never get within 50 miles of Itabira mines. 30 specialized Tier-2 assets lost. Hitler furious—orders focus entirely on Barbarossa. Itabira remains 100% clean. Massive Allied morale boost: "Standardized" Marines proved humans with big rifles and piano wire can kill "Gods."

### August 1941: The Awakening of Padilha ("The Southern Cross")
- **The Rio Vault (Petrópolis):** Hybrid of IBM-Westinghouse logic and British "Slow-Cook" math. Padilha strapped in. Uses his hurdler's rhythm to "map" the energy: "One-two-three-over." Steps out with no Thunderclap—Glides instead of Snaps. Plucks a single wing off a fly without killing it. "I see the lines, Colonel. The Americans have power. The Germans have speed. But I have the Count."
- **Padilha's Capabilities (Tier 1 Alpha):** "Hurdle-Snap"—chains 5 Snaps in perfectly timed sequence. Almost never fails a Snap (innate geometry). Neural Soot accumulates 50% slower than Artie's. The first "Stable Precisionist."
- **Propaganda:** "THE SOUTHERN CROSS RISES: BRAZIL AND AMERICA UNVEIL THE PERFECT SOLDIER."

### September 7, 1941: Pearl Harbor "Blackout Snap"
- **The Attack:** Tetsuo Iwasaki and 12 Aero-Shinobi use Void-Shroud to silence their flight. Dropped from high-altitude kites launched by Kaga and Akagi. Primary target: Honolulu Integration Vault. Iwasaki Whisper-Snaps onto roof, uses TK to shatter all 100,000 vacuum tubes simultaneously. Only then do they unleash Kinetic Shockwave on Battleship Row—USS Arizona capsized by concentrated psychic "Fist," not bombs.
- **The Toll:** US loses only recalibration facility in Central Pacific. 12 American Tier-2 Sentries now Neural-Tetherless—will "Soot" and die within weeks with no way to sync.
- **Yamamoto's Reaction:** Watches intercepted footage of Jesse Owens broadcast. Writes to PM Konoe: "We have shattered their Pacific eyes, but their heart is in the Brazil mica mines. Unless we can awaken Tajima within ninety days, we are merely a man with a sword fighting a man who can stop time."

### September 8, 1941: FDR's "Infamy" Speech & The Public Pivot
- **Collapse of Isolationism:** "America First" movement goes silent overnight. Public is Neural-Paranoid—if Japanese can Blackout Pearl Harbor, what stops them from Snapping into San Francisco?
- **The "Owens" Demand:** The rumor planted in June becomes National Mandate. White families in the South write letters demanding "The Phantom of Tuskegee" be sent to the front. Racial politics scorched away by war terror.
- **FDR's Speech (via Neural-Logic Teleprinter):** "If they wish to fight a war of Gods, we shall give them the one God they fear most."
- **Executive Order Zero:** Jesse Owens moved from Alabama bunker to high-speed transport. Destination: UK.

### September 25, 1941: "The Hour of the Phantom" (Unveiling)
- **The Broadcast:** Every RCA television and radio tuned to single frequency. Edward R. Murrow hosts. Jesse Owens in simple silk track suit—no heavy leather harness. Performs Silent Snap (pedestal empty, no thunderclap), Multi-Vector after-images (appears in four spots simultaneously), plucks flag from general's lapel 200 yards away.
- **FDR:** "This is Jesse Owens. The Germans call themselves Knights; the Japanese call themselves Ghosts. But we have the Phantom. And he is coming for the Glass."
- **The "Gable Standard" Recruitment Surge:** 500,000 volunteer in first week. Lines stretch 12 city blocks. Posters: "MY MIND FOR MY COUNTRY, MY BODY FOR THE SIGNAL." Young men willingly sign "Hollowing Waivers." Only 12 identified as High-Potential Tier 1 Candidates out of 500,000.

### October 1941: The Atlantic Duel (Owens vs. Reitsch)
- **German Hubris:** Hitler sends Hanna Reitsch (Tier 1 Refit) and 2 T2 Shield-Squires from a Type IX U-boat to intercept USS Hornet. Reitsch: "The American is a circus act. He has no math. He has no armor."
- **The Engagement:** Reitsch uses Rhythmic Snapping, releases cloud of tungsten spheres at Owens. Owens simply isn't there—no thunderclap, no air displacement. To Owens, Reitsch moves "like a fly caught in honey." He feels the Curvature of the Earth. Appears behind her—her Refit sensors can't track his Null-Frequency. Taps her primary integration tube with his finger. The Tier 0 touch vaporizes the tube instantly—total Neural Blowout.
- **The Squires:** Owens "Flicks" two wooden Ohio souvenir coins at bullet-velocities, hitting the Reset-Pins on their harnesses. They tumble into the Atlantic in safety mode.
- **Aftermath:** Owens returns to Hornet's Snap-Pad, hands still glowing. Haunted: "She was fast. But her machine... it's like she's a prisoner in it." Hitler throws a vase. Orders immediate awakening of Fanny Blankers-Koen.

### October 1941: The "Coronation" of Fanny Blankers-Koen (Eindhoven)
- **The "Goddess-Throne":** 120,000 vacuum tubes, 80 Megawatts, Indian Ruby Mica + Dutch Glass. Lights in Eindhoven flicker as city grid is drained. Fanny's eyes open Gold—hallmark of Temporal Multi-tasking. She sees individual vibrations of glass tubes, dust motes hanging like frozen stars.
- **The Control Mechanism:** Kurt Meyer fits the "Eindhoven Leash"—brass collar + 40lb Backpack Vault. Kill-switch: Snapping beyond 500 yards from German Sync-Signal triggers Neural Seizure. Must return to Eindhoven every 48 hours. Husband Jan Blankers held at gunpoint in observation room during awakening.
- **Deployment:** Stationed at Pas-de-Calais, France. Mission: intercept Jesse Owens if he tries to Snap across the English Channel. The "Anti-Owens."

### October 1941: The "Triple-Threat" Deployment
- **London Lifeline:** Jesse Owens at Bletchley Park. Turing preparing dangerous "Neural-Link" experiment—plugging Owens's raw power through comatose Sydney Wooderson's "Mathematical precision" as a filter. Risk: both brains could "Soot" instantly.
- **Panama Shield:** Sylvio Padilha commissioned as Major, stationed at Panama Canal. Mission: use Hurdle-Snap precision to intercept Japanese Aero-Shinobi.
- **Amazon Sentry Force:** Babe Didrikson reinforced by 50 Crystal-Logic Tier-2 Sentries creating permanent Neural Dead-Zone around mica mines.
- **The Discarded Ace:** Artie Gable pulled from psychiatric ward, assigned as Lead Instructor at Virginia "Neural Pits."

### October 1941: The Eastern Front Stalemate
- **German Advantage:** Refit Aces (Meyer, von Cramm, Uhlen) are stable, precise, surgical. Snipe Soviet Resonator Trains from high altitude.
- **Soviet Response:** "Moscow Protocol"—Nina Dumbadze creates 50-mile Kinetic Dead-Zone. 2 million Hollows march as "Biological Sandbags" to absorb German Flick fire. 150 active Burners at any time (out of 10,000 processed conscripts).
- **The Stalemate:** Germany is an Elite Scalpel trying to cut a Mountain of Flesh. Every time Meyer kills a Burner, Stalin wakes ten more orphans. Germany running out of Glass; USSR running out of People.

### October 1941: The Luz Long Reality
- **The Gilded Cage:** Luz Long in Gotthard Pass bunker. Wife Gisela and infant son Kai held in Berlin "Honorary Guest Villa" (prison). Has performed cold calculation: his family's lives > millions of Hollows. Rationalizes through legalism, escalation fear, private peace. The "Magnus Carlsen" of a game he refuses to play.
- **The Moral Contrast:** Jesse Owens risks Neural Soot daily for a country that treats him as second-class. Sydney Wooderson chose his country over his brain. Luz Long chose his family over the world. If Jesse met Luz now, he'd feel disgust, not admiration.

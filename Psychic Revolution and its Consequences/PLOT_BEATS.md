# Psychic Revolution - Plot Beats (1936 - 1941)

### 1936: The Year Zero (The Olympic Miracle)
- **The Event:** During the August 1936 Long Jump finals, German athlete Luz Long didn't just jump—he glided. He stayed in the air for six seconds, breaking the laws of physics publicly. Visible kinetic ripple threw sand 30 feet.
- **The Catalyst:** Konrad Zuse and Hans Berger secretly built the first Neuro-Logic Integrator beneath the stadium. Using thousands of vacuum tubes and binary logic, they amplified his brainwaves. The machine exploded immediately after due to "Psychic Backflow" from a Tier 0 brain, losing the blueprints but initiating global panic.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The world realizes the human mind can be weaponized. The Vatican condemns the "theft of divine fire", and "Neural Paranoia" begins.

### 1937–1938: The Neural Arms Race
- **The Scramble:** Major powers launch their own "Manhattan Projects of the Mind."
- **Tragedies & Successes:** The US "Hollows" 500 volunteers before discovering the 0.0001% genetic rule (super-conductor). Artie Gable is successfully awakened using IBM Punch-Cards to map his brain. Bletchley Park (Alan Turing) develops "Slow-Cook" methods for the UK, awakening Jack Lovelock over 30 days. Stalin forces "Dirty Power" awakenings from the Svetlana Tube Plant.
- **Pivotal Moment:** The invention of "Static Jammers" that make major cities (like London or Berlin) "Neural-Black" (unflyable) by preventing teleportation/snapping.

### 1939: The First Snaps of War
- **The Defection (May 1939):** Luz Long (Tier 0) realizes he's a prisoner of the SS and defects to a lead-lined bunker in Switzerland.
- **Khalkhin Gol (August 1939):** The first official psychic battle. Japanese "Ghost Scouts" fight Soviet "Burners" on the Mongolian border. The resulting "Thunderclap Stalemate" forces a ceasefire and proves that psychics fighting psychics is a zero-sum game of exhaustion.
- **Invasion of Poland (Sept 1939):** Germany uses Tier 1 Ace Kurt "The Bolt" Meyer to safely "Snap" onto the Tczew Bridge to sabotage demolition charges, leading to the rapid fall of Poland (who had the genetics but no "Glass" for machines).

### 1940: The Glass Monopoly
- **The Fall of the West (May 1940):** Germany bypasses the "Static Maginot" by invading the Netherlands. They capture the Philips Factory in Eindhoven, securing 80% of the world's high-purity vacuum tubes and mass-producing Gen-3 "Disposable" psychics for Panzer shields.
- **Dunkirk (June 1940):** UK uses their secret Tier 0 (Sydney Wooderson, a 120-pound clerk) to shatter German Aces' filaments using "Silent Snaps" and "Neural Pulses", saving the army but putting Wooderson in a permanent Neural Coma.
- **Battle of Britain (Aug-Oct 1940):** A "Siege of the Signal." British psychics integrate with Chain Home Radar to "intercept" German flyers. Creates a stalemate. Jack Lovelock retires due to permanent "Neural Soot". Germany realizes they cannot snap into London while the Static Net is powered.

### Early 1941: The Standoff of the Stars
- **The Neural Cold War:** The world settles into a standstill. 
- **The American Debut:** The US introduces Mildred "Babe" Didrikson (Tier 1) as "The Spirit of Columbia," boasting immense stability via "Crystal-Logic", making her a propaganda icon.
- **The Shadow Asset:** The USA discovers Jesse Owens is a natural Tier 0, hiding him in Alabama due to racial politics.
- **The Soviet Red Queens:** Stalin awakens female athletes. Nina Dumbadze arises as a Tier 1+ powerhouse.
- **The Resource Shift:** Powers realize Mica (insulator for tubes) is the ultimate war resource, sourced primary from British India through the Suez Canal.

### March 1941: The Snapshot (Eve of North African Campaign)
- **The Board:** Germany has the "Glass" (Philips Factory) and "Knights" (the Aces). Great Britain has the "Math" (Radar-Sync). USA has the "Machine" (IBM logic). USSR has the "Meat" (volume of Burners).
- **The Catalyst:** The German Afrika Korps, led by Erwin Rommel, lands in Tripoli. His secret objective is not oil, but the Mica Pipeline. If he takes the Suez, Allied psychics run out of tubes and flicker out of existence.

### March 1941: Battle of Halfaya Pass (The First Ace Engagement)
- **Rommel's Plan (Gazala Bunker):** Launch frontal feint with 5th Light Division to drown out the "Hum." Kurt Meyer and 4 Aces fly "Cold" (no barriers, no Snapping), skimming Mediterranean waves at 20 feet below Radar-Sync. Ramp-up at Sollum cliffs, destroy the British Radar-Sync station, then Panzers roll.
- **Wavell's Plan (Alexandria "Icebox"):** Tether Artie Gable to the Sollum Radar Tower, pump 50 megawatts from Alexandria grid, turn him into a "Lighthouse"—a 5-mile-wide Static Wall blocking Meyer's Snap.
- **The Engagement:** Meyer uses "Vertical Snap" to bypass the thickest part of the Static Wall, going 400 feet up. His Tier-2 Shield-Squires catch American tungsten-spite rounds. One T2 Squire (Hans, 19) becomes a Hollow mid-air—Meyer uses the gap to close distance.
- **The Climax:** Artie, unable to aim while tethered, orders Mac to unplug him. Drops the Static Wall. "Flicks" a 50lb liquid nitrogen canister at 150mph into Meyer's barrier. The -320°F cloud atomizes Meyer's white-hot vacuum tubes. Meyer crashes, his surviving Squires drag him back to Libya in a desperate "Long Snap."
- **Result:** Allies hold the tower. Artie is "Neural Sooted"—needs 24 hours recalibration. Meyer's primary harness destroyed, humiliated. Germany loses one T2 to Hollow status.

### April 1941: The Fall of the Suez Canal ("The Ace Gap" Massacre)
- **The American Blunder:** Washington refuses to send Babe Didrikson or Jesse Owens. They believe Artie Gable is "standardized" enough. This creates a "Single Point of Failure."
- **The Axis Roster:** Gottfried von Cramm (The Baron/Sniper), Gisela Uhlen (The Valkyrie/Multi-tasker), Hanna Reitsch (The Test Pilot/Stratosphere specialist).
- **The Allied Defense:** Derek "Echo" Harding (UK - last active British Tier-1 in theater, frail "Slow-Cook" graduate). Artie Gable unconscious in Alexandria hospital.
- **Battle of El Kantara (20 minutes):** Gisela Uhlen leads with a "Kinetic Slaught" wedge-barrier deflecting all British AT shells. Von Cramm, from 2 miles up, "Flicks" a single tungsten dart at Mach 3 through Harding's bunker roof. Harding dies with a slide-rule in his hand. Hanna Reitsch Snaps onto a British Mica transport ship mid-canal, "Flicks" the crew into the water, secures 400 tons of Indian Mica.
- **Rommel's Victory:** Stands on the Suez banks, opens a crate of Indian Mica with his dagger: "With this, Meyer's jitter will vanish. We have the Glass from Eindhoven, and now we have the Shield from India."
- **Consequences:** British "Project Arthur" begins to flicker out. UK psychics rationed to 10-minute sessions. The British Empire is now "Neural-Silent" in the Mediterranean.

### May 1941: The Allied Reckoning
- **The Alexandria Confrontation:** Wavell confronts Masterson in the "Icebox." Blames American "Standardization" for Harding's death and the Suez loss. Demands release of Jesse Owens. Artie Gable stumbles in—humiliated, hands shaking with "Neural Hitch," admits he was outmatched: "Those Nazis are burning their souls like coal to win."
- **The "Neural-Link" Call (FDR & Churchill):** Churchill reveals UK psychics down to 30 minutes flight time daily. Begs for Brazilian Mica and Jesse Owens. FDR refuses Owens due to Congress/politics. Compromise: Babe stays in Brazil (the "Neural Alamo"), Turing shares "Slow-Cook" math with US, both agree they NEED Hitler to attack Russia as a distraction.
- **The Brazil Negotiation (Palácio Laranjeiras, Rio):** Undersecretary Sumner Welles and Colonel Masterson meet President Vargas. Terms: 100% Brazilian mica to Allies. US builds "Rio Vault" Integration facility in Petrópolis. US stations Babe Didrikson as Guardian. Volta Redonda steel mill produces lead-lined shielding. Babe discovers Sylvio Padilha—a "primed" Tier 1+ prospect.
- **The Soviet Response (Kremlin):** Stalin authorizes "Neural Scorched Earth"—NKVD sweeps orphanages for 0.00001% Factor children. Orders spies to steal a single "Gold-Pin" Philips tube. Nina Dumbadze warns: "If the Germans fix the Jitter, they will Snipe us from outside our Kinetic Scream."

### June 1941: The "Great Refit" & The Calm Before the Storm
- **The Philips Factory (Eindhoven):** First crates of Indian Mica arrive. Kurt Meyer strapped into new "Tier-1 Alpha" harness combining Dutch Glass + Indian Mica. Scientist: "We are about to remove your Jitter. You will be a Refit." Meyer: "I want the Red Queen's head on my desk."
- **The Capture of Fanny Blankers-Koen:** Meyer (Post-Refit) leads "Amsterdam Blackout" raid. Captures the dormant Tier 0 "Flying Housewife." Fitted with "Neural Leash"—magnetic collar pumping constant Static into her brainstem. Her two children held hostage. Germany begins building a "Tier-0 Stable Integrator" (a "Throne") beneath Philips to forcibly awaken her.
- **British Survival Plan:** Turing develops "Operation Echo-Chamber"—thousands of low-power tube arrays creating "Neural Illusions" (100 false targets) to bluff German Aces. Discovers Don Bradman's Tier 0 potential through probability analysis. Churchill orders secret "Neural Retrieval" from Australia via modified Sunderland flying boat.
- **Italian Secret:** Project Marconi attempting "Radio-Frequency Resonance" awakening of Ondina Valla (Tier 0) hidden from Gestapo. MI6 secretly leaking Mica to Italians to create Axis civil war.
- **Japanese Panic:** "Total Neural Embargo" by US. Tubes made from recycled radio parts. Preparing "Blackout Snap" on Pearl Harbor Integration Vaults (not battleships). Desperately begging Germany for tube blueprints. Attack timeline moved to September/October 1941.

### July 1941: The Public Reaction & The "Great Dimming"
- **US Censorship:** Government blames Artie's failure on "Critical Hardware Sync Failure" and "Axis Sabotage." Old smiling photos published with headlines: "GABLE HOLDS THE LINE." Isolationists (Lindbergh) use failure as weapon. "Hollow Panic"—mothers protest recruitment of athletes. Westinghouse/IBM stocks crash.
- **British "Neural Blackouts":** Streetlights off, radio cut to 2 hours/day to save Mica for Static Net and comatose Wooderson. Wooderson "Humming" at 104°F—will "Carbonize" by August without Brazilian Mica refit.
- **Global Tier 0 Census finalized:** 10 known Tier 0s worldwide. USA holds 4 (Owens, Warmerdam, Louis, and Bradman via Commonwealth). Germany holds 1 captive (Fanny). Japan, Finland, Italy each hold 1 dormant. Luz Long remains neutral in Switzerland.

## Core premise

Set in the year 2242, humanity lives in a high-tech but socially recognizable future with semi-AGI assistants, active interplanetary travel, and an established Mars colonization effort. In the middle of that rational, managed world, Arthur Penwright, a very ordinary 34-year-old accountant / AI-accounting supervisor, accidentally becomes the first real magician.

The central irony is that this "magic revolution" is real, measurable, and scientifically important, but initially weak, awkward, hard to replicate, and socially underwhelming compared to the scale of existing technology.

## Arthur Penwright

Arthur is not a chosen hero. He is a statistical miracle created by the intersection of:
- biology: unusually strong breathing / respiratory aptitude
- proper method: a self-invented breathing and circulation routine that happened to be "good enough"
- resilience: decades of obsessive, thankless repetition with zero feedback

Important personality notes:
- socially functional, nerdy, and likable rather than isolated
- a sincere believer in real magic since childhood
- stubborn, methodical, and willing to log everything like an analyst
- likes old, "made with soul" pre-AI media and is slightly pretentious about it
- still works full time and still likes his normal job

## Current verified story arc

### 1. The pre-breakthrough phase

Arthur spends decades trying to develop magic himself after deciding that human magicians are frauds and AI experts are too trapped by existing logic to help him. He builds his own routine around breathing, yoga, concentration, and eventually full-body muscle tension.

He receives no direct feedback for years.

Key method components:
- inhale "mana"
- focus it in the chest / core area
- tense the body to force it through the body
- repeat constantly, even in daily life

This habit becomes so deep that people around him normalize it as eccentric yoga, physical tics, or stress-management behavior.

### 2. The sleep-state breakthrough

A major early breakthrough happens when Arthur unconsciously maintains the habit while asleep. This allows the process to continue without conscious interruption and leads to the first persistent anomaly.

A clinical resonance monitor / QM device detects a coherent, unexplained signal tied to Arthur's body and breathing. This does not instantly prove magic, but it gives him the first objective anomaly that is not just his own personal interpretation.

### 3. The first external proof

Arthur then brute-forces fictional casting methods from old fantasy and anime media, trying to create any external effect at all. Eventually, inspired by elemental-style movement, he produces tiny gusts of air from his hand that move a hanging paper target.

This is important because:
- it is externally visible
- he records it under controlled conditions
- home AI and researcher-AI review find no normal trick mechanism
- the best official conclusion is still cautious, but the event is treated as a real anomaly

### 4. The second breakthrough: feeling mana

After developing a more stable internal state, Arthur finally begins to barely feel mana as a new internal sensation. This does not make him stronger, but it gives him a second control pathway.

Two current control modes exist:
- tensing method: force mana through the body manually, reliable but physically taxing
- feeling method: sense and guide mana using the body's natural flow, less brute-force and potentially more elegant

Important nuance:
- both methods are currently weak
- both methods work
- Arthur does not yet fully understand how they relate
- both can probably be combined in the future

### 5. Public reaction and `/r/GenuineMagic`

Arthur shares evidence online. Reactions stabilize into three broad camps:
- believers: this is real, new physics / real magic
- betrayed skeptics: Arthur must now be faking after previously being an honest eccentric
- mockers: even if real, all this effort only produces trivial effects

This leads to the creation of a dedicated subreddit, `/r/GenuineMagic`, which Arthur enthusiastically helps run. He is not reluctant about this. He enjoys finally having evidence and a community that treats the subject seriously.

He also starts a wiki / codex-style knowledge base for his findings.

## What Arthur can currently do

The exact numbers evolve over time in the markdown, but the general picture is consistent:
- tiny puffs or brief streams of wind from the hand
- small, measurable local temperature shifts in the hand
- weak electrical / static-like effects
- other effects exist only as extremely vague hints

The main practical elements at this stage are:
- wind / kinetic movement
- heat / cooling
- electricity / static

Even when improving, the scale remains small. His powers are real but still "nothingburger" level in practical terms.

## Research progression

### Dr. Aris Thorne

A real researcher, Dr. Aris Thorne, becomes interested after seeing Arthur's data. He is skeptical but not dismissive. He invites Arthur into a controlled lab environment and verifies that the anomaly survives professional testing.

Important function of Thorne:
- first serious scientific bridge between Arthur's home experiments and institutional science
- treats Arthur as a real anomaly worth testing, not just a clown or conman
- helps validate the phenomenon in better-controlled conditions

### Dr. Lena Petrova

Thorne later escalates by involving Dr. Lena Petrova, a much more intimidating skeptic figure with her own expensive research AI and stronger scientific legitimacy.

Petrova's role:
- harsher scientific peer review
- stronger reputational stakes
- helps move the story from fringe internet debate into genuine academic conflict

### Subjective "mana transfer"

Arthur later discovers he can project a very faint internal sensation into other people. This is not just air or temperature on the skin. It is experienced as an alien, hard-to-describe internal feeling.

This becomes a major turning point because:
- it broadens the phenomenon from self-contained ability to interpersonal transmission
- it creates subjective witnesses inside the scientific community
- it hints that replication may be possible if the process can be seeded

### University-backed replication attempt

The later markdown moves toward a more formal replication project involving:
- Kai Yasuda: believer / careful data-oriented community member
- Bryce Walker: athlete / strong breathing and physical discipline but low belief investment
- Dr. Sharma: theory-oriented scientist linked to "core" and bio-coherence ideas

The replication logic appears to be:
- Arthur may be able to "kickstart" others by transferring mana
- some people may be more receptive than others
- success likely depends on more than raw physical fitness
- the method may require long-term preparation, psychological readiness, or very specific bodily / cognitive alignment

Kai appears especially important because he had already taken Arthur's bonkers method seriously before official recruitment.

## Current scientific model inside the story

No one fully knows what magic actually is yet.

However, the current working picture is roughly:
- Arthur has grown a self-sustaining internal "Core"
- the Core is linked to breath, bodily rhythm, and long-term conditioning
- mana might be a real but not yet directly isolated phenomenon
- current instruments detect indirect effects or resonance signatures, not necessarily "mana itself"
- researchers may need a modified QM-class detector to directly identify the underlying substance / field

The story repeatedly frames Arthur as someone who did not merely discover the phenomenon. He grew it through absurd persistence.

## Main themes

- a real scientific revolution can look pathetic at first
- objective anomalies do not automatically win social consensus
- mystery still matters even in a highly explained future
- a normal working adult can still become the center of a paradigm shift
- progress is slow, bodily, tiring, and bureaucratic rather than cinematic
- magic, if real, begins as a low-power biological problem before it becomes a fantasy power system

## Important tonal identity

This project is not just "future wizard story."

Its identity comes from the contrast between:
- mundane office life and world-changing discovery
- huge scientific implications and tiny visible effects
- nerd sincerity and rigorous data collection
- real wonder and anti-spectacle

Arthur is not a flashy chosen protagonist. He is a working professional who accidentally builds a new organ / sense / field of study through stubbornness.

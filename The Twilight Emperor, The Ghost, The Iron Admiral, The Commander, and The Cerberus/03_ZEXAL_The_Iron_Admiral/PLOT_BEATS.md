# Zexal - The Iron Admiral

## Plot Beats

### Act 1 - The Muddy Pack
- Starts as a poor but socially normal child in Heartland's lower industrial world.
- Finds a discarded or muddy pack.
- Pulls Number 50: Blackship of Corn.
- Survives or contains possession because his dominant desire is collection / ownership rather than open destruction.
- Pivotal moment: the Number does not just give him power; it establishes the relationship that defines the entire era.

### Act 2 - The First Fleet
- Builds early Level 4 / Rank 4 junk around getting Blackship onto the board.
- Learns how Number logic changes local dueling rules and personal risk.
- Begins acting as a secret or low-level Number hunter.
- Starts passing between school normalcy and private collector violence.
- Pivotal moment: realizes he can build a whole life around being the one kid who owns a real Number and knows how to use it.

### Act 3 - The Workshop Identity
- Family or father workshop becomes economically important.
- Duel Disk repair and mechanical literacy feed back into his dueling identity.
- Machine-type decks start replacing random extender shells.
- Machina and later Geargia move him from scramble to structure.
- Pivotal moment: the deck stops being random survival tech and becomes an extension of class identity and labor.

### Act 4 - The Iron Admiral
- Blackship becomes flagship rather than gimmick.
- The machine fleet metaphor stabilizes the whole era identity.
- School, local street reputation, and workshop legitimacy begin merging.
- The protagonist becomes known as more than a collector: he becomes a machine duelist with authority.
- Pivotal moment: he starts commanding a fleet, not just summoning monsters.

### Act 5 - WDC Legitimacy
- Enters the World Duel Carnival.
- Uses machine control and non-destruction pressure to beat flashier duelists.
- Gains public reputation and economic upgrade path.
- Beats Quattro or equivalent high-profile rivals.
- Pivotal moment: Heartland is forced to acknowledge that the mechanic's son belongs on the big stage.

### Act 6 - Ceiling Contact and Expansion
- Vetrix or higher-tier threats expose the limit of his current fleet.
- Barian escalation pushes the era beyond simple tournament prestige.
- Deck upgrades become city-defense or broader-control tools rather than only personal ladder-climbing.
- Pivotal moment: he stops being a local grinder with a cursed ace and becomes a city-level force.

### End State
- local poverty has been transformed into industrial power
- the family/workshop life survives and expands
- Blackship remains central, meaning the era ends not with purity, but with managed obsession

## Identity Ladder
- poor collector
- Number holder
- machine duelist
- The Iron Admiral
- higher-end fleet / sovereign-adjacent phase

## Core thematic turns
- poverty -> ownership
- ownership -> obsession
- obsession -> system-building
- system-building -> public legitimacy
- legitimacy -> expansion

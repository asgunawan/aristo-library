### 4. The Mechanic: Xyz Summoning (Exceed)
This mechanic kills "Levels" and introduces "Ranks."
*   **The Card Frame:** Black with stars flying from left to right.
*   **The Summon:** You take 2 (or more) monsters of the **SAME Level**.
*   Example: Two Level 4 Monsters.
*   You stack them on top of each other (Overlay).
*   You put the Xyz Monster on top.
*   **The Cost:** You don't "spend" the materials. They sit under the card as **Overlay Units (Materials)**.
*   To use an effect, you "detach" (send to grave) one unit.
*   *Resource Management:* You have limited ammo. Once you run out of units, the monster is usually vanilla.

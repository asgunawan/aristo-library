# Arc-V - The Commander

## Character Notes

### The Protagonist / The Commander
Role:
- instructor, organizer, and eventually war-system builder

Core traits:
- disciplined
- anti-spectacle
- class-conscious
- emotionally colder than earlier incarnations
- sees systems before people, but still believes systems can save people if forced hard enough

Narrative function:
- reinterprets Arc-V as a story about educational capital, organizational violence, and command structure
- turns deckbuilding into doctrine and doctrine into governance

### Gonta / Kenji / Yumi / Diesel / student cohort
Role:
- recruits, students, lieutenants, or proof of system viability

Narrative function:
- show how the Commander's logic affects real people
- convert abstract strategy into social organization
- make the era about training, loyalty, and replacement rather than only one-on-one glory

Important split from source:
- Gonta: the first major proof that slow, mocked kids can win if the field is shrunk and the doctrine fits them
- Kenji: over-thinker who becomes useful through repetition and correct loops
- Yumi: fear redirected into controlled obedience and trap discipline
- Diesel: later lieutenant / field-operator energy, more than just a student

### Mr. Tanaka / failing-school ownership figures
Role:
- remnants of the old school order

Narrative function:
- make the collapse visible and material
- show the difference between sentimental stewardship and ruthless institutional survival

### LDS / Reiji / corporate schooling power
Role:
- larger machine / dominant institution / talent extractor

Narrative function:
- force the protagonist to define himself against a polished, richer, more legitimate educational empire
- eventually recognize his value once he proves he can produce disciplined results from scraps

### Local gangs / slum actors / displaced students
Role:
- manpower pools, threat sources, and absorbed populations

Narrative function:
- transform the era from school story into colonization / insurgency story
- show the moral cost of turning logistics into power

### Nezumi
Role:
- corrupt supplier / compromised institutional middleman

Narrative function:
- crucial bridge between gated elite resources and the Commander's poor-school arsenal
- shows how the colonial / insurgency arc begins through corruption and procurement rather than destiny
- makes the Metalfoes transition feel materially earned and morally dirty

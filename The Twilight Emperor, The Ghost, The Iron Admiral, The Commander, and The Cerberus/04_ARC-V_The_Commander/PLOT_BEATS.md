# Arc-V - The Commander

## Plot Beats

### Act 1 - The Dying School
- Starts as an instructor or near-instructor figure inside a struggling small duel school / dojo.
- Faces a city where schools compete like businesses under corporate pressure.
- Watches poaching, underfunding, bad equipment, and elite school dominance hollow the institution out.
- Pivotal moment: realizes the school is not losing because its students are weak, but because the whole system is designed to absorb and kill small schools.

### Act 2 - Iron Statue / Pre-Metalfoes Doctrine
- Rejects Action Dueling glamour and acrobatic luck as the governing logic of play.
- Uses weaker anti-spectacle normal-monster / stun logic first to establish the school's philosophy.
- Recruits kids LDS rejected specifically because they do not fit the flashy system.
- Pivotal moment: stops trying to raise stars and starts building soldiers.

### Act 3 - Metal Will Rebrand
- Acquires defective, failed, or surplus Metalfoes material through corrupt supply lines.
- Rebrands the school into Metal Will.
- Introduces Metalfoes as a curriculum instead of a personal deck secret.
- Turns the poor school's deck problem into a doctrine advantage.
- Pivotal moment: Metalfoes stops being a deck and becomes the school's industrial language.

### Act 4 - Ranked Students and Formation Logic
- Gonta, Kenji, Yumi, Diesel, and others stop functioning as isolated kids and start functioning as ranked pieces of a command structure.
- The school survives anti-poaching pressure by becoming a phalanx rather than an ace factory.
- Deck logic expands from personal competence to group doctrine.
- Pivotal moment: education turns into command.

### Act 5 - Debt Trigger and City Deployment
- The early school success is still not enough to erase the debt.
- Nezumi tightens the contract: deed, labor, and future supply are all conditional.
- The Commander is sent into the Synchro Dimension through a smuggling route because Nezumi sees market expansion potential.
- Pivotal moment: the protagonist stops being a school defender and becomes a transported asset in someone else's supply chain.

### Act 6 - Colonization / Insurgency Logic
- Four people are not enough to survive the city, so conquest becomes the rational solution.
- The Commander defeats local gangs, offers them armed incorporation instead of annihilation, and converts their spaces into Metal Force cells.
- Scarcity, fear, and logistics become tools.
- The Commander hoards key resources, especially Tuners and Fusion access, and turns them into leverage.
- Children, gangs, and stragglers are integrated into a functioning machine.
- Pivotal moment: helping the desperate turns into occupying them.

### Act 7 - War Economy and Police Escalation
- Protection money and loot flow upward to the Commander.
- Debt payments and proof of success flow back to Nezumi.
- Nezumi returns more weapons: fusions, Tuner packages, counterfeit speed tools, and officer-grade upgrades.
- The deck evolves alongside the command structure.
- Metalfoes becomes full doctrine; later anti-summoning and reinforcement tech widen its use.
- Bounty systems, food distribution, and loyalty payments make the command network sustainable.
- Sector Security and Roget escalate because the operation now looks like an unauthorized military economy.
- Pivotal moment: the Commander is no longer reacting to war; he is producing a war-ready order.

### End State
- the school or dojo no longer functions as a normal educational space
- the protagonist becomes a war organizer, not just a teacher
- command, scarcity, and deck discipline merge into one identity

## Identity Ladder
- underpaid instructor
- systems coach
- Metal Will founder
- school rebuilder
- indebted expeditionary asset
- field commander
- colonial organizer / war-economy figure

## Core thematic turns
- underfunding -> bitterness
- bitterness -> doctrine
- doctrine -> organization
- organization -> coercion
- coercion -> command
- command -> colonization

### 1. The Dormitory System (The Caste System)
The most unique feature of Duel Academy is its hierarchy. Students are separated into three dorms based on their grades and dueling skills. These are named after the three Egyptian God Cards:

*   **Slifer Red (Osiris Red):**
*   **Rank:** The lowest rank.
*   **Who goes here:** Students with low grades, "drop-out" candidates, or those (like the main character Jaden Yuki) who scrape by in written exams despite having high practical skills.
*   **Conditions:** They live in a shabby, rundown dorm building. The food is terrible (often just plain rice and sardines), and the rooms are small and cramped.
*   **Ra Yellow:**
*   **Rank:** The middle rank.
*   **Who goes here:** Students who are skilled and have good grades but aren't quite at the elite level yet. This is often the default dorm for new students who score well on entrance exams (like Bastion Misawa).

*   **Rank:** The highest/elite rank.

### 2. The Curriculum
It functions like a real high school, but every subject is related to cards:
*   **Classroom Lessons:** They study "Duel Theory," which includes learning about card mechanics (Chain Links, Spell Speeds), history of famous duelists, and probability/statistics.
*   **Exams:** They have both written tests (which Jaden frequently sleeps through) and practical exams (where they must duel a teacher or another student).
*   **Physical Education:** They even have gym class, often involving drawing cards while running or stamina training to ensure they don't faint during long duels.

### 3. Progression and Promotion
*   **Entrance Exams:** To get in, you must pass a written test and a practical duel against an instructor.
*   **Promotion Matches:** A Slifer Red student can move up to Ra Yellow or Obelisk Blue if they get high enough grades and win a "promotion duel." However, many characters (like Jaden) choose to stay in Slifer Red because they prefer the laid-back atmosphere and their friends.
*   **Graduation:** At the end of the third year, students participate in graduation duels. The top student is honored, and graduates are scouted by card sponsors or Pro Leagues.

### 4. Campus Life
The academy is located on a remote volcanic island, so students are isolated from the rest of the world. The campus includes:
*   A card shop (where rich students buy the best packs and poor students get the leftovers).
*   A harbor/lighthouse.
*   An abandoned dorm (which is usually forbidden due to dark magic rumors).
*   A volcano (for dramatic duels).

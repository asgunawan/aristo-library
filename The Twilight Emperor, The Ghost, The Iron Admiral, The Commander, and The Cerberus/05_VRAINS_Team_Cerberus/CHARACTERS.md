# VRAINS - Team Cerberus

## Character Notes

### Team Cerberus
Role:
- three-part protagonist system rather than one conventional lead

Narrative function:
- lets the era distribute deck identity, labor, and personality instead of forcing one avatar to carry everything
- turns matchup choice and specialization into character structure

### The Hoarder
+Role:
+- eldest sibling / chaos-value specialist
+
+Deck identity:
+- Thunder Dragon / Grass / big-yard value logic
+
+Narrative function:
+- represents accumulation, overstock, and brute-force resource inevitability
+- keeps the team's oldest "pile" instinct alive in digital form
+
+### The Griefer
+Role:
+- middle sibling / humiliation and tempo specialist
+
+Deck identity:
+- early Prank-Kids idea gives way to SPYRAL once the timeline and power questions sharpen
+
+Narrative function:
+- represents the team's most socially sadistic and matchup-abusive face
+- makes the team feel hated rather than merely respected
+
+### The Architect
+Role:
+- youngest sibling / systems specialist
+
+Deck identity:
+- Orcust and related machine recursion logic
+
+Narrative function:
+- gives the team structural patience and end-board architecture
+- the one most comfortable turning trash code into working systems
+
+### Shared body / access constraint
+Role:
+- practical poverty limiter
+
+Narrative function:
+- one headset or one functional high-end access point keeps the team's early life desperate and interdependent
+- prevents the triplets from feeling overpowered too early
+
+### Playmaker / Revolver / larger VRAINS figures
+Role:
+- world-scale pressure and ceiling markers
+
+Narrative function:
+- remind the reader that Cerberus is powerful but not the center of cosmic destiny
+- help frame the team as survivors, contractors, and meta predators instead of prophesied heroes

### 2. The New Mechanic: Link Summoning
This is the mechanic that fundamentally changed the board layout.
*   **The Cards:** Dark Blue Hexagonal cards.
*   **The mechanic:**
*   They have **Link Arrows** pointing to specific zones.
*   **The Cost:** You send monsters equal to the "Link Rating" (Link-1, Link-2, etc.) to the grave.
*   **The Rule:** You must summon Extra Deck monsters (Fusion, Synchro, Xyz) to a zone a **Link Arrow** points to (or the Extra Monster Zone).
*   *Strategic Implication:* You can't just spam 5 Fusions anymore. You need to build a "Circuit" of Link Monsters to open up zones.

# VRAINS - Team Cerberus

## Plot Beats

### Act 1 - Broken Knee Start
- The triplets begin poor, under-equipped, and structurally incomplete.
- They share one real point of access to LINK VRAINS.
- Each deck is missing its true enabling pieces.
- Pivotal moment: the team realizes the real advantage is not any one deck, but the fact that three specialists can share one identity.

### Act 2 - Role Lock-In
- Eldest becomes the Hoarder.
- Middle becomes the Griefer.
- Youngest becomes the Architect.
- The team stops being "three poor duelists" and becomes a functional distributed dueling machine.
- Pivotal moment: specialization becomes stronger than general competence.

### Act 3 - Cerberus Shift
- The team uses shared entry / switching logic to choose who actually takes the duel.
- Outsiders start experiencing Cerberus as an impossible matchup-shifting urban legend.
- Information advantage becomes part of the deck philosophy itself.
- Pivotal moment: identity becomes a tactical resource.

### Act 4 - Data Economy Climb
- The team farms data shards, scavenges code, and takes low-end bounties.
- Missing key cards and programs are acquired step by step.
- The decks evolve from broken prototypes into coherent threat packages.
- Pivotal moment: the team starts compounding value instead of merely surviving match to match.

### Act 5 - Network Reputation
- Team Cerberus becomes known in VRAINS circles.
- Opponents begin preparing for one deck and meeting another.
- Their mystique grows because they seem to violate normal one-player expectations.
- Pivotal moment: they are no longer rats in the server room; they are an identifiable force in the network.

### Act 6 - Contract and War Pressure
- SOL, bounty systems, and larger VRAINS conflicts force the team into higher-risk engagements.
- Playmaker, Revolver, Blood Shepherd, Lightning, and larger system conflicts redefine the stakes.
- The triplets remain mercenary and meta-aware rather than heroic in the clean shonen sense.
- Pivotal moment: they discover that surviving the network is no longer enough; they must choose where to stand inside its wars.

### End State
- Team Cerberus rises from basement poverty to durable network power
- they become data brokers / high-level survivors rather than moral champions
- the final fantasy is not clean victory, but optimized persistence

## Identity Ladder
- basement rats
- specialist siblings
- Cerberus
- feared counterpick entity
- wealthy data operators

## Core thematic turns
- scarcity -> specialization
- specialization -> information advantage
- information advantage -> reputation
- reputation -> leverage
- leverage -> survival at scale

# 5D's - The Ghost

## Deck and Economy Notes

## Why 5D's is different from GX
GX was a school economy.
5D's is a survival economy.

That means:
- cards are harder to access
- transportation is a power system
- legitimacy requires hardware, not just wins
- your bike is as important as your deck
- the law is part of the meta

## Economic ladder

### 1. Satellite scarcity
- no real card shop economy
- scavenging and repair culture
- damaged cards are normal
- most resources are about food, shelter, parts, and chips

### 2. D-Wheel threshold
- the bike is the first major capital investment
- no bike means no real future in the city-side system
- chips, frames, engines, and control units matter as much as singles

### 3. Urban illegality
- once topside, the protagonist needs not just cards but a fake signal, a hidden garage, sleeves, clothes, and a presentable body
- economic rise is tied to camouflage

### 4. Reputation income
- bounty work and tournament invitations become the real sources of upward movement
- the deck must stay dangerous enough to win, but cheap enough to remain plausible for an outlaw

## Deck ladder

### 1. Chain Burn / Trash Punishment
Function:
- early Satellite survival
- punishes the speed and swarm of Synchro-era decks

Meaning:
- ugly adaptation
- no pride
- no field presence required

### 2. Bike Burn / Speed World abuse
Function:
- turns Riding Duel infrastructure itself into part of the burn plan

Meaning:
- the setting's own rules become a weapon
- the Ghost wins by abusing pace rather than board beauty

### 3. Disaster Burn / upgraded outlaw build
Function:
- city-capable anti-meta refinement
- uses stronger staples and cleaner timing

Meaning:
- the Ghost is no longer just surviving; he is specializing

### 4. Macro Monarch pivot
Function:
- anti-grave, anti-Synchro, anti-flow budget power deck

Meaning:
- carries the old Emperor logic into the faster era
- an ideological bridge from GX into 5D's

### 5. Luxury anti-meta / prestige phase
Function:
- later refinements, possible Gladiator Beast or prestige anti-meta options
- used when the Ghost is no longer just poor, but established

Meaning:
- outlaw taste becoming elite taste without losing the underlying cruelty

## Key economic theme
In 5D's, the story is strongest when the deck is never separable from transport, legality, and shelter.

The Ghost does not just upgrade cards.
He upgrades the conditions under which he is allowed to continue existing.

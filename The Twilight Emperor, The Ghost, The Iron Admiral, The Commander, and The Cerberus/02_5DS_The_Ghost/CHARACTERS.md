# 5D's - The Ghost

## Character Notes

### The Protagonist / Scrap Phantom / The Ghost
Role:
- outlaw anti-hero / slum survivor / technical predator

Core traits:
- practical
- materially paranoid
- anti-flashy
- highly adaptive
- willing to weaponize legality gaps, mechanics, and bad optics

Narrative function:
- reinterprets 5D's as a survival-economy story rather than only a Signer story
- stands outside the prophecy while profiting from the cracks in the system
- turns anti-meta play into urban identity

### Yusei Fudo
Role:
- heroic comparison point / ceiling contact

Core traits:
- stoic
- genius mechanic
- protagonist-coded
- structurally cleaner than the Ghost

Narrative function:
- represents the noble version of Satellite competence
- the Ghost is what survival looks like without chosen-hero framing

### Officer Trudge / Sector Security
Role:
- state pressure / legal violence / institutional contempt

Narrative function:
- transforms poverty into criminality
- gives the Ghost a state enemy rather than just rival duelists

### Blister / underworld mechanic figures
Role:
- fixer / legitimizer / technical upgrader

Narrative function:
- make the transition from raw survival to sustainable outlaw professionalism possible
- embody the half-legal infrastructure that the Ghost needs to continue existing

### Warden / Facility figures
Role:
- prison-state bridge into the main plot

Narrative function:
- convert arrest into narrative escalation instead of simple failure
- give the Ghost contacts, scars, and structural relevance

### Dark Signers / later supernatural enemies
Role:
- setting escalators

Narrative function:
- force the Ghost to matter in a world that is suddenly about gods, marks, and apocalypse
- prove he is not a prophecy hero, but still too dangerous to discard

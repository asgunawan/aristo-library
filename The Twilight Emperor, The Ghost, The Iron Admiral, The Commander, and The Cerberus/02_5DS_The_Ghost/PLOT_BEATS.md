# 5D's - The Ghost

## Plot Beats

### Act 1 - Scrap Survival
- Starts in Satellite as a garbage recycler / bottom-tier survivor.
- Learns GX-style static stall does not scale into Synchro-era speed.
- Builds cheap chain-burn / punishment logic out of damaged commons.
- Establishes the first identity: the Scrap Phantom.
- Pivotal moment: realizes the world is too fast for pride, so every card choice becomes about surviving acceleration.

### Act 2 - The Escape Machine
- Scavenges and assembles a functioning D-Wheel.
- Learns that the bike is not cosmetic but the actual passport out of Satellite.
- Attempts or completes the pipeline run into the city.
- Uses Riding Duel mechanics as a weapon rather than a burden.
- Pivotal moment: the D-Wheel changes him from trapped prey into a moving illegal threat.

### Act 3 - Illegal City Life
- Arrives in the underground / warehouse / black-market side of the city.
- Lives as an unregistered or semi-forged existence.
- Upgrades clothes, sleeves, housing, and signal camouflage.
- Begins functioning as a bounty-hunter / hustler under city rules.
- Identity shifts from Scrap Phantom toward The Ghost.
- Pivotal moment: he learns that in the city, invisibility is a profession.

### Act 4 - Capture, Mark, and Network
- Risk of arrest / Facility route becomes central.
- Marking, prison logic, or semi-carceral contact links him into bigger plot networks.
- Meets key underworld / mechanic / fixer figures such as Blister-type support.
- Gains access to better hardware, real upgrades, or legal camouflage.
- Pivotal moment: being caught does not end the story; it upgrades the scale of the game.

### Act 5 - Tournament Recognition
- Converts survival competence into a tournament-capable anti-meta strategy.
- Enters the Fortune Cup or equivalent major stage.
- Uses outsider tactics against cleaner, richer, protagonist-style decks.
- Becomes a known urban legend / anti-hero presence.
- Pivotal moment: the city is forced to see that a Satellite outlaw can compete in its own arenas.

### Act 6 - Supernatural War Utility
- Signer-level protagonists move into prophecy and dragon territory.
- The Ghost remains useful because he handles the ugly practical side of the war.
- Dark Signers and later threats transform his role from simple outlaw to battlefield specialist.
- His identity hardens around survival, denial, and anti-meta pragmatism.
- Pivotal moment: he stops being a random survivor and becomes an indispensable non-chosen asset.

### End State
- outlaw competence becomes professional identity
- the Ghost is no longer trash, but neither is he clean
- he survives by making illegality into a repeatable system

## Identity Ladder
- Scrap Phantom
- The Ghost

## Core thematic turns
- poverty -> improvisation
- improvisation -> escape
- escape -> illegality
- illegality -> camouflage
- camouflage -> reputation
- reputation -> utility in war

# WORLD BIBLE — FULL DRAFT

## THE NATURAL ORDER
Predation is fatal and has always been fatal. There is no natural recovery, no cycling, no respawn. A predator eats a human and that human is permanently gone. This has been true since the caveman era and human civilization evolved around it rather than against it.
The Persistence of Progress: While death is functionally "reset" by the Reformation System, the biological data of the individual is preserved. Transformation progress is never lost upon reformation. If a human was 40% through a metabolic shift toward becoming a predator, they return at 40%.
 Stat Decay: Physical regression and stat loss are decoupled from the death/reformation cycle. Stats only decay through nutrient starvation (failure to maintain the predator diet). Dying and being reconstructed does not weaken the subject; only failing to feed the transformation does.
In the natural pre-reformation state, the statistical odds of any individual being eaten on any given day were low, in most cases. The cultural nonchalance around predation is not bravery or cope. It is accurate probability assessment built up over thousands of years of civilization. If a predator gets you, that is tragic the way a freak accident is tragic. But most people go their entire lives without it happening to them directly, so ambient fear stays low and life proceeds normally. This nonchalance is specific to the 1,000:1 world. It does not survive the K-Squeeze intact.
Living in a high-density urban environment like NYC means accepting a higher baseline predation rate. New Yorkers do not claim the city is safe. They decided the tradeoff — jobs, culture, opportunity, energy — was worth the cost. Predation risk is simply priced into city life the same way crime rates, noise, and rent are. People in rural areas under the natural ratio look at NYC the same way rural populations have always looked at cities: "Why would you live like that?" New Yorkers answer the same way they always have.

## THE GOLDEN RATIO: 1,000:1
The natural, self-stabilizing balance of the world is approximately one Predator for every 1,000 Humans. This ratio held from the Paleolithic era through the modern day and is what allowed human civilization to develop at the same pace and complexity as our own world.
How it scaled through history:
The tribal and caveman era saw villages of roughly 1,000 people supporting one predator per settlement. Consumption ran at one to two humans per month, usually the elderly, sick, or socially willing. The predator functioned as an apex protector — keeping rival tribes and large animals away in exchange for the quiet social understanding that some people would be eaten. The role carried spiritual weight, treated as a necessary death figure rather than a monster.
By the medieval era, cities of 50,000 supported roughly 50 predators who had become the aristocracy. Feeding was formalized as a Blood Tax — structured, scheduled, and nearly bureaucratic. Consumption rose to three to four humans per month per predator as social infrastructure made management more efficient.
The industrial era saw cities of one million supporting roughly 1,000 predators. Urban anonymity enabled higher consumption frequency and institutional feeding infrastructure began to emerge.
In the modern era, NYC is the model city. A population of 8 million supports roughly 8,000 predators. Population density and constant migration inflow make the sustained consumption rate viable. A person disappearing in NYC is a missing persons file, not a community crisis. The city functions as a living organism that perpetually replenishes its human population through births and incoming migration.

## WHY ONLY 1 IN 1,000 PEOPLE BECOME A PREDATOR
This is not genetic. Anyone can become a predator in theory. Almost nobody does because of four compounding barriers that made it practically impossible in the natural world.
Barrier 0: The Psychological Line
Before any biology is relevant, a person has to eat someone. Not metaphorically. The majority of humans will never cross this line regardless of potential upside, the same way most people will not commit murder even when they could likely get away with it. This is not a legal barrier or a biological one. It is a fundamental identity threshold — I am not that person — and it holds for most of the population absolutely. This is the first and most effective barrier. Everything else only applies to the small fraction who have already crossed it.
Barrier 1: The Gradual Metabolic Spiral
Eating one person does not make you a predator. It makes you a human who ate a person, with no permanent transformation. The shift is gradual and cumulative — each consumption quietly logs in the body, producing small changes that are easy to rationalize and easy to dismiss. A little more energy. Better sleep. Joints that stopped aching. The decision to become a predator never actually happens as a single moment. It accumulates through small choices until the person looks back and realizes they are already past every moral threshold they had, and stopping now means they crossed every line and received nothing for it. This is the sunk cost spiral. It is psychologically the most dangerous part of the process because it never announces itself.
Every person has a genetic ceiling — a personal apex form their biology will grow toward and then plateau at. Growth is driven by both the quantity consumed and the caloric density of what is consumed. Predators are significantly more calorie-dense than baseline humans, meaning a predator who has consumed other predators can push past ceilings that a human-only diet cannot reach. This creates a quiet internal food chain within the predator class. The curve of growth also has diminishing returns — each subsequent consumption yields slightly less gain than the last, so growth naturally flattens without infinite scaling.
Barrier 2: The Maintenance Trap
Once the metabolic shift is underway, the body is no longer efficient on a normal human diet. It requires regular high-density human protein to maintain what it has built. Under-feeding does not cause immediate collapse — it causes slow, visible regression. The gains reverse. The body shrinks back toward baseline. Among predators this is deeply shameful, the equivalent of visible poverty. A regressing predator is someone who could not keep up.
This makes predation extremely expensive and time-consuming, particularly in a city like NYC where rent already consumes most of a person's income. A poor predator is a genuinely sad situation — caught between the cost of maintaining their transformation and the cost of existing in the city at all, forced into a slow dietary regression, watching everything they crossed ethical lines to build quietly disappear. The city already has homeless predators. They are slightly taller than they used to be, wearing clothes that used to fit differently, slowly shrinking back. They function as an ambient cautionary tale that no formal warning system could replicate as effectively.
The corporation solved the maintenance problem for those who can afford it through Maintenance Subscriptions — pre-arranged, legally clean, logistically handled feeding that removes the time cost entirely. Rich predators subscribe. Poor predators freelance and absorb all the chaos that comes with it. The structural symmetry is worth noting: poor humans go into reformation debt, poor predators go into maintenance debt. The corporation has a debt product calibrated for every position on the food chain.
Barrier 3: The Vulnerability Window
The moment a person begins ascending — the moment they become measurably mid-transformation — they become the most nutritionally interesting thing on the menu. A mid-transformation human is more calorie-dense and more metabolically rewarding than a baseline human. They are also not yet strong enough to defend themselves against an established predator.
This is not a strategic enforcement by the predator class to protect their food supply. It is simply appetite. A smaller, faster, tactical predator can take down a larger one if they are smart about it, which means size alone is not protection and established predators are not fully safe from each other either. The entire class exists in a state of low-grade tension. The ascending human just happens to be at peak vulnerability precisely when they have committed too far to turn back. Most aspirants quietly disappear in this window, which does more to maintain the 1,000:1 ratio than any formal enforcement ever did.
Why the Corporation dismantled all four barriers:
The corporation ran decades of normalized media and propaganda that eroded the psychological threshold. They sell Starter Packages — enough initial supply to trigger the metabolic spiral without being a warlord. They sell Maintenance Subscriptions that remove the running cost for those who can afford them. And the established predator class was bought out or absorbed rather than left to self-enforce, neutralizing the vulnerability window for corporation-sponsored ascension programs. The 1,000:1 ratio was not broken by violence or accident. It was purchased.

## THE K-SQUEEZE: THE REFORMATION INDUSTRY CONSEQUENCES
K-Squeeze is a consequence of the Reformation Center's existence, not a condition that preceded it. When the corporate ratio shift pushes from 1,000:1 toward 100:1, the city does not collapse ecologically. The human population does not go extinct. What happens is more insidious: humans become overwhelmingly reliant on Reformation Centers, and the Centers become the most powerful financial institutions in the city.
At 80,000 predators sustaining a consumption rate of roughly 520 humans per year each, the city processes approximately 41.6 million consumption events annually against a population of 8 million. The average human is consumed and reformed roughly five times per year. This is no longer predation as a background fact of life. This is an industrial cycle.
At street level, the old nonchalance — statistically comparable to a car accident — is gone. Witnessing an incident on the way to work is unremarkable. The new nonchalance is not calm. It is survival exhaustion. People still do not panic, but only because panicking every day is not sustainable. The city runs on a low hum of managed dread.
People now wear High-Visibility Mementos — oversized jewelry, neon DNA tags, corporate-registered ID chips — as a signal to any predator that they are worth the paperwork. The street is more chaotic not because of violence spikes but because of the bureaucratic friction of constant death and recovery saturating every institution.
The Reformation Centers do not want humans to go extinct. A dead human generates one payment. A human reformed five times a year generates five payments, rising insurance premiums, compounding debt, and potential enrollment in the Work Repayment Program. The corporation's product is not reformation. Its product is subscription to continued existence.

## THE REFORMATION SYSTEM
What Reformation Is: A post-death reconstruction technology. The subject dies first. Given the right conditions they are brought back. It did not exist naturally. It is an industrial invention that changed the world the way antibiotics did — quietly, then all at once.
The Memento Rule: A human can only be reformed if a recoverable anchor exists. While the Orphanage Badge is the default starting anchor, any stable biological sample (DNA, hair) or high-use personal item suffices.
Predator Authority: The predator who consumes the prey holds absolute authority over whether reformation occurs. Even if the prey is fully insured, the predator must "release" the memento or trigger the process. This makes reformation a tool of social and political leverage. A predator may choose to deliberately destroy a memento (either after or before “releasing” it) as a "power move," resulting in permanent death (Game Over). There is no legal obligation (yet) to reform prey. Most predators in the natural era did not bother. Reformation is an act of affection, obligation, or financial arrangement.
Cost Scaling: Cost scales with the size and complexity of the person being reconstructed. Larger, older, and healthier humans cost more to reform. Functionally this means the poor are cheaper to eat and cheaper to abandon.
Who Pays: The prey pays. The cultural framing sold by the industry positions reformability as a privilege — the technology exists, you should be grateful, personal responsibility covers the rest.
Asset Protection: A consumed human's assets are frozen pending reformation or a statutory waiting period. They do not transfer to the predator. This exists to legally close off predation as a wealth extraction tool, though enforcement is imperfect and loopholes exist.
The Insurance Model:
Premiums: A weekly flat fee required to keep a policy active.
Deductibles: A fixed fee paid upon death to trigger the reconstruction.
Uninsured Costs: Without insurance, reformation requires a full, scaled market-rate payment based on the subject's complexity.
The Orphanage Head’s Cut: For wards of the state, the Orphanage Head acts as the legal guardian of the memento. They will forcibly reform a player if the player has assets to seize. If the player is penniless and their memento is destroyed or abandoned, it results in a permanent Game Over.
Debt Options: Credit (reformed, you owe the debt) or the Work Repayment Program (structured labor to pay it off over time).
The Protected Debtor Rule: A human enrolled in the Work Repayment Program is legally classified as a Protected Debtor. Predators are barred from consuming them — not out of moral concern, but because consuming someone mid-repayment creates an unresolvable financial liability the government refuses to absorb. The protection is narrow and bureaucratic. Predators who violate it face fines, not imprisonment. Whether the fine is worth it is a personal calculation.
The Unsaid Thing: If you have no anchor, no insurance, no assets, and no one who cares enough to hold your memento, no one will reform you even if they technically could. The system is available to everyone. In practice it is available to people who planned ahead, which means people who could afford to.

## DIGESTION MECHANICS
Oral — Standard
The stomach receives prey into an initially inert environment — warm, dark, tight, no acid yet. This phase lasts roughly twenty to forty minutes and represents the primary dramatic and reformation window. The prey is fully conscious and aware of the situation. Whether that awareness is experienced as terror or as something else depends entirely on the individuals involved and the circumstances of the consumption.
Acid production begins after the initial phase. Once active, the process becomes irreversible without external intervention. Full digestion completes in approximately two hours for an unwilling prey. An unwilling prey is physically struggling, producing stress responses, and the predator's body accelerates through the process accordingly. That struggle is not just atmospheric — it is the operative condition of Phase 2, where the prey's stats are tested directly against the predator's internal environment for as long as either the prey's stamina or the window holds.
A willing prey extends the timeline significantly. The predator's body is responsive to the psychological and emotional context of the situation — a relaxed, consenting process allows the predator to consciously slow digestion. Average duration for a willing prey is approximately eight hours, with the outer limit approaching twenty-four. This is not a separate biological system. It is the same system running under fundamentally different conditions.
Other Methods: Anal, unbirth, cock vore, and breast vore each have distinct mechanical properties and survival window profiles. These will be detailed separately in a later section.
Willingness System — Base rate is 5/95 willing/unwilling in the pre-reformation world, shifting to approximately 30/70 post-reformation as cultural attitudes change generationally. Willingness is also individual and situational, modified by allure/seduction stats and charisma stats. Stomach passenger state matters — a willing prey in stomach creates social proof that shifts nearby NPC willingness upward, an unwilling prey signals danger and shifts it downward.
Player Agency: The player is not a passive participant. As prey, the player can actively request to be eaten (Seduction/Submission) or attempt to force an unwilling predator into the act via a physical or psychological Stat Check.
Predator Nature: Every "Named NPC" has a Nature Stat (1-5) representing their willingness to consume or interact.
1: Highly Reluctant/Principled.
5: Gluttonous/Aggressive.
Readability: A predator’s Nature is visible to the player through Intelligence, Dialogue, or specific Skill Checks.
The Knockout Mechanic: If a player annoys or harasses an unwilling predator, the predator may choose to knock the player out in self-defense. The outcome of being unconscious is determined by a dice roll modified by the predator’s Nature—ranging from being left on the street to being consumed while "to-go."
Classes on Willingness — Willing skews toward the rich (insurance, lifestyle choice), the genuinely devoted (psychological disposition, exists at every income level), and the desperate poor (nothing left to lose). Unwilling skews toward middle class and working poor who have just enough to lose that survival instinct dominates. But this is probably only for worldbuilding logic and does not apply on game logic (which probably would just be a slider?)
Workplace Predation Logic — Predator employers operate on rational economic self-interest. Top performers are structurally protected not by law but by their replacement cost. Interns and underperformers are structurally exposed. The board functions as a higher predator that holds predator bosses accountable for poor performance, including eating valuable assets. Performance is literal survival currency.
Antacid Pills and Packets — Pills require manual use and sufficient motor control and space to activate. Deep stomach position, tight compression, or multiple prey prevent use. They delay the next acid cycle rather than neutralizing digestion permanently. Packets auto-rupture at a compression threshold, buying a shorter automatic window without player control over timing. Both are time-buying mechanics, not escape mechanics — they extend the window during which a predator could choose to release prey or external intervention could occur.
## STATS SYSTEM
The game uses six primary stats. Four are active roll stats (STR, AGI, CHA, INT), while two represent the character's "tank" (END, VIT).
Strength (STR): Raw physical force. Used in Phase 1 to secure a grab or break a hold, and in Phase 2 to physically force an escape.
Agility (AGI): Speed and reflexes. Functions as the initiative tiebreaker and governs the "Chase" portion of Phase 1. Works in Phase 2 by making escape more effective and less stamina intensive.
Charisma (CHA): Social force and psychological pressure. CHA is not a separate "win condition" but an inevitability mechanic. It acts as a cumulative debuff; a character with superior CHA erodes the opponent's other stats over time. If a character’s effective stats are pushed into the negative by CHA pressure, they "naturally" lose their rolls, making their submission or victory inevitable.
Seduction (SED)
Seduction is a dedicated stat separate from CHA. Where CHA operates through psychological dominance — eroding the opponent's will and competence — Seduction works on an involuntary physical register. It does not debuff stats. Its sole output is the Arousal meter.
Intelligence (INT): Tactical awareness. INT provides a bonus to other stat rolls. It is done by having a 2 round preparation phase (mark), then the execution phase which amplifies your other stat. This bonus scales based on the gap between your INT and the stat being amplified, rewarding tactical specialization.
Endurance (END): Your stamina pool. Every lost stat check or physical exertion drains END. It is the primary buffer in combat for both phase 1 and phase 2.
Vitality (VIT): Your physical integrity (HP), Nausea, and Arousal Threshold. The digestion process begins draining VIT directly in phase 2. If it reaches 0, you die. Acid, STR, and AGI checks acts as the main way of reducing vitality for prey. For threshold stats, it be clarified further in the “Nausea” or “Arousal” section
Luck: A hidden stat functioning as a crit modifier.
CHA vs. Seduction — The Distinction:
 CHA dialogue is about power: dominance, futility, psychological pressure. Seduction dialogue is about desire: the body betraying the mind, involuntary response, want rather than fear. A character can be high CHA and low Seduction — cold, commanding, terrifying — or high Seduction and low CHA — disarming, magnetic, impossible to focus against. They are different archetypes with different combat roles and should feel different in prose.
## COMBAT SYSTEM
The combat system is a text-based autobattler where outcomes are determined by tiered success levels and attrition.
#### Phase 1: The Outside (The Encounter)
Each round resolves in a fixed order:  INT → CHA → SED → AGI → STR
INT (Mark/Execute Pipeline):
Each round, INT will spend one turn to mark the target. The following round, every stat rolls (CHA, SED, AGI, STR) are amplified, and a new mark can be set in the same round the execution fires. Once the cycle is running, it looks like this:
Round 1: Mark A
Round 2: Mark B, Execute A
Round 3: Mark C, Execute B
And so on.
Amplification scales with the gap between INT and the targeted stat, rewarding characters who use INT to compensate for weaker stats rather than stack on top of already strong ones.
CHA: Resolves every round without setup. Applies a cumulative debuff to the opponent's STR, AGI, and INT. It never pauses.

SED: Resolves after CHA in the round order without setup. It is a direct Seduction vs. Seduction contest. A successful check ticks the target's Arousal meter upward. The gap between attacker and defender's Seduction determines the size of the tick.
1. The Chase (AGI vs. AGI):
Evaded: Clean dodge. Light END penalty to Predator.
Grazed: Briefly caught, pulled loose. Light END penalty to prey.
Seized: Grabbed but wrenched free. Heavy END penalty to prey.
Caught: Full grab secured. Proceeds to The Secure.
2. The Secure (STR vs. STR):
Escaped: Hold broken. Significant END penalty to Predator. Resets to The Chase.
Defiant: Prey wrenches free under hard resistance. Heavy END penalty to prey. Resets to The Chase.
Dragged: Prey nearly overwhelmed but recovers. Moderate END penalty to prey. Resets to The Chase.
Overwhelmed: Prey fully subdued. Enters Phase 2.
The Rule: Phase 2 only occurs on Caught → Overwhelmed. Everything else resets to The Chase.
#### Phase 2: The Inside (The Struggle)
Once inside, the struggle becomes a war of attrition. Each round involves simultaneous stat checks across INT → CHA → AGI → STR
Asymmetric Drain: Failed struggle attempts drain the prey's END significantly more than the predator's. Conversely, sustained containment effort drains the predator's END more slowly but consistently.
VIT Drain: VIT begins draining once phase 2 starts. Acid acts as a pressure escalator — it will deal progressively higher damage the longer Phase 2 goes. Higher acid pressure means faster VIT loss. STR and AGI checks in Phase 2 can also directly reduce VIT. VIT reaching zero is always the kill condition.
### Arousal
The Arousal Meter
For the prey, Arousal is a debuff meter representing loss of bodily control, not a resource. It climbs toward 100% and cannot be spent or managed — only endured, at 100%, the prey is stunned.
Stun in Phase 1: The pred/prey is stunned and will automatically fail all of their checks. Arousal does not reset to 0. Each stun resets to a higher floor than the last — 60%, then 70%, then 80%, then 90%. By the fourth stun, three consecutive successful Seduction checks will re-trigger it. A prey who cannot contest Seduction is on a closing loop.
Stun in Phase 2: The prey simply loses their round of checks. No additional mechanical effect — being inside the stomach is already a losing position, and the Seduction path into Phase 2 represents a prey whose resistance was psychologically dismantled before their body gave out.
For the predator and prey, Arousal will accumulate passively during Phase 2 as a byproduct of active digestion. The prey/pred however, will still seduce each other to increase each other’s arousal. Once its full, it will first add a significant chunk to the nausea meter (which will eject the prey if it bypasses the limit) then deals a direct VIT hit to the prey through an involuntary acid spike. The pred is not in control of the surge so much as releasing it. It is empowering and destabilizing at once.
Prey Seducing the Pred from Inside:
 A prey inside the stomach can still make Seduction checks against the predator. Landing them accelerates the pred's passive Arousal buildup, pushing them toward a surge faster. This is a deliberate gamble: a surge means a Nausea chunk that benefits the prey's escape odds, but the accompanying acid spike means the prey also takes direct VIT damage. The prey is betting that the pred's Nausea threshold will break before their own VIT does. Fighting passively is safer in the short term but closes the escape window over time — the same logic as the existing Nausea system.
When the pred's Arousal hits 100%, the resolution branches based on how it got there:
Passive buildup (prey landed no SED checks): surge fires as normal. Nausea chunk, then acid spike. No branch.
Active buildup (prey landed at least one SED check): at the peak moment, the prey gets a single closing SED check.
Pass: The pred releases the prey voluntarily. No acid spike. No Nausea chunk. The prey initiates Phase 2 immediately with roles reversed — the pred enters as the swallowed party.
Fail: Surge fires as normal. Nausea chunk, then acid spike. The gamble is lost.
The closing check is a single high-stakes roll, not a campaign. The investment was every SED check that got the pred to 100% — the close is one moment. Missing it means all that accumulated acid exposure paid off nothing.
### Nausea
Nausea is a hidden meter that builds on the pred's side during Phase 2. The prey does not fill it directly — it rises as a byproduct of successful stat checks. Every round the prey lands a meaningful STR or AGI check, Nausea ticks upward. INT checks can accelerate the rate. CHA checks from the prey can also contribute, representing psychological discomfort rather than physical resistance.
The Threshold is set by the prey's VIT at the start of Phase 2. Higher VIT means a higher threshold — the pred's body simply handles the resistance better and takes longer to be forced into rejection. Acid pressure works against this by actively draining VIT each round, which lowers the remaining threshold ceiling as the fight goes on. This means a prey who fights passively is not just losing HP — they are also making their own escape harder.
On Threshold Reached: The pred involuntarily expels the prey. The prey exits Phase 2 with heavily degraded END and whatever VIT remains after acid exposure. The pred enters a Post-Eject debuff lasting 3 rounds — STR, AGI, and CHA rolls are all penalized, and END takes an immediate hit from the heave. The fight can resume from Phase 1, but the window is real.

Simultaneous Resolution & Attrition
All four stats are compared at once each round. Most rounds are a split — both parties land something, both parties lose something. A fully clean round in either direction is rare.

## GAMEPLAY STRUCTURE
The game is a DOL-style stat-raising sandbox set in NYC. The core loop is free-roaming time progression across Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, and Night phases, with most named NPCs operating on schedules the player can learn and exploit. Stats govern nearly every interaction — what you can do, what gets done to you, and how much of either you survive. Encounters follow a two-phase structure: Phase 1 plays out in the open world, Phase 2 plays out inside the predator. The full mechanical detail for both phases lives in the COMBAT SYSTEM section.
Save and Reload System: The game autosaves at the start of each day. A permanent game over does not force a reload — it offers one. The player can choose to reload from the day's opening save, or accept the outcome and continue forward with the consequences. Consequences include reformation debt, enrollment in the Work Repayment Program, or finding themselves in the custody of whoever consumed them, dragged through their predator's daily routine until the situation resolves. Reloading is always available. It is never required. This is a deliberate design choice: some outcomes are worth living through.
Early Game: Approximately the first 30 days. The Reformation Center does not exist yet. Death during this window is handled purely through the autosave system — reload or consequences, no institutional safety net. The player is a ward of a for-profit NYC orphanage operating as a housing arrangement with a school component. Rent is owed. No reformation coverage is provided. No institutional protection exists. Wards are the most structurally exposed demographic in the city and the game does not soften this.
Mid Game: The Reformation Center opens as a milestone unlocked through a combination of elapsed time and player-influenced conditions. The player can work at the branch to accelerate its development, which also builds their own position within it. The Center's opening changes the city's economic texture around the player — insurance becomes available, the debt system activates, and predation starts functioning as an industrial cycle rather than a background risk.
Corruption Mechanic: A background variable that tracks the pace of the world's shift from the 1,000:1 natural ratio toward the K-Squeeze. Player actions can slow or accelerate both their own the player's own position on the human-to-predator spectrum and the broader city-level shift. High personal corruption opens options. High city corruption raises ambient danger. Both run simultaneously and interact. Basically, the player experience their corruption by looking at themself, such as their own transformation, traits, size, traits, etc and they view the city’s corruption by experiencing their life in the city.
Late Game and Win Condition: The long arc is orphan to demigod — the player begins with nothing and can end the game as CEO of the NYC Reformation Center branch, the single most powerful financial and institutional position in the city. Reaching this does not end the game. It changes what the game is. Post-win, the primary threat shifts from street-level predation to elite-tier predators for whom the player is now the most nutritionally and politically interesting thing in the room. The hunter becomes the most wanted prey at the top of the food chain.
NPC Structure: Named NPCs are the only characters capable of sustained relationships, alliances, rivalries, and narrative weight. Generic NPCs populate the economic and predation ecosystem. Predator nature among named NPCs runs on a 1–5 scale and is readable through Intelligence, Dialogue, and skill checks.
FLUFF / BRAINSTORM DUMP — TO BE SORTED
Regressing Predators
 Homeless predators are a visible cautionary tale on NYC streets — wearing clothes that used to fit differently, slowly losing the size they earned. They exist in a genuine danger spiral: still calorie-dense enough to be appetizing to established predators, but weak enough to be easy targets. Other regressing predators in the same sinking boat may consume them out of desperation, which is widely considered the more undignified ending. The alternative — full regression back to baseline human — leaves a person with a normal body and a very abnormal memory. No social category exists for former predators. The Reformation Center is technically available to them but reformation is unlikely in practice since no one has legal obligation or financial incentive to hold or release their memento.
The Asset Law Arc
 Original asset freeze law was well-intentioned robbery prevention. Accidentally made reformation economically pointless for predators. Reformation industry patched this with subsidized bonuses, which made them bleed money. Solution was to lobby for the repeal, rerouting payment from prey's own unfrozen assets instead. Industry stops losing money, predators get compensated, reformation becomes accessible. Asset transfer through predation is now legal again, dressed in bureaucratic legitimacy.
The CEO Trap
 Reinstating the asset freeze law is technically possible from the CEO chair. The board will show you the math neutrally and accurately. The only subsidy available is your own net worth. The game offers no correct answer, just a mirror.
Mid-Process Reversal
 Not illegal, court would likely side with predator. You made the leap voluntarily. Legal exposure minimal, social awkwardness is the bigger consequence.
Memento
 Lore answer: whatever holds the most sentimental value or has been carried longest. Common case is a wallet. For an orphan with nothing, it's likely something small and weird that survived every chapter of their life because it had no monetary value. Natural storytelling hook.
CEO as Prestige State
 Not an ending, a sandbox prestige state. Early game threats are physical. Mid game social and economic. Late game institutional and political. The board is the late game version of street danger.
Family/Inheritance
 Far future content. Marriage and kids are mechanical decisions as much as personal ones post-asset law repeal. Named NPC spouses have their own stat profiles and place in the food chain.
Orphan
 Deliberately vague, self-insert, Degrees of Lewdity approach.
Digestion Timeline Overhaul
 Three modes, all predator-controlled. Accelerated (combat state — fast neutralization, costs predator END, leaves them sluggish after). Passive (default 6–8 hour overnight rhythm, no effort required). Slowed (willing scenario, 24–48 hours, requires active suppression from the predator). "I'm staying at someone's place this weekend" has a specific meaning in this world.
VIT = 0 means Neutralized, not Dead
 Prey is helpless and contained but still alive and conscious. Actual death happens somewhere during digestion after neutralization. Pred is free to move around, walk on the street, triggering flavor texts. Or do whatever.
Reformation — Corrected
Reformation only triggers after full digestion. Memento is whatever survives and gets expelled naturally. No intermediate window, no interrupting the process. If a predator has second thoughts, the only option is releasing the prey alive before completion. Once fully digested, the prey is dead and the memento is the only path back — pending someone choosing to use it.
Memento Origin
The memento isn't pre-designated. It's whatever naturally survives digestion — wallet, phone, shoes, ring, anything indigestible. Reformation technology was built around this biological reality after scientists noticed remnants retained enough imprint to reconstruct from.
Memento Destruction Variants
 Destroying it inside: quiet, intimate, internal, potentially witnessed only by the prey. Spitting it out and stomping it: performed, theatrical, meant to be seen. Both are permanent death but communicate completely different things about the predator.

Prey is mechanically neutralized but flavor text still describes futile struggling — predator is casually indifferent because they already won. Tick system runs in background on every passage transition, surfaces as ambient flavor text.
Stat Bleed Into Foreground
 Prey still actively fighting bleeds into real life through stat debuffs at inconvenient moments. Not a survival threat, just a lifestyle consequence. Scales with stakes — coffee shop is funny and low stakes, surgery or court testimony is serious. The humor and drama both come from the predator making a bad scheduling decision.
Endurance Persistence
 END persists across all activities — combat, work, social, and background digestion. Carrying an active prey passively drains END throughout the day, compounding with everything else the player does. Rest mechanic and bed/home quality becomes the central recovery loop, same philosophy as DOL's bed upgrade. Prey VIT and player END run parallel but need careful tuning to avoid punishing snowball. The persistent END system is what gives every daily scheduling decision real weight.

PREY SAFETY ARC
The actual safety curve is counterintuitive. Prey are most endangered during SATURATION through "FLOURISHING" — peak propaganda, social permission fully constructed, no competing economic logic yet. Once INCENTIVE launches and especially into K-SHAPED SQUEEZE, prey become economically irrelevant because eating a pred gives stat growth, caloric value, competitive elimination, AND the branch payout simultaneously. Eating a prey is only rational if desperate, cocky, or completely unobserved. Prey safety during K-SHAPED SQUEEZE is real but deeply wrong-feeling — they're not protected, they're just not worth the trouble.
The branch takes credit for prey safety during its most predatory stage. They didn't reduce predation, they redirected it upward. Prey are safe the same way a dollar bill is safe in a room full of people fighting over hundreds.

THE INCENTIVE PROGRAM
Not altruistic. The branch looked at their intake numbers and realized preds were eating people without bringing anyone in, generating zero revenue. The incentive is a customer acquisition solution. The prey surviving is a byproduct of a business pipeline problem. Their public messaging is framed entirely in the language of safety and coexistence while the internal logic is purely transactional.
Exposure doesn't work because the branch operates in a world where the social contract around eating people is actively being rewritten. Any exposure either bounces off or becomes free advertising. The CEO takeover is the only tool that actually touches the machine — you can't shame it or starve it of legitimacy. You have to get inside it and change what it optimizes for.

THE ASSET FREEZE LAW
When a pred eats a prey, the prey's assets freeze and go to the government — closing the robbery loophole entirely. Once the prey is reformed, the assets unfreeze and the prey pays the branch fee out of their own returned money. The prey funds their own rescue from their own frozen account. The branch is self-liquidating and needs no outside revenue source.
Game over is not death. Game over is a pred eating you and deciding the reformation fee isn't worth their time. Your assets stay frozen in government indefinitely.

THE LOBBYING-OUT STRATEGY
Technically possible if the player has more money than the CEO without working with them. But it's a spending race the player structurally cannot win long-term — the branch's revenue compounds with every reformation processed while the player's income is individually capped. It's best understood as buying time toward the CEO takeover, not a standalone winning condition. Holding through LOBBYING to delay NO STEP BACK is plausible, but K-SHAPED SQUEEZE is where the math overtakes you if you haven't moved on the CEO track already.

## Core premise

A dark speculative setting where human society has always coexisted with predators that literally consume people, but a newer reformation industry turns that long-standing fact of life into an industrial, debt-driven system.

The world is built around two linked ideas:
- predation used to exist as a grim but relatively stable background condition
- the rise of reformation technology and private incentives transforms that balance into something more commercial, bureaucratic, and socially corrosive

## Best short description

A predation-based dystopian world where being eaten is not always the end, but surviving it can trap people inside systems of insurance, debt, status, and institutional control.

## What feels most central right now

### 1. The world logic

The strongest part of the project is the large-scale social logic:
- a natural predator-to-human ratio once kept things relatively stable
- predators are rare enough to coexist with civilization
- becoming a predator is possible but difficult, gradual, and socially costly
- modern institutions begin to commercialize that relationship and distort the old balance

### 2. Reformation as industry

Reformation is one of the main anchors of the setting.

General function:
- death is not always final if a usable "memento" or anchor survives
- recovery is expensive, conditional, and controlled by institutions and power relationships
- the system creates insurance models, debt pipelines, and incentives that reshape everyday life

This pushes the setting away from simple monster horror and into a predatory political economy.

### 3. The K-Squeeze direction

A major setting shift is the move from a relatively stable older order toward a much more saturated and exploitative one.

The rough idea is:
- predators become more numerous and more normalized
- reformation centers and related corporations profit from repeated consumption / recovery cycles
- the city becomes more bureaucratically predatory rather than merely physically dangerous

This seems to be one of the project's clearest thematic cores.

### 4. Game / sandbox framing

The markdown is not just a story bible. It also strongly frames the project as a game-like sandbox, especially in a modern NYC setting.

Broad structure present in the file:
- player starts vulnerable and poor
- early game is survival-heavy
- mid game involves reformation infrastructure and city corruption rising
- late game shifts toward institutional power, with the player potentially becoming part of the system they were navigating

This upward arc from exposed victim to elite operator appears to matter a lot to the current shape of the project.

## Tone and identity

The project reads as:
- bleak and systems-heavy
- more interested in social logic than shock value alone
- focused on exploitation, incentives, and status hierarchies
- willing to merge horror, bureaucratic satire, and sandbox mechanics

## Distinctive strengths

- strong macro-level worldbuilding logic
- clear interest in how institutions reshape taboo or violent realities
- memorable use of ratios, thresholds, and city-level change
- good tension between street-level danger and boardroom-level optimization

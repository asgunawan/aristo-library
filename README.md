# Aristo Library

A personal all-in-one creative writing library.

This repository stores story projects, worldbuilding notes, and brainstorm-heavy drafts. Most projects keep both source and working formats:
- `.docx` for original writing sessions
- `.md` for easier review, searching, and iterative editing

## Project Overview

### Psychic Revolution and its Consequences
Alt-history WW2 concept where rare psychics emerge through early neuroscience and vacuum-tube era experimentation.

Focus areas:
- military doctrine and logistics around psychic warfare
- grounded constraints (ramp-up, calorie burn, static jammers)
- faction-level arms race and geopolitical escalation

Folder:
- `Psychic Revolution and its Consequences/`

### THE Magical Revolution and Its Consequences
Flagship fantasy-tech worldbuilding project centered on mana corruption, the Crown, golem evolution, and civilization-scale consequences.

Focus areas:
- world bible and systems-first lore
- scenario branches (tragic conquest vs reluctant leadership)
- political, economic, and moral fallout of forced modernization

Related files in this project:
- `THE Magical Revolution and Its Consequences/TMRAIC World Bible.txt`
- `THE Magical Revolution and Its Consequences/TMRAIC quotes/`

Folder:
- `THE Magical Revolution and Its Consequences/`

### Inconsequence of The Magical Revolution
Science-fantasy brainstorming set in an interplanetary future where magic appears during active space colonization.

Focus areas:
- collision between mature technology and newly discovered magic
- social inertia and institutional response
- character-first implications of a world-changing anomaly

Folder:
- `Inconsequence of The Magical Revolution/`

### One Atom To The Left
Philosophical fantasy concept about pact-based survival, timeline rerolls, and identity erosion across altered realities.

Focus areas:
- causal reroll mechanics and anchor points
- memory persistence vs world inconsistency
- escalating stakes as more people are bound into the system

Folder:
- `One Atom To The Left/`

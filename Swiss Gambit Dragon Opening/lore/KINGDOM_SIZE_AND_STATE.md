# Kingdom Size And State

Brief reference for the kingdom and the wider fantasy world. Kept deliberately short: this is a consistency anchor, not a setting bible. Promote an item from "open" to "locked" only when a scene forces it.

## Scale And Governance

- roughly "Germany-sized" / HRE-ish — a major continental power, one crown over many semi-autonomous provinces and marches
- loose governance, not modern-centralized: a king who cannot micromanage, frontier lords with real autonomy
- why the scale matters: a loosely-governed big kingdom is exactly the kind that has frontier problems, a king who cannot be everywhere, and a genuine need for a "systems-logic" coordinator like Colin — that is why he is valuable without being a wizard-king

## Mana And Magic

- baseline mana is the DEFAULT everywhere. Earth is the anomaly — the only true dead zone. There are no natural mana-poor regions; mana is only missing where someone deliberately suppresses it (e.g., a dungeon whose maker keeps it sealed)
- magic users ≈ 5-10% of humans, but tiered:
  - ~5-10% have the SPARK — can sense mana, light a candle, a weak cantrip (the broad upper-class pool)
  - ~1-2% are trained CASTERS — actually useful: mages, healers, paladins
  - a HANDFUL per generation are elites — the singularity mage, an arch-healer, a high paladin
- magic is BOTH heritable AND aptitude-based, not discounted on either axis:
  - the spark runs partly in bloodlines (why noble houses exist)
  - it also shows up randomly in commoners (why a kid from nowhere can be a hero, and why wasted potential exists)
  - training is expensive, so training filters who actually becomes a caster — casterdom skews noble even though the spark does not
- the "singularity" mage is a premiere-scientist figure, rare even among casters

## The Strategic Resource (resource curse)

- the kingdom is the biggest human power NOT because of gold (gold is a bonus) but because of its MANA-CRYSTAL MINES — the concentration of mana crystals
- crystals are the oil/uranium of this world: stored mana, the strategic prize
- this is a resource curse: the kingdom is powerful because of the crystals and targeted because of them. Rival powers covet the mines, which is the material motive behind the coalition that forms against it
- Colin's Earth-side fuel (crystals) is this same resource — which is why his carry-load of crystals matters and why the kingdom treats gold as incidental ("50 or 100 kg, double production?" is a rounding error to them)

## The Wider World (the coalition against the kingdom)

- the kingdom is the most powerful — and most feared — human nation. Colin's systems-logic modernization made a united humanity terrifying to everyone else
- the threat is a MIXED ALLIANCE, not a single rival: elves, dwarves, orcs, a rival human faction (the "Iron Ring"), and demons loosely in the picture
- the rival human faction (e.g., an "Alaric Empire" type) believes they should lead humanity and resents the kingdom's primacy; the elven and other non-human powers fear a united humanity
- demons: post-demon-lord-war, demon remnants are scattered. Their relationship to the coalition is "neutral-with-them" — not fighting for the coalition, but not enemies either. HOW KNOWN this demon involvement is = RUMORED / SUSPECTED, not confirmed (see below; this is a plot lever, not a settled fact)

## The Church (supranational)

- supranational institution, medieval-Catholic model: present in every kingdom, its own sovereign, its own hierarchy, congregations on both sides of any war
- a HUMAN institution with a strong anti-demon doctrine
- the healer and paladin are Church EMISSARIES — their vows bind them to the Church, not to the crown. They fought the demon-lord war at the kingdom's side because demons are an existential threat the Church blesses fighting. A rival HUMAN/non-human coalition is a different matter

### The Church's war-posture — OPEN (resolved in Callback 3)

When the coalition declares war, the Church is pulled three ways at once:

- HUMAN SOLIDARITY — the biggest human power is being cut down by non-humans; the Church is a human institution
- SUPRANATIONAL NEUTRALITY — a rival human kingdom is in the coalition, and the Church has flocks on both sides
- ANTI-DEMON DOCTRINE — demons are rumored to be involved, which pulls toward blessing the kingdom

The demon involvement is deliberately RUMORED / SUSPECTED, not confirmed, so the Church can hesitate and stay officially uncommitted while being visibly torn. What it actually decides is a plot question (Callback 3), kept open on purpose. The dramatic payoff: the kingdom cannot even be sure it can count on its own heroes — which is part of why Colin's endgame has to be about RECOGNITION (a political footing), not more gold or medicine.

## RPG Vibe

- the world is DQ/Octopath-flavored: hero + party, monsters in the wild, dungeons (some with deliberately suppressed mana), a Church with paladins/healers, a demon-lord threat now defeated
- the party: tank, healer, mage, ranger + Colin (the glue)

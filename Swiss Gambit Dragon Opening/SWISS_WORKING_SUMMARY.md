# Swiss Working Summary

This file consolidates the current Swiss Gambit direction into one working reference.

Detailed notes now live in `lore/`, `scenes/`, and `archive/`.

## Core Identity

Swiss Gambit works best as:

- a protagonist-centered two-world political fantasy
- a burnout story before it becomes a diplomacy story
- a bridge story, not a generic portal-war escalation

The strongest version is not "Earth versus fantasy" at full scale right away.

It is:

### a returned hero trying to stop both worlds from swallowing him while he buys time, recognition, and material support for a kingdom that is not done being in danger.

## Hero Baseline

Current least-wrong baseline:

- Aiden returns to Earth around age 20
- he was summoned around 18
- he spent roughly 2 years in the fantasy world
- before transfer he was a broad practical student, strongest current fit being information systems
- he has a light but real Earth anchor rather than being a full orphaned blank slate

He is strongest as:

### a missing student turned reluctant interdimensional quartermaster-statesman.

## What Makes Him Interesting

Aiden is not most interesting as:

- a pure mage genius
- a pure diplomat
- a pure chosen-one demigod

He is most interesting as:

### the glue of the party who is powerful in one world, constrained in the other, and forced to become the human bridge between two incompatible systems.

## Strength Model

The two-year timeline works if he is:

- elite, not omnipotent
- top-tier but not unique
- strongest in the fantasy world because mana is abundant there
- still dangerous on Earth, but limited by internal reserves, crystals, preparation, and time

He should be:

### an elite wartime playmaker and party glue, not the strongest being alive.

His real battlefield value is:

- timing
- interruption
- support layering
- rhythm control
- making stronger specialists work at full efficiency

## Earth Mana Rule

Current best rule:

### Earth is functionally mana-dead.

That means:

- no meaningful ambient recharge on Earth
- the hero relies on his internal core and stored crystals
- real magical growth requires exposure and adaptation in the fantasy world's mana-rich environment
- Earth states do not get instant battlemages just by discovering the portal

Low-level magic can still become revolutionary in utility later, but that is not the same as immediate elite combat power.

## Summoning Logic

The original summoning should feel like a real kingdom-level gamble.

Best current version:

### a desperate, costly, politically dubious ritual that can narrow toward viable hero candidates but cannot guarantee a clean outcome.

It is not perfect prophecy.
It is not random nonsense either.

It is a risky wartime gambit that happened to produce Aiden.

## Party Structure

Best current structure:

- 4-person combat core: tank, healer, mage, Aiden
- 5-person roaming version: the core plus a male ranger

The ranger is useful for:

- scouting
- harassment
- outer-ring pressure
- pursuit
- handling things the outsider hero is worse at reading locally

This means:

### 4 is the clean boss-fight unit, 5 is the stronger war-roaming unit.

## Gear Direction

Aiden's equipment should be:

- a functional wartime loadout tailored to him by the kingdom
- built to preserve rhythm, support, survivability, and efficient magic use
- not a divine legendary full set

His key symbolic item should be:

### a hero robe with a short mantle.

That works because it:

- carries battlefield and diplomatic identity
- can be thrown over modern clothes in his Earth field kit
- looks half-ridiculous and half-legitimate on Earth

His sword matters, but the robe is the real identity marker.

## Earth Loadouts

Three-tier model:

### 1. Unprepared
- civilian clothes
- one tiny reserve crystal
- one hidden ward or emergency item

Dangerous, but only in short bursts.

### 2. Field kit
- modern clothes
- backpack
- hero robe with short mantle
- hidden protection
- several crystals
- one proper focus item
- papers, cash, phone, and emergency supplies

This should be his default serious Earth mode.

### 3. Fully decked out
- robe in full display
- visible armor pieces
- sword
- larger reserves
- insignia of office
- primed wards and real demonstration capacity

This is the "High Chancellor incident" version.

## Arc 1 Direction

Arc 1 should be:

### a double-life burnout arc that becomes a political self-definition arc.

Best current shape:

1. Earth-side false normalcy
2. first callback to the kingdom
3. about 3 meaningful double-life cycles
4. shortcuts under pressure
5. financial traces and related patterns trigger suspicion
6. America becomes psychologically impossible as the place where he can stay free and sovereign
7. the Swiss gambit is born
8. he jumps in prepared field-kit mode
9. he proves himself in a controlled way that leaks semi-publicly
10. Arc 1 ends with Swiss recognition in principle

## Opening Direction

The story should open on Earth, not on the Swiss stunt and not in the middle of an emergency.

It should first establish:

- safety
- nostalgia
- minor celebrity weirdness
- the feeling that maybe normal life is still recoverable

Then the kingdom starts pulling him back in.

## Swiss Pivot

At the Swiss pivot, he should not lead with full battle-mode flex unless forced.

Best current version:

- he arrives in field-kit mode
- he looks strange but not maximally warlike
- he uses a controlled proof sequence
- the proof should show both that he can inherently use magic and that the mana crystal is politically valuable
- only if pressed does the scene escalate toward heavier display

This preserves tension and gives later escalation room to matter.

## Why The Story Has Dough

At this point, yes, it has enough structure to keep developing.

The strongest reason is that the project now has a clean central promise:

### a young returned hero tries to preserve ordinary life, a threatened kingdom, and his own political autonomy at the same time, and fails his way into becoming a bridge-state actor.

That is specific enough to carry plot.

The next major writing surface should probably be:

- Swiss plot beats
- especially Arc 1 beats and early Arc 2 transition

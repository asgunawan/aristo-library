## System Rules (locked)

- Earth is mana-dead: no ambient recharge; mages there feel "something missing" (weightless air) and can't recover
- In the kingdom the air has weight/warmth; the Swiss feel "something present" for the first time
- analyze: shallow read (name/age/occupation, near-free, surface-only) vs deep read (private, costs mana + time + proximity + target resistance); Earth people are HARDER to read (no mana to read)
- teleport: needs an anchor + confirmed reference; Earth-side jumps need more symbol-dense etching; the etched circle is a two-way anchor
- passenger teleport: costs more for living/mass; a kingdom-side mage does the heavy teleports, not Aiden
- "broke" (in teleport terms) = the fragile thing in transit failed under uncalculated load (golem's crystal cracked, camera's lens cracked) — an engineering/courier term, NOT a violence term; never got explained to the Swiss
- potions: health = self-contained, mends body only, works anywhere; mana-regen = ambient multiplier, dead weight on Earth; NOTHING you drink creates mana
- ward = a spell fixed to an object, not an item; the robe is lightly warded (passive); the phone is actively warded (one-shot: mana shield + blinding light)
- translation = a script/spell running through Aiden; solves words, NOT meaning
